## Basic Information

Cribbage is the counting card game played on a pegging board to 121 points: players meld hands with a shared 'crib', then 'peg' during play counting 15s, pairs, runs and reaching 31; the cribbage board is an iconic handcrafted object (WWI trench art boards are preserved). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Rummy](rummy.html), [Euchre](euchre.html), [Piquet](piquet.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## Rules

52-card deck; 6 cards each (six cards each for two players, five for four); cut for start (starter card); the crib belongs to the dealer; counting: 15=2, pairs=2, runs=per card, 31=2, last card=1 ('go'); first to 121 on the board wins (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## History

Cribbage was invented in 17th-century England (legend credits the Earl of Cumberland, 1630s) and spread with English settlers; the 120-hole board and WWI trench-art boards (2015) are preserved; the modern hand photo (2026) shows play (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
