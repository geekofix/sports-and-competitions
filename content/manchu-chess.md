## Basic Information

Manchu chess (Qing dynasty chess) is the variant of xiangqi played by the Manchu bannermen in the Qing dynasty on a 9x10 board with modified cannon and palace rules. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Xiangqi](xiangqi.html), [Minixiangqi](minixiangqi.html), [Banqi](banqi.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

9x10 board as xiangqi; banner-specific rules for the cannon (can capture without a screen in some versions) and the palace; otherwise xiangqi rules (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Manchu chess was played in the Qing imperial court and banner garrisons from the 17th–19th centuries; it is documented in Qing-era manuals and distinct from standard xiangqi (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
