## Basic Information

Head pull is an Arctic neck-strength event: two competitors sit facing each other with a leather or rope loop over their ears (or forehead) and try to pull the opponent's head over the centre line (AWG, n.d.; WEIO, n.d.). Contested in men's and women's divisions at the Arctic Winter Games and WEIO; at the 2026 WEIO the men's event was won by Fra... (results published by WEIO). ## Rules

The loop is placed over the ears; seated with feet braced, competitors lean back and pull. Best of three; hand crossing the centre line loses the bout (WEIO, n.d.).

## History

Head pull tests neck and back strength, historically linked to hauling loads on head-straps; it has been an AWG/WEIO event for decades (Wikipedia, n.d.).
