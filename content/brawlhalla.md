## Basic Information

Brawlhalla is Blue Mammoth Games' free-to-play platform fighter (2017), with 2v2 — up to 8-player — modes; its world championship at EVO has been a staple since 2018 (Wikipedia, n.d.) (Blue Mammoth Games, n.d.). Matches: best-of-3 (best-of-5 finals), stock — lives per legend ; open to all genders.

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are [Super Smash Bros](super-smash-bros.html), [MultiVersus](multiversus.html), [Rivals of Aether](rivals-of-aether.html), [Skullgirls](skullgirls.html) and the other [platform fighters](street-fighter.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

Competitive rules: 1v1 and 2v2, best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 grand finals), weapon — gadgets, stocks — three lives ; stage select; double-elimination at EVO and the world championship; open brackets, all genders (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Brawlhalla launched in 2017 (early access 2015) and surpassed 100 million players (the 100M logo is commemorated ; its world championship has run at EVO since 2018 (Wikipedia, n.d.).
