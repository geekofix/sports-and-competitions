## Basic Information

Mölkky is a Finnish outdoor throwing game in which players toss a wooden pin at a group of 12 numbered wooden skittles (1 to 12) trying to reach exactly 50 points. It was invented in 1996 by the Lahti-based company Lahden Paikka (formerly Tuoterengas) as a gentler, more accessible relative of the centuries-old Karelian skittles game kyykkä, and the name Mölkky is a protected trademark. Today it is played worldwide, with national associations in more than 25 countries and an annual Mölkky World Championship organized by the International Mölkky Organization.

## Rules

At the start of a game, the 12 skittles stand upright in a tight formation 3.5 m (about 11.5 ft) from the throwing line, arranged in four rows (1-2; 3-10-4; 5-11-12-6; 7-9-8). Players take turns throwing the throwing pin. Knocking over exactly one skittle scores the number printed on it; knocking over two or more skittles scores only the number of skittles felled (for example three fallen pins score 3 points). A pin counts only if it lies flat on the ground, not leaning on another pin. After each throw the fallen skittles are stood up where they landed, not in their original positions. The first player to reach exactly 50 points wins; scoring more than 50 sends the player back to 25 points, and a player who misses all skittles three turns in a row is eliminated.

## History

Mölkky was created in 1996 by Lahden Paikka in Lahti, Finland, and sold nearly 200,000 sets in Finland alone before the rights and trademark were sold to Tactic Games Oy in 2016; all sets are manufactured in Pori, Finland. The first Finnish championships were held in Lahti in 1997, and the Mölkky World Championship was held annually in Lahti from 1996 to 2015 before moving internationally: Le Rheu (France) 2016, Prague (Czech Republic) 2017, Pori 2018, Samoëns (France) 2019 and Hakodate (Japan) 2024. In Australia and New Zealand the game is sold under the trademarked name Finska, with the first Australian championships in 2010.
