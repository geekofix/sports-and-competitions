## Basic Information

Three-check chess is standard chess with an extra win condition: you win by giving check to the enemy king three times (checks are tracked); checkmate still wins immediately. Within the chess variants and mind games on this site, its closest relatives are [King of the Hill Chess](king-of-the-hill-chess.html), [Racing Kings](racing-kings.html), [Chess](chess.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Lichess, n.d.).

## Rules

Standard chess plus: each time you give check, a counter increments; three checks win the game (even without mate). Checkmate wins instantly regardless of the counter. The variant is rated separately on lichess (Lichess, n.d.).

## History

Popularized online in the 2000s (chess.com '3-check' and lichess 'threeCheck'); world championships are held online (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
