## Basic Information

The Hemp Olympics — also called Hemp Olympix — are a series of satirical novelty competitions created in 1996 at the Nimbin MardiGrass festival in Nimbin, New South Wales, Australia (a cannabis-culture festival held since 1993), designed by activists as humorous, educational events advocating cannabis law reform under the slogan "End Prohibition 2000"; a special edition was staged in Sydney's Victoria Park in 2000, timed just before the Sydney Olympic Games and covered by the BBC (Wikipedia, n.d.-b; MardiGrass, n.d.). The events parody Olympic disciplines: an artistic joint-rolling competition (timed — historically ten minutes), a speed roll, a blindfolded roll, a bong throw for distance (a javelin parody) and munchies contests, with humorous prizes and no formal rulebook beyond the organizers' guidelines (MardiGrass, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-b). All genders take part in the mixed, light-hearted events, and the Hemp Olympix are officially listed in the festival's participation programme at nimbinmardigrass.com (MardiGrass, n.d.). A related event is Seattle Hempfest in Washington, USA, the world's largest cannabis-policy rally from 1991 to 2020, which peaked at about 310,000 visitors in 2008 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

The Hemp Olympix have no formal rulebook; the organizers publish event guidelines on the MardiGrass participation page (MardiGrass, n.d.). In the classic events, contestants roll a cannabis joint — in the artistic event judged on neatness and style within a time limit (historically ten minutes), in the speed roll purely against the clock, and in the blindfolded roll without sight; the bong throw is judged for distance like a javelin, with the thrower's technique free; munchies contests judge eating within set rules announced on the day (Wikipedia, n.d.-b; MardiGrass, n.d.). Prizes are humorous rather than monetary, and the events are open to all genders in mixed competition; participation is part of the festival programme and follows the festival's general rules of conduct (MardiGrass, n.d.). Because the events are a parody of sport and of prohibition-era policy rather than an athletic competition, there are no age or gender classes and no world ranking (Wikipedia, n.d.-b).

## History

The Hemp Olympix were created in 1996 at the Nimbin MardiGrass — which began in 1993 as a protest festival for cannabis law reform in Nimbin, NSW — by activists including the organizers known as Chibo, Bob and Michael, as a satirical "Olympics" to draw attention to the demand to end cannabis prohibition by 2000 (Wikipedia, n.d.-b; MardiGrass, n.d.). In 2000 a Hemp Olympix edition was held in Victoria Park, Sydney, deliberately timed to precede the Sydney 2000 Olympic Games and covered by the BBC, making international headlines (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The festival and its Hemp Olympix continued annually in Nimbin, and the related Seattle Hempfest grew from 1991 into the world's largest cannabis rally before ending its run in 2020 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a, n.d.-b).
