## Basic Information

Rocket League is a competitive video game developed by Psyonix and released in July 2015, in which teams of rocket-powered cars play football — the standard format is 3v3 — using boost, double jumps and aerial maneuvers to hit a large ball into the opponent's goal (Psyonix, n.d.). Its professional circuit, the Rocket League Championship Series (RLCS), was founded in 2016 and is organized by BLAST under license from Psyonix since 2024 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). At the RLCS 2026 World Championship, held 15–20 September 2026 at Dickies Arena in Fort Worth, Texas, with 20 teams and a $1.2 million prize pool, Team Falcons of Saudi Arabia — Rw9, Kiileerrz and Dralii — beat Spacestation Gaming 4–0 in the grand final to take the first MENA world title, with Kiileerrz named Worlds MVP, while the 1v1 world championship went to Nassim "nass" Bali of Gentle Mates, the first player to win multi-discipline titles (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The scene is open to women and men, with dedicated women's circuits and female pros competing alongside the open RLCS (BLAST, n.d.).

## Rules

Competitive Rocket League is played 3v3 on the standardized Soccar pitch, with 5-minute matches that go to golden-goal overtime if tied, a kickoff countdown after each goal, and cars limited to 100 boost collected from pads (Psyonix, n.d.). Ranked online play runs on an MMR ladder from Bronze to Supersonic Legend, and professional events use group stages (Swiss or round-robin) followed by best-of-five playoff series; at the 2026 Worlds, 20 teams qualified through regional circuits for the $1.2 million prize pool, with $300,000 for the champion (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Alongside the 3v3 main event, 2v2 and 1v1 circuits were added to the RLCS from 2026 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). A match is forfeited if a team cannot field three players, and tournament admins enforce roster locks and minimum-age rules — RLCS competitors must be at least 15 years old (BLAST, n.d.). There are no gender classes in the open circuit; women compete in the open RLCS as well as in dedicated women's tournaments (BLAST, n.d.).

## History

Psyonix, founded in 2000 by Dave Hagewood, released its first game, Supersonic Acrobatic Rocket-Powered Battle-Cars, in 2008, and Rocket League — its sequel combining car football with rocket boost — followed on 9 July 2015 and became one of the most successful esports titles, passing 40 million players by 2018 (Psyonix, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The RLCS launched in 2016 with season 1 won by iBUYPOWER Cosmic, and the world championship roll of honor is headed by legendary players: Kaydop, a four-time world champion (2018, 2021, 2022, 2024), Fairy Peak!, the 2019 world champion, M0nkey M00n and Turbo (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The 2025 world championship at LDLC Arena in Lyon on 14 September 2025 was won by NRG — At0mic, Daniel and BeastMode — who beat Team Falcons 4–1, while Karmine Corp, with Vatira, Atow and Juicy, dominated the 2026 season's early majors before Falcons' Fort Worth triumph (Wikipedia, n.d.-a; Wikipedia, n.d.-b).
