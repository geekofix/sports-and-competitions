## Basic Information

Rocket League is a competitive video game in which teams of rocket-powered cars play football, using boost, jumps and aerial manoeuvres to hit a ball into the opponent's goal. Developed by Psyonix and released in July 2015, it became one of the most successful esports titles of its generation, with well over 40 million players by 2018 and a professional circuit, the Rocket League Championship Series (RLCS), founded in 2016 and organised since 2024 by Blast ApS under licence from Psyonix. It is played at the highest level as a three-versus-three (3v3) team game, with a 1v1 series added to the RLCS from the 2025 season.

## Rules

Competitive Rocket League is played three-versus-three in a walled arena, with each individual game played to a five-minute clock and unlimited overtime periods if the score is tied; professional RLCS series are typically contested as best-of-five games between teams of three players each. All games share one physics ruleset: cars may jump, double-jump, dodge and fly through the air, boost is a finite resource refilled by pick-ups on the field, and demolitions — destroying an opponent's car by hitting it at supersonic speed — are legitimate tactics. At league level the RLCS divides the world into regions (North America, Europe, South America, Oceania, the Middle East and North Africa, Asia-Pacific and Sub-Saharan Africa, among others), each running online qualifiers and splits that award RLCS Points and seed in-person international Majors, with the combined standings qualifying teams to the Rocket League World Championship — expanded to 20 teams for the 2025 season.

## History

Rocket League was released by Psyonix in July 2015, following up on its 2008 predecessor Supersonic Acrobatic Rocket-Powered Battle-Cars, and its competitive scene formed almost immediately. Psyonix announced the Rocket League Championship Series in early 2016 in partnership with Twitch; the first season concluded in June 2016 with a US$55,000 prize pool, won by iBUYPOWER Cosmic on the North American side of an enduring transatlantic rivalry. The circuit grew rapidly — prize pools passed $100,000 in 2016, $300,000 in Season 3, and $1,000,000 in Season 6 (2018) — and in 2019 the Epic Games acquired Psyonix. The league moved through seasons with promotion and relegation (via the Rocket League Rival Series, 2017–2019), a restructured open circuit named RLCS X in 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic, and a global multi-region circuit from 2021–22 with a US$6 million seasonal prize pool. Since 2024, Blast ApS has organised the RLCS; Team BDS, winner of the 2021–22 and 2024 world titles, has been the circuit's dominant team, and the 2025 season also staged the first official 1v1 world championship alongside the 3v3 series, won by the European player Mawkzy.
