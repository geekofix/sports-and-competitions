## Basic Information

Swimrun is a race that alternates open-water swimming and trail running, with teams carrying all their gear between the two disciplines. It was born with ÖTILLÖ in Sweden: in 2002 four friends — Anders Malm, Janne Lindberg and the Andersson brothers — bet they could race from Utö to Sandhamn through the Stockholm archipelago (about 24–26 hours for the "original four"); Michael Lemmel and Mats Skott commercialized the race in 2006, and the name "swimrun" was coined in 2011 by Erika Rosenbaum (ÖTILLÖ Swimrun, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The flagship ÖTILLÖ World Championship is held the first Monday of September, running Sandhamn to Utö over about 70–75 km with roughly 24 islands, 24 running and 23 swimming sections and 46 transitions; the course record is 7:00:59, set in 2022 by Hugo Tormento of France and Max Andersson of Sweden (ÖTILLÖ Swimrun, n.d.). Teams race in pairs — men's, women's or mixed — or solo; athletes run in wetsuits and swim in shoes, and must carry a whistle, GPS and first-aid kit, with teammates never more than 10 m apart; pull buoys and hand paddles are optional (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The ÖTILLÖ Swimrun World Series has run since 2015/16 at venues including Utö, Engadin, Gothenburg, Cannes, Whistler, Casco Bay, Mackinac, Orcas and Austin, in Sprint (~15 km), Experience (~8 km) and World Series (~40 km) distances — the World Championship covers the full 70–75 km — and the sport now counts more than 600 races and about 50,000 athletes worldwide (ÖTILLÖ Swimrun, n.d.). Women's and mixed teams are a core part of every race (Wikipedia, n.d.-b).

## Rules

A swimrun course alternates fixed swim and run sections; teams of two (men's, women's or mixed) or solo athletes must carry all equipment — mandatory whistle, GPS device and first-aid kit — through both disciplines, may use a pull buoy and hand paddles in the swims, and teammates must stay within 10 m of each other at all times (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). Athletes run in wetsuits (cut or full) and swim in shoes; the transition zones between sections are where tactics are decided (ÖTILLÖ Swimrun, n.d.). Distances range from the ~8 km Experience and ~15 km Sprint races through the ~40 km World Series events to the 70–75 km World Championship; the first team or solo athlete to the finish wins, with no cut-offs on the flagship course beyond the overall time limit (ÖTILLÖ Swimrun, n.d.). Men's, women's and mixed divisions are scored identically (Wikipedia, n.d.-b).

## History

The ÖTILLÖ race began as a 2002 bet among four friends racing Utö→Sandhamn (~24–26 hours); Michael Lemmel and Mats Skott turned it into a commercial race in 2006, and Erika Rosenbaum coined the name "swimrun" in 2011 when the sport was still unnamed (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The World Series launched in 2015/16 and the sport exploded internationally — by the 2020s more than 600 swimrun races were held worldwide with about 50,000 annual athletes; in 2023 the US Swimrun AG acquired ÖTILLÖ's American races (Ödyssey) (ÖTILLÖ Swimrun, n.d.).
