## Basic Information

F1 Academy is an all-female, Formula 4-level single-seater racing championship founded by the Formula One Group and run by Formula Motorsport Limited — a spec series in which all drivers race identical Tatuus F4-T421 chassis on Pirelli tyres at selected Formula 1 Grands Prix (F1 Academy, n.d.). It is part of the formula-racing ladder below F3 and belongs to the motorsport family.

Champions so far: Marta García (2023), Abbi Pulling (2024) and Doriane Pin (2025, with Prema Racing also taking the teams’ title) — the 2025 title was decided at the season finale in Las Vegas; the champion receives a fully funded seat in the next step of the ladder (F1 Academy, n.d.). The grid expanded to 18 cars in 2025 with Hitech TGR joining as the sixth team, and wildcard entries at each round bring in local talent — at Shanghai 2026, for example, Chinese driver Shi Wei (photo above) (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## Rules

Each race weekend runs a qualifying session plus two or three races (opening/reverse-grid race and the feature race on Grand Prix Sunday); in 2026 select rounds add a third race with a new opening-race format (F1 Academy, n.d.). Points follow the F1-style scale; the season champion is crowned F1 Academy Champion. Eligibility is limited (a two-year participation rule applies) and the series is women-only by design, with the goal of building a pipeline of female talent toward F3 and F1 (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

F1 Academy was announced in March 2023 as the successor to the W Series (which folded in 2022) and debuted that year with five teams; it is the only series below F3 that races exclusively as an F1 support series (Wikipedia, n.d.). Its first two champions, García and Pulling, both received funded seats onward — García to the Formula Regional European Championship, Pulling to GB3 — and Pin tested a Mercedes W12 after her 2025 title (Wikipedia, n.d.).
