## Basic Information

The Supercars Championship is Australia's premier touring car series, contested with V8-powered, front-engine sedans built to a control "Gen3" formula and sanctioned by the FIA; the drivers' title has been awarded since the Australian Touring Car Championship began in 1960 (Supercars, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Its centerpiece is the Bathurst 1000, a 1000 km, 161-lap endurance race at Mount Panorama in Bathurst since 1963 (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The 2025 season introduced the Finals Series format: Chaz Mostert won the overall drivers' title, Broc Feeney the Sprint Cup and the Payne/Tander pair the Enduro Cup, with Triple Eight taking the teams' title (13th) and Chevrolet the manufacturers' title; Will Brown won in 2024 and Brodie Kostecki in 2023 (Supercars, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Toyota joins the series in 2026 (Supercars, n.d.). Women have raced at the highest level — Simona de Silvestro became the first full-time female driver in 2017, and 35 women had attempted the Bathurst 1000 as of 2025, including the all-female "Castrol Cougars" entry of Melinda Price and Kerryn Brewer in 1998 (Wikipedia, n.d.-c).

## Rules

Gen3 cars — Ford Mustang, Chevrolet Camry (and from 2026 Toyota Camry) — use 5.2-litre Chevrolet or 5.0-litre Ford V8 engines with rear-wheel drive, a six-speed transaxle and identical aerodynamic and suspension packages to ensure parity (Supercars, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-a). A season mixes sprint races of roughly 120–250 km and the 1000 km Bathurst enduro, scored on a descending points scale, with co-drivers mandatory at endurance rounds; since 2025 the points decide three titles — overall drivers', Sprint Cup and Enduro Cup — culminating in the Finals Series (Supercars, n.d.). Safety cars, full-course yellows, tire and fuel rules and a Superlicense points system for eligibility govern race control (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The championship is open to all genders; women compete in the main series and its support categories on identical machinery and rules (Wikipedia, n.d.-c).

## History

The Australian Touring Car Championship began in 1960, and the Armstrong 500 at Phillip Island became the Bathurst 1000 at Mount Panorama in 1963, growing to 1000 km and 161 laps in 1973 (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). Peter Brock — "the King of the Mountain" — won the Bathurst 1000 a record nine times and defined the sport for a generation, alongside rivals Dick Johnson and Allan Moffat; Jamie Whincup holds the record for drivers' titles, and Shane van Gisbergen won four championships between 2016 and 2023 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The series raced as "V8 Supercars" from 1997 and was renamed the Supercars Championship in 2017 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
