## Basic Information

Four-man carry is a WEIO relay-style strength event: three carriers bear a fourth teammate on their shoulders/arms and race a set course; fastest team wins (WEIO, n.d.). It is one of the traditional WEIO events alongside the blanket toss and high kicks, with men's and women's teams. Within the Arctic and Indigenous sports on this site, its closest relatives are [Blanket Toss](blanket-toss.html), [Snow Snake](snow-snake.html), [Two-Foot High Kick](two-foot-high-kick.html), [Finger Pull](finger-pull.html), [Hand Games](hand-games.html), [World Eskimo Indian Olympics](world-eskimo-indian-olympics.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

The carried athlete sits on the interlocked arms of three carriers; the team races a marked course without dropping the carrier. Drops add penalty time or cause elimination. Teams of four, mixed formats at some editions (WEIO, n.d.).

## History

The four-man carry mimics transporting a hunter or an elder across rough terrain and has been a WEIO event since the early decades of the games (Wikipedia, n.d.).
