## Basic Information

Idi probak (ox test) is the Basque oxen-pulling contest: a pair of oxen (idiak) harnessed to a wooden sledge drags an irregular stone — typically 1,500–4,500 kg — along a marked course in the town square; the heaviest stone moved, or the longest pull within the time limit, wins. It is one of the most photographed herri kirolak, held at fiestas in Elgeta, Astigarraga, Urduliz and across Gipuzkoa and Bizkaia. Within the Basque rural sports on this site, its closest relatives are [Zaldi Probak](zaldi-probak.html), [Asto Probak](asto-probak.html), [Giza Probak](giza-probak.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Euskal Herri Kirolak Elkartea, n.d.).

## Rules

The stone is weighed and marked before the contest; the ox pair must pull it the set distance within the time limit without the yoke breaking. Modern federation rules add veterinary checks, harness inspection and animal-welfare provisions; women participate as handlers and in mixed teams at festival events (Euskal Herri Kirolak Elkartea, n.d.).

## History

Ox-drawn stone pulling comes from agricultural life — oxen were the farm's engine for moving stone and timber. The 1944 photograph of two oxen pulling a stone in Elgeta shows the classic setup, and the carved contest stones of Astigarraga are still used. The Herri Kirolak circuit standardized weights and safety rules in the late 20th century (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
