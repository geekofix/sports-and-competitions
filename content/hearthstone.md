## Basic Information

Hearthstone is Blizzard's 2014 digital collectible card game (mana crystals, minions, spells, hero classes); its esport is the Hearthstone World Championship and the seasonal Masters Tour, played in the conquest format (bring four decks, ban one, win with each remaining deck once) — open to all genders, and the game also sits in the Esports World Cup program (Wikipedia, n.d.) (Blizzard, n.d.).

## Rules

Competitive rules: conquest format — each player brings four decks (Masters Tour), the opponent bans one, then the match is won by winning one game with each remaining deck; best-of-5 in later rounds; sideboarding between games; open brackets (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Hearthstone launched in March 2014 (developed by Team 5) and became the biggest digital CCG esport within a year; the first World Championship was held in 2014 (won by James Kostesich, 'Firebat') and annual world championships with seven-figure prize pools followed (Wikipedia, n.d.).
