## Basic Information

Aquathlon is a multisport race combining open-water swimming and running, governed internationally by World Triathlon. Athletes swim a set distance in open water and then run immediately after, with no transition area. It is the swim-run offshoot of triathlon and is contested at World Triathlon Aquathlon World Championships.

## Rules

Standard aquathlon distances are a 750 m to 1.5 km swim followed by a 5 to 10 km run, depending on the event category (sprint or standard). Wetsuit use follows World Triathlon rules based on water temperature. Athletes start together in a mass start, exit the water, and run straight to the finish; the fastest total time wins. Variants: some national events add a second swim-run loop ("double aquathlon") or include a beach start.

## History

Aquathlon emerged in the 1980s and 1990s as a simpler offshoot of triathlon. World Triathlon has organized Aquathlon World Championships since the late 1990s, and the sport is popular in coastal countries with strong open-water swimming scenes.
