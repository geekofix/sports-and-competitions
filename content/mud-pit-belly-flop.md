## Basic Information

The mud pit belly flop is a novelty competition in which contestants leap or dive belly-first into a pit of mud, and are judged on the size of the splash, the style of the flop and showmanship rather than on distance or speed. It is best known as one of the signature events of the Redneck Games, an annual festival of deliberately silly rural competitions held in East Dublin, Georgia, and of similar events such as the Canadian Redneck Games in Minto, Ontario.

## Rules

Contestants take turns jumping from a platform or the pit edge into a shallow pit filled with watery mud, aiming to land flat on their stomach and chest to create the biggest splash. Judges score each attempt on splash size, style and overall entertainment value, awarding a trophy (traditionally a half-crushed beer can at the Redneck Games) to the winner. There are no age or fitness requirements beyond signing a waiver, and fancy dress is common. Related mud-pit novelty events at the same festivals include mud pit tug-of-war, mud pit volleyball, bobbing for pig's feet and a mud pit slip and slide.

## History

The Redneck Games were started by Mac Davis, general manager of radio station WQZY-FM in East Dublin, Georgia, after commentators said the 1996 Atlanta Olympics would be run by "rednecks"; the first Redneck Summer Games drew about 5,000 people, more than double the town's population, and the Mud Pit Belly Flop became one of its most photographed events. The games ran annually until 2013, were canceled amid declining revenue and environmental scrutiny, and returned in September 2023 under the name "Redneckin' 4 Jesus". Similar Redneck Games spread to Canada, with the Minto Canadian Redneck Games running since 2006 and including a Mud Pit Belly Flop contest alongside events supporting the Make-A-Wish Foundation.
