## Basic Information

Competitive jigsaw speed puzzling is the timed sport of assembling jigsaw puzzles: the World Jigsaw Puzzle Championship (WPC) has been held since 2019 with individual (1,000 pieces) and team (2,000 pieces) events. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Sudoku](sudoku.html), [Crossword](crossword.html), [Nonogram](nonogram.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

Individual: 1,000-piece puzzle, time limit (e.g. 100 minutes); team: 2,000 pieces with 4 members; fastest complete-and-correct time wins; WJPC 2023 (WJPF photos) shows the events (World Jigsaw Puzzle Federation, n.d.).

## History

Organized by the World Jigsaw Puzzle Federation since 2019; Alejandro Clemente won the 2022 individual title (photographed) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
