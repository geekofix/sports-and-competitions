## Basic Information

Horde chess is an asymmetric chess variant: White commands a 'horde' of 36 pawns (no other pieces) against Black's standard 16-piece army; White wins by checkmating Black, Black wins by capturing all of White's pawns. It is a standard lichess variant. Within the chess variants and mind games on this site, its closest relatives are [Antichess](antichess.html), [Chess](chess.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Lichess, n.d.).

## Rules

White's 36 pawns start on ranks 1–4 in a fixed formation and move/capture like normal pawns (pawns on the first rank cannot double-step). Black plays normally. White wins by mate; Black wins by capturing the entire horde (Lichess, n.d.).

## History

The variant was formalized in the 19th century and standardized on lichess in the 2010s; it is widely used for handicap games and puzzles (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
