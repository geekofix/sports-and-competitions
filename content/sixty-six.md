## Basic Information

Sixty-six (Hungarian/Slovak 'hatvanhat') is the two-player trick-taking game with a 24-card deck (A,10,K,Q,J,9 of each suit): the last trick scores 10 and marriages in trump score; the first player to reach 66 points wins; it is the national game of Hungary. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Schnapsen](schnapsen.html), [Belote](belote.html), [Preferans](preferans.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## Rules

24-card deck; 6 cards each; follow suit; trump A>10>K>Q>J>9; marriage (K+Q of trump) ; the 'sixty-six' (A+10 of trump) is the A+10 of trump worth 66 when announced; last trick +10; first to 66 points wins (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## History

Sixty-six is documented in Hungary from the 19th century and is played across Central Europe; Hungarian playing-card sets (1930s, Takkk photo) show the German-suited decks used (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
