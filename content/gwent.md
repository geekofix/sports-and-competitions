## Basic Information

Gwent is CD Projekt Red's standalone card game spun out of the card minigame in The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt (2015), relaunched as a free-to-play CCG in 2018; its esport was the GWENT World Masters series, wound down in 2024 as CDPR shifted focus (Wikipedia, n.d.) (CD Projekt Red, n.d.). Matches: best-of-3 over three rounds with row bonuses (melee, ranged, siege); open to all genders.

## Rules

Gwent rules: faction decks with minimum card counts; three rounds won by round points; mulligan phase at match start; leader abilities; open brackets (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Gwent originated as a minigame in The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt (2015, designed at CD Projekt Red), became a standalone game in 2018, and ran the GWENT World Masters from 2017; it entered maintenance mode in 2024 (Wikipedia, n.d.).
