## Basic Information

Ear pull is a WEIO and Arctic Winter Games event: two competitors have a loop of leather or cord hooked behind one ear each and pull against each other; the first whose ear loop slips or whose head crosses the centre loses (WEIO, n.d.). At the 2026 WEIO, Amanda Ahsogeak (Anchorage) won the women's event (ICT News, 2026). Women and men compete separately. Within the Arctic and Indigenous sports on this site, its closest relatives are [Blanket Toss](blanket-toss.html), [Snow Snake](snow-snake.html), [Two-Foot High Kick](two-foot-high-kick.html), [Finger Pull](finger-pull.html), [Hand Games](hand-games.html), [World Eskimo Indian Olympics](world-eskimo-indian-olympics.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

The loop is placed behind the ear; competitors pull head-to-head, leaning back. The bout is best of three; slipping loop or crossing the centre line loses (WEIO, n.d.).

## History

Ear pull has been a WEIO event since the early games and is also contested at the Arctic Winter Games; it tests pain tolerance and neck strength (Wikipedia, n.d.).
