## Basic Information

Motocross (MX) is off-road motorcycle racing on enclosed dirt circuits with steep jumps, banked turns and whoops. It is governed worldwide by the Fédération Internationale de Motocyclisme (FIM), and its premier competition is the FIM Motocross World Championship (MXGP), held mainly in Europe but also in the Americas, Asia, Africa and Australia. A closely related indoor variant on artificial tracks is [supercross](supercross.html). Motocross is also an Olympic-adjacent discipline at the Youth Olympic Games and a core sport of the X Games.

## Rules

A Grand Prix is run over two races (motos) per class, each of about 30 minutes plus two laps, and championship points are awarded for the combined results. The main classes are MXGP for 251-450cc four-stroke machines, MX2 for 150-250cc four-stroke machines, and the Women's class (WMX) run on the MX2 format; junior classes cover 65cc, 85cc and 125cc bikes. Riders start together behind a starting gate, wear full protective gear including a helmet, goggles, boots and chest protector, and the rider with the most points at the end of the season is world champion. At the end of the season, national teams of three riders (MXGP, MX2 and Open) compete at the Motocross des Nations, held since 1947 and famously at Red Bud in the United States.

## History

Motocross developed in Europe in the early 20th century from motorcycle scrambles and trials; the first events were held in the United Kingdom around 1924, and the sport spread to the continent, where the FIM Motocross World Championship was inaugurated in 1957 with a 500cc formula, adding 250cc in 1962 and 125cc in 1975. In 2004 the classes were restructured into MX1 (up to 450cc four-stroke) and MX2 (up to 250cc), renamed MXGP and MX2 in later years as four-stroke engines took over. Belgian and Swedish riders dominated the early decades, and stars such as Stefan Everts, Jeffrey Herlings, Romain Febvre and Tim Gajser have defined the modern era. In the United States, the AMA runs the Lucas Oil Pro Motocross Championship and Supercross series.
