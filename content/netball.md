## Basic Information

Netball is a fast team ball sport played by two teams of seven players on a rectangular court 30.5 m long and 15.25 m wide, divided into thirds, with a raised goal ring (no backboard) at each end. The aim is to pass the ball down the court and shoot it through the opponent's ring from within the 4.9 m shooting circle. Netball was created for women in England in the 1890s as an adaptation of basketball and is today played in more than 80 countries, especially across the Commonwealth. The world governing body is World Netball (worldnetball.sport), IOC-recognized since 1995.

## Rules

Each team has seven positions with restricted areas: Goal Shooter (GS) and Goal Attack (GA) may shoot from the goal circle, Centre (C) plays in the centre and goal thirds, Wing Attack (WA), Wing Defence (WD), Goal Defence (GD) and Goal Keeper (GK) are restricted accordingly. A player in possession may take no more than one step and must pass or shoot within three seconds; dribbling and running with the ball are prohibited. Goals are scored only from the shooting circle and count one point each in standard rules. A match lasts 60 minutes in four 15-minute quarters, ends change each quarter, and the centre pass alternates. Some domestic leagues modify scoring: Australia's Suncorp Super Netball awards two points for a "Super Shot" taken from outside the goal circle, and the Fast5 format scores one, two or three points depending on the shooting zone, with a power-play quarter doubling all goals.

## History

Netball developed in England in the 1890s from women's basketball rules (published by Clara Gregory Baer in the United States and adapted in England), with the three-zone court, ring without backboard and no-dribbling rules fixed by 1901. The first international match was played in Melbourne on 20 August 1938 between Australia and New Zealand (40-11). Rules were standardized internationally from 1957, and in 1960 the International Federation of Women's Basketball and Netball Associations (later World Netball) was founded in Colombo with Australia, England, New Zealand, South Africa, Wales and the West Indies. The first World Tournament was held in Eastbourne in 1967, netball joined the Commonwealth Games in 1998 (Kuala Lumpur) and the World Games in 1985, and World Netball was recognized by the IOC in 1995. Jamaica, Australia, New Zealand and England dominate international play.
