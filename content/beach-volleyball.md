## Basic Information

Beach volleyball is volleyball played on sand by two-player teams. It is governed by the FIVB (through Volleyball World) and has been an Olympic sport since Atlanta 1996. The flagship events are the FIVB Beach Volleyball World Championships and the Olympic tournament, with a professional World Tour in between.

## Rules

Under FIVB rules, the sand court is 16 x 8 m with a net 2.43 m high (men) or 2.24 m (women). Teams of two have a maximum of three touches to return the ball; blocking counts as the first touch. The serve is from behind the end line, and teams rotate serving order. Matches at the World Championships and Olympics are best of three sets to 21 points (win by two, capped at 30), with the deciding set to 15. Side-out scoring is used, and teams switch ends every 7 points (every 5 in the third set).

## History

Beach volleyball began in the 1920s on the beaches of Santa Monica and Hawaii, where six-player volleyball was played on sand. Two-player beach volleyball was pioneered in California in the 1940s-50s and professionalized in the 1970s-80s. It became a full Olympic medal sport at Atlanta 1996. The FIVB Beach Volleyball World Championships have been held since 1997, and Brazilian and American pairs have dominated the Olympic medal table, with stars like Karch Kiraly (USA) and Kerri Walsh Jennings.
