## Basic Information

MLB The Show is Sony San Diego Studio's MLB baseball simulation; its esport is the official MLB The Show competitive circuit (the World Series event, formerly the MLB The Show League) on the current title in 1v1 brackets, plus community Players League events (Wikipedia, n.d.) (Sony / San Diego Studio, n.d.).

## Rules

MLB The Show esports rules: matches on standard squads in the current game with league-set innings; open qualifiers and bracket playoffs; best-of-3 sets; season rules published by Sony; open to all genders (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

The MLB The Show series began in 2006; Sony launched the official competitive circuit (the MLB The Show League, now World Series event) in the late 2010s with six-figure prize pools; the game is a regular at the Esports World Cup (Wikipedia, n.d.).
