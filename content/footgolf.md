## Basic Information

FootGolf is a hybrid sport in which players kick a football along a golf course into a large hole (a 53 cm diameter cup) in as few strokes as possible. It is governed by the Federation for International FootGolf (FIFG), which calls itself the world's governing body, runs the FIFG FootGolf World Cup (since 2012) and a World Tour with ranked tournaments (FIFG 100/250/500/1000 points).

## Rules

Under the FIFG Rules of Play, each hole is played like golf but with the feet: the ball is placed on a tee area and struck toward the cup, with the lie being wherever the ball stops (a marked ball spot in some formats). Par is set per hole (usually 3-5); the hole is holed when the ball rests in the cup. Out-of-bounds and hazards follow golf conventions adapted by FIFG: a ball landing in a hazard or out of bounds is dropped with a one-stroke penalty (or replayed), and balls in unplayable lies get relief. Players may use any part of the foot and may wear studded or turf shoes per competition regulations. Matches are stroke play over 18 holes (individual) or team formats; the FIFG World Cup is played over 4 rounds of 18 holes with a cut. Local variations exist, and FIFG Competition Rules govern World Tour events, rankings and the World Cup qualification system.

## History

FootGolf was formalized in the Netherlands in 2008-2009 by the Dutch FootGolf Association, building on informal games played by footballers on golf courses since the 1970s; the first official tournament was held in 2009. The FIFG was founded in 2012 (registered in Hungary) and held the first FootGolf World Cup in Budapest in June 2012. The sport spread across Europe and to the US, where the American FootGolf League (2011) and American FootGolf Federation (2012) organized play; the US national team has competed at every World Cup. The 2026 FIFG FootGolf World Championship is scheduled for Acapulco, Mexico. Leading nations are Hungary, the Netherlands, the Czech Republic, Slovakia, the US and Argentina; stars include former footballers who switched to FootGolf.
