## Basic Information

Tic-tac-toe (noughts and crosses) is the 3x3 two-player game: players alternate X/O aiming for three in a row; with perfect play it always draws. Variants (3D boards, on walls/glass) are photographed; 'world championships' exist as a novelty (e.g. ultimate tic-tac-toe). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Connect Four](connect-four.html), [Dots and Boxes](dots-and-boxes.html), [Hex](hex.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

3x3 grid; X starts; three in a row wins; full board without a line = draw; solved game (proven a draw with best play) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Tic-tac-toe is ancient (Roman 'terni lapilli' boards, 1st century BC); it got its modern name in 19th-century Britain; wall and 3D versions are photographed (Mödlareuth 2020, Rethymno 2023) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
