## Basic Information

Popdarts is a target game in which players toss double-ended suction-cup darts at a small target marker on any smooth surface — countertop, window, glass door or table — combining the accuracy of darts with the scoring feel of cornhole (Popdarts, n.d.). The game went viral on social media in the 2020s and is now organized under the American Popdarts League (APL), the official governing body operating under license from the trademark owner, which sanctions league nights and a national championship with a $25,000 prize pool broadcast on ESPN2 (American Popdarts League, n.d.). The 2026 APL National Championship, decided live on ESPN2 at ESPN Wide World of Sports in Orlando on 7 August 2026, was won by Colton Davis, the back-to-back reigning champion (American Popdarts League, n.d.). Leagues are mixed and open to women and men, and the APL national rankings list hundreds of players of both genders across 40 states (American Popdarts League, n.d.).

## Rules

In the standard game, players or teams alternate throws, each player tossing three Popdarts per round from a throwing box about 10 feet from the target in the classic floor format or across a table in league play (Official Game Rules, 2025). A dart must stick upright to count; a dart that is not upright is dead but stays in play until the round ends, and knocking an opponent's dart loose is legal. The upright dart closest to the marker scores 3 points and every other upright dart scores 1, with a "Kiss" bonus of 1 extra point for a dart touching the marker; rounds use cancellation scoring like cornhole, only the net difference is credited, and the first team to the target total — commonly 21 — wins (Popdarts, n.d.). Each player has one called "Instant Win" attempt per game, a dart landing fully inside the Instant Win circle ends the game immediately. APL sanctioned play adds a 10-second shot clock, one timeout per round and strict equipment rules — only unmodified league-approved darts (American Popdarts League, n.d.).

## History

Popdarts was invented in the United States as an indoor-friendly alternative to pointed darts, the suction-cup design making it safe for players, pets and walls; the brand's Rookie, Pro and Board editions spread through viral TikTok and YouTube demonstrations in the early 2020s (Popdarts, n.d.). The APL's first full season ran from 30 September 2025 to 29 September 2026, with the 2026–27 season running to 31 July 2027; the 2025 national championship went to Colton Davis with a $6,000 grand prize, and the 2026 edition raised the prize pool to $25,000 (American Popdarts League, n.d.). The game's growth is driven by bar and game-room culture, where short rounds and the Instant Win mechanic suit casual leagues (Official Game Rules, 2025).
