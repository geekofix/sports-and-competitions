## Basic Information

Popdarts is a target game in which players toss double-ended suction-cup darts at a small target marker placed on any smooth, non-porous surface — countertop, window, glass door, refrigerator or car windshield — combining the accuracy of darts with the friendly, cornhole-style scoring of a tossing game (Official Game Rules, 2025; Popdarts, n.d.). The game was invented in the United States by PD Games LLC, owner of the Popdarts® trademark, and went viral on TikTok and YouTube from 2024 onward (Popdarts, n.d.). Organized competition is run by the American Popdarts League (APL), the official governing body operating under license from PD Games LLC, which sanctions local leagues and a national championship with a $25,000 prize pool broadcast live on ESPN2 (American Popdarts League, n.d.). The 2026 APL National Championship, decided live on ESPN2 at ESPN Wide World of Sports in Orlando on 7 August 2026, was won by Colton Davis — his second consecutive title (American Popdarts League, n.d.; ESPN, 2026). Leagues are mixed and open to women and men alike, and the APL's 2026–27 national rankings list 319 players across 35 clubs in 40 states (American Popdarts League, n.d.).

## Rules

In Classic Play with the retail packs (Rookie, OG and Pro), two or more players or teams play; rock-paper-scissors decides who goes first, and the winner chooses the first target-marker location and the team colors (Popdarts, n.d.). Each player throws three Popdarts of a single colorway, standing at least 10 feet from the target marker on a smooth surface, and players alternate throws until all darts are thrown (Popdarts, n.d.). A dart that does not stand upright is a dead dart and remains in play until the end of the round; after all darts are thrown, points are awarded with cancellation scoring — the points of one team cancel out the opponent's (Popdarts, n.d.). The closest stuck dart scores 3 points, every other stuck dart scores 1, a dart touching the target marker earns a "Kiss" bonus of 1 additional point, and with the Pro Pack a dart landing on top of the marker is worth 10 points and suppresses the 3-point closest dart; a "Wigglenobber" (a dart sticking to another dart's opposing suction cup) is worth double the points of the dart it lands on, while a "T-Bone" is worth nothing (Popdarts, n.d.). The first team to reach or exceed 21 points wins the game (Popdarts, n.d.; Official Game Rules, 2025).

*Figure: Loong, J. (2008, February 24). Red-tipped suction-cup darts piled together — the double-ended projectiles of Popdarts-style target games [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 2.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:DSCF4106_(2289070721).jpg*

In Board Play with the Board Edition, two teams (singles or doubles) alternate throwing three darts each from the same side at the board; a dart completely inside the white PD circle scores 6, inside the grey circle 3, and every other stuck dart 1, again with cancellation scoring to 21 (Popdarts, n.d.). Each player gets one "Instant Win" attempt per game, which must be called before throwing; if the dart does not land completely inside the team's Instant Win circle, it is removed and normal play continues (Popdarts, n.d.). APL-sanctioned league and tournament play (2026–27 rulebook) uses a table of at most 24 × 48 inches and 36 inches high with the target marker centered and a tower in the front right corner; two player boxes of at most 3 × 4 feet stand with their front edge 17 feet from the marker, with at least 10 feet of vertical clearance (American Popdarts League, n.d.). Players alternate throws with both feet in the box, three darts per player in one colorway, under a 10-second shot clock (a violation makes the next dart dead) and one timeout per team per round; leaving the box early costs 1 point, throwing from outside the box makes the dart dead, and no dart may be touched before the round is scored (−2 points) (American Popdarts League, n.d.). Only unmodified APL-approved Popdarts-branded darts may be used, and scoring adds the specialty shots: T-nobber on the marker 10, closest landed dart 3, every other landed dart 1, Kiss/Lippy +1, Fender Bender +1 (stackable), Wigglenobber 2×, tower shot 5 and Inch Worm 11, still with cancellation scoring and first to 21 (American Popdarts League, n.d.). The rule source is the world governing body, the American Popdarts League. Gender, age and classification differences are explicit: leagues are mixed and open to women and men; the Rookie Pack uses softer darts built for younger throwers while the Pro Pack is tournament weight for older kids and adults; and the APL Wheelchair Policy lets players using a wheelchair compete from 14 feet with ADA accommodations (American Popdarts League, n.d.; Popdarts, n.d.).

## History

Popdarts was invented in the United States by PD Games LLC as an indoor- and family-friendly alternative to pointed darts — the double-sided suction cups make it safe for players, pets and walls — and the brand's Rookie, Pro and Board editions spread through viral TikTok and YouTube demonstrations from 2024 (Popdarts, n.d.). The American Popdarts League was then established as the official governing body under license from PD Games LLC: its 2025 Mini Season was the first season, the first full season ran from 30 September 2025 to 29 September 2026, and the 2026–27 Full Season runs from 29 August 2026 to 31 July 2027 with rankings scored through the Scoreholio app (American Popdarts League, n.d.). The 2025 National Championship aired on ESPN2 on 1 August 2025 and was won by Colton Davis with a $6,000 grand prize; the 2026 edition raised the prize pool to $25,000, was decided live on ESPN2 at ESPN Wide World of Sports in Orlando on 7 August 2026, and made Davis the back-to-back champion (American Popdarts League, n.d.; ESPN, 2026). The game's growth is driven by bar, garage and game-room league culture, where short rounds and the Instant Win mechanic suit casual play (Official Game Rules, 2025).
