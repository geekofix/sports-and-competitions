## Basic Information

Langa is a traditional Nigerian hopping game played in rounds: one player per team, the Ruwa (point-scorer), hops on one foot while holding the other foot with one hand and tries to reach a designated area on the opponent's side to score; opponents may dislodge the held hand, eliminating the Ruwa for that round (Wikipedia, n.d.). Within the folk and festival games on this site, its closest relatives are [Abula](abula.html), [Ampe](ampe.html), [Asafo Atwele](asafo-atwele.html), [Gidigbo](gidigbo.html), [Kho Kho](kho-kho.html), [Tag Rugby](tag-rugby.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

Each round, teams nominate a Ruwa; all players hop on one foot holding the raised foot. The Ruwa must cross to the opponent's marked zone and back without being touched on the hand holding the foot; a dislodged hand ends the attempt. Points accumulate over rounds; the team with the most points wins. Played by children of both sexes in Nigerian schools and festivals (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Langa is among the traditional Nigerian games preserved at the National Sports Festival; like abula it is a playground game with regional rule variants (Wikipedia, n.d.).
