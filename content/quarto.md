## Basic Information

Quarto is a 4x4x4x4 deduction game invented in 1991 by Swiss mathematician Roland Siegwalt: 16 pieces each with four binary attributes (tall/short, round/square, black/white, solid/hollow); players alternately place a piece chosen by the opponent; the first to complete a line of four sharing an attribute wins. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Blokus](blokus.html), [Kamisado](kamisado.html), [Amazons](amazons.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Gigamic, n.d.).

## Rules

4x4 board, 16 pieces; you place the piece your opponent chose; you win by aligning four pieces sharing at least one attribute. No draws with perfect play analysis. World championship exists (Gigamic, n.d.).

## History

Invented by Roland Siegwalt in 1991 (Switzerland) and published by Gigamic; it became a classic deduction game with a world championship (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
