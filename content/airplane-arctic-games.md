## Basic Information

The airplane is a prone hold strength event: the athlete holds a plank position (body straight, supported on forearms and toes) for as long as possible; the longest time wins (AWG, n.d.; WEIO, n.d.). Contested at the Arctic Winter Games and WEIO in men's and women's categories. Within the Arctic and Indigenous sports on this site, its closest relatives are [Blanket Toss](blanket-toss.html), [Snow Snake](snow-snake.html), [Two-Foot High Kick](two-foot-high-kick.html), [Finger Pull](finger-pull.html), [Hand Games](hand-games.html), [World Eskimo Indian Olympics](world-eskimo-indian-olympics.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

From a prone position, the athlete rises to a forearm plank with the body straight; the hold is timed until the hips sag, a knee touches, or the athlete drops. Best of three or single hold depending on the Games' package (AWG, n.d.).

## History

The airplane tests core strength and has been an Arctic sport at AWG and WEIO for decades, named for the outstretched-arms pose (Wikipedia, n.d.).
