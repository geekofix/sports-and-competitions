## Basic Information

The official FIA World Rally Championship game (Codemasters/EA, e.g., EA SPORTS WRC 2023) carries the WRC Esports Championship (eWRC), run with the FIA and Codemasters on stage-based rally events with realistic damage (Wikipedia, n.d.) (Codemasters / EA, n.d.). The eWRC championship crowns a world champion each season on the current WRC title.

## Rules

eWRC rules: players run identical cars on the same rally stages with realistic damage; combined stage times decide the result; qualification rounds lead to a final rally; points decide the championship; the FIA sanctions the championship alongside the real WRC (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Codemasters' WRC game esport began with community cups in the 2010s; the official eWRC championship has run since 2019 on WRC 8, WRC 9, WRC 10, WRC Generations and EA Sports WRC, with the FIA involved since 2022 (Wikipedia, n.d.).
