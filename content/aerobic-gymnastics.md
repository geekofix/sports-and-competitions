## Basic Information

Aerobic Gymnastics is a high-intensity FIG discipline in which athletes perform continuous, complex aerobic movement patterns to music. Senior routines last about 1 minute 20 seconds and are performed by individuals (men and women), pairs, trios or groups of five. It is contested at FIG World Championships.

## Rules

Under the FIG Code of Points, routines must include elements from four groups — dynamic strength (e.g. push-ups, straddle jumps), static strength (e.g. V-sits, supports), jumps (e.g. split jumps) and flexibility (e.g. pancake, split) — plus the seven basic aerobic steps (march, jog, kick, jack, lunge, knee lift, step touch). Judges score difficulty, execution and artistry, with deductions for falls and out-of-bounds. The highest total wins.

## History

Aerobic gymnastics grew out of the aerobics fitness boom of the 1980s (popularized by Jane Fonda's workout videos) and became a FIG discipline in 1995, with World Championships held since the mid-1990s.
