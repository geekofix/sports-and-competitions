## Basic Information

Canasta is a rummy variant invented in 1939 in Montevideo, Uruguay (by Segundo Santos and Alberto Serrato): two partnerships play with two 52-card decks plus jokers; melding sets of seven ('canastas') scores big; the game ends when a side reaches 5,000 points. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Rummy](rummy.html), [Gin Rummy](gin-rummy.html), [Cribbage](cribbage.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## Rules

Two decks + 4 jokers; 15 cards each, draw/discard; melds of 3+ same rank (wild cards allowed); a canasta = 7 cards; red 3s are special (can't be melded, penalty); going out requires one canasta— red canasta; phase scoring; target 5,000 (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## History

Invented in 1939 in Uruguay (by Segundo Santos and Alberto Serrato); it became a world classic, US craze in the 1950s (CanASTa clubs); the example layout and the 2025 meld photo document play (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
