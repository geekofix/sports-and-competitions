## Basic Information

Futsal is FIFA's official indoor version of five-a-side football, played on a 40 m x 20 m hard court with a low-bounce ball, a 3 m x 2 m goal, kick-ins instead of throw-ins, a 4-second goalkeeper limit and accumulated-foul free kicks. It is governed by FIFA, which runs the FIFA Futsal World Cup (since 1989), and — in parallel — by the AMF (Asociación Mundial de Futsal), successor of FIFUSA, which runs the AMF Futsal World Cup. See also [Five-a-side Football](five-a-side-football.html).

## Rules

Under the FIFA Laws of the Game for Futsal, two teams of five (four outfield players plus a goalkeeper) play two 20-minute halves (stopped clock). The ball is smaller and lower-bounce (size 4, 400-440 g, 62-64 cm circumference). Throw-ins are replaced by kick-ins; the goalkeeper may hold the ball for a maximum of 4 seconds and may not handle a ball played back directly from a teammate's kick-in. Fouls accumulate: from the sixth foul per half, the opposing team takes a direct free kick from the 10 m mark (or closer) without a wall. Rolling substitutions are allowed, and a "flying goalkeeper" (an extra outfield player replacing the keeper) may be used. Matches tied in knockout rounds go to extra time (two 5-minute periods) and then penalties. The FIFA Futsal World Cup (2024 in Uzbekistan, won by Brazil) is the premier event.

## History

Futsal was codified in 1930 in Montevideo, Uruguay, by Juan Carlos Ceriani for YMCA indoor play; FIFUSA (founded 1971 in Rio de Janeiro) organized the first world championship in 1982 (Brazil won). FIFA launched its own futsal in 1989 after failing to reach agreement with FIFUSA, and the FIFA Futsal World Cup became the dominant international event, with Brazil winning most editions (6 titles). FIFUSA reorganized as the AMF in 2002, continuing the AMF World Cup (Paraguay most successful). Futsal was included in the Youth Olympic Games program from 2018 (Buenos Aires). Futsal is played professionally in Brazil, Spain, Russia, Portugal, Argentina and Iran, with top clubs and stars such as Falcão of Brazil.
