## Basic Information

The official Formula 1 video game series (Codemasters/EA, e.g., F1 24, F1 25) carries F1's esport: the F1 Esports Series (renamed F1 Sim Racing), with open qualifiers, a professional championship and a world championship at the end of each season on the current F1 title (EA / Codemasters, n.d.). The series also appears at the Esports World Cup (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## Rules

F1 Esports rules: time-trial qualifying on the current game, then race events on official F1 circuits with the current F1 cars (no custom liveries in official events); points per race decide the championship over the season; open to all genders in the open qualifiers (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

F1's game esport began in 2017 with the first F1 Esports Series; the series ran annually on the F1 titles from F1 2017 to F1 23 and continues as F1 Sim Racing on the annual releases, with the world championship as the season finale (Wikipedia, n.d.).
