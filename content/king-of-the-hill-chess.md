## Basic Information

King of the Hill is a chess variant: besides checkmate, you win by moving your king to the central four squares (d4, d5, e4, e5 — the 'hill'). It is one of the most popular lichess variants. Within the chess variants and mind games on this site, its closest relatives are [Racing Kings](racing-kings.html), [Three-Check Chess](three-check-chess.html), [Chess](chess.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Lichess, n.d.).

## Rules

Standard chess, plus the king may win by occupying any of the four central squares; entering the hill wins immediately (even if it would be 'check'). Checkmate also wins. Castling into/out of the hill is allowed per standard rules (Lichess, n.d.).

## History

The variant dates to the 19th century and is a standard online variant; world championships on lichess (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
