## Basic Information

The uneven bars are the women's artistic gymnastics apparatus of two horizontal rails — the high bar at 2.50 m and the low bar at 1.70 m, adjustable up to 1.80 m apart — on which the gymnast swings, circles, performs release moves and pirouettes between the bars and finishes with a dismount; the routine lasts at most 90 s and is not performed to music (Fédération Internationale de Gymnastique, n.d.-c; Fédération Internationale de Gymnastique, n.d.-a; Wikipedia, n.d.-b). It is one of the four women's Olympic apparatus, second in the rotation; at Paris 2024 the title went to Kaylia Nemour of Algeria — the first Olympic gymnastics gold for an African nation (Wikipedia, n.d.-a; Olympics, n.d.).

Among the leading bar workers of the 2026 season, the women lead by definition — this is a women's apparatus: Kaylia Nemour (pictured), the 2024 Olympic champion and multiple world champion, Skye Blakely of the United States, the 2026 world all-around champion, and Olympic silver medallist Hezly Rivera of the USA and the Italian duo Alice D'Amato and Manila Esposito (Wikipedia, n.d.-a, n.d.-b). The 2026 World Championships in Rotterdam decided the apparatus titles with Nemour and Blakely among the favourites (Fédération Internationale de Gymnastique, n.d.-b).

## Rules

The uneven bars stand at 2.50 m (high) and 1.70 m (low) above the floor, with the distance between the bars adjustable up to 1.80 m; the rails are about 2.40 m long and a landing mat with an additional 10 cm soft mat is mandatory (Fédération Internationale de Gymnastique, n.d.-c; Fédération Internationale de Gymnastique, n.d.-e). The routine may last at most 90 s; it must include swings, at least one release move re-caught on the bar and a dismount; eight elements count toward the D-score with connection bonuses for directly linked difficult elements, and the E-panel starts at 10.0 with deductions for amplitude, execution and landing (Fédération Internationale de Gymnastique, n.d.-a).

A fall costs 0.5 plus execution deductions, and the gymnast may continue within 30 s; the top eight from qualification advance to the final, where the highest D + E wins (Fédération Internationale de Gymnastique, n.d.-a; Fédération Internationale de Gymnastique, n.d.-e). The uneven bars are a women-only apparatus at elite level — the men's counterpart is the horizontal bar; junior categories use lowered bars, and the event is part of the team and all-around competitions (Fédération Internationale de Gymnastique, n.d.-e).

## History

The uneven bars evolved from the single high bar of the 1950s–60s, when the apparatus was lowered and split into two bars of different heights at the 1952 Helsinki Olympics; the release-move era began in the 1970s (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). Nadia Comăneci scored the first perfect 10.0 in Olympic history on the uneven bars at Montreal 1976, and Olga Korbut's 1972 back somersault on bars changed the event; Svetlana Khorkina of Russia, pictured, the “Queen of the Bars”, won the 2000 Olympic title and three world all-around titles with her original releases named after her (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
