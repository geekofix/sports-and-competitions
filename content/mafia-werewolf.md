## Basic Information

Mafia (known as Werewolf in Europe) is the social-deduction party game invented in 1986 by Dimitry Davidoff at Moscow State University: an informed minority (mafia/werewolves) secretly 'kills' a villager each night while the majority votes to eliminate a suspect by day; the mafia wins when they equal the villagers, the villagers when all mafia are eliminated. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Avalon](avalon.html), [The Resistance](the-resistance.html), [Coup](coup.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Davidoff, D, 1986).

## Rules

A moderator runs the game: night phase (mafia choose a victim secretly), day phase (discussion + majority vote to execute). Roles: mafia/werewolf, villager, and special roles (seer/doctor/policeman). No fixed player count (8+ typical); played in circles (photographed 2006–2024) (Davidoff, D, 1986).

## History

Invented by Dimitry Davidoff in 1986 in Moscow; the werewolf variant spread in Europe in the 1990s–2000s; world championships (e.g. the World Werewolf Championship) are held; mafia game photos from Krakow 2024 show modern play (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
