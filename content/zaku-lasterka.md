## Basic Information

Zaku lasterka (sack race) is the Basque sack race: competitors hop a marked course inside heavy grain sacks — classically 81 kg sacks — individually or in relays; the first to the finish wins. Sack racing is a staple of Basque and European village fêtes (the same tradition as the Indonesian balap karung and the British village-show sack race). Within the Basque rural sports on this site, its closest relatives are [Untziketariak](untziketariak.html), [Segala](segala.html), [Lasto Altxatzea](lasto-altxatzea.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Euskal Herri Kirolak Elkartea, n.d.).

## Rules

Runners must keep both hands on or inside the sack, hop on both feet and stay in their lane; falling is allowed, rejoining the sack is required. Distances are typically 50–100 m with heats and a final; relays pass the sack. Men's, women's and children's categories are run at festivals (Euskal Herri Kirolak Elkartea, n.d.).

## History

Sack racing appears in Basque festa programmes from the late 19th century and remains one of the most popular novelty races; historic photographs (e.g. a 1921 university contest) show the same format a century ago (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
