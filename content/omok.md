## Basic Information

Omok ('five stones') is the Korean gomoku variant: five in a row on a 15x15 (or 19x19) board wins, overlines count as wins; it has a professional league in Korea (Korean Baduk/Omok Association) and is popular online. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Gomoku](gomoku.html), [Renju](renju.html), [Go](go.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Korea Omok Association, n.d.).

## Rules

Korean rules: black first; exactly five or more in a row wins for both colors (no forbidden moves in casual omok); professional omok uses stricter rules. Tournaments are organized by the Korea Omok Association (Korea Omok Association, n.d.).

## History

Omok developed in Korea in the 20th century from gomoku; the professional omok league started in the 2000s (Korean Omok Association), with TV coverage (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
