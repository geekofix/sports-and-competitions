## Basic Information

The crossword is the word puzzle of interlocking answers in a grid with numbered clues; competitive solving (American-style and cryptic) has world championships (e.g. the American Crossword Puzzle Tournament since 1978). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Sudoku](sudoku.html), [Kakuro](kakuro.html), [Scrabble](scrabble.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (American Crossword Puzzle Tournament, n.d.).

## Rules

Grid with across/down clues; black squares; unique letters per square; timed solving in competitions (ACPT and world crossword championships); cryptic crosswords use wordplay clues (American Crossword Puzzle Tournament, n.d.).

## History

Crosswords appeared in the New York World in 1913 (Arthur Wynne); cryptic crosswords in the UK from the 1920s (Torquemada, Edward Powys Mathers); the Signpost crossword cover (2018) and setter photos document the scene (American Crossword Puzzle Tournament, n.d.).
