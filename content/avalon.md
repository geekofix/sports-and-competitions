## Basic Information

The Resistance: Avalon (2012, Don Eskridge) is a social-deduction board game for 5–10 players with hidden roles: loyal servants of Arthur vs minions of Mordred; Merlin knows the minions; missions succeed or fail by secret votes; three failed missions win for Mordred. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [The Resistance](the-resistance.html), [Mafia (Werewolf)](mafia-werewolf.html), [Coup](coup.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Indie Boards & Cards, n.d.).

## Rules

5–10 players; roles: Merlin, Percival, loyal servants, Mordred, assassin, minions, Oberon (in some editions); five missions, each with 2–3 players voting success/fail secretly; three successes = good wins (assassin may kill Merlin); three failures = evil wins (Indie Boards & Cards, n.d.).

## History

Designed by Don Eskridge, published 2012 by Indie Boards & Cards/Hidden Agenda; derived from his 2009 The Resistance; world championships are held; the Deskohraní 2009 board-game-fair photo shows the genre's tournament scene (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
