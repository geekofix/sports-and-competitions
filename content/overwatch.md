## Basic Information

Overwatch is Blizzard Entertainment's team-based hero shooter (5v5 in Overwatch 2), played with role-specific heroes, ultimates and objective control (escort, control, hybrid maps); the premier circuit is the Overwatch Champions Series (OWCS), which replaced the franchised Overwatch League when it closed in January 2024 (Wikipedia, n.d.; Liquipedia, n.d.) (Blizzard Entertainment, n.d.). In 2026, Crazy Raccoon won the OWCS Champions Clash in Tokyo back-to-back (24 May 2026, 4–3 over Twisted Minds in the grand final), ZETA DIVISION won the Midseason Championship at the Esports World Cup in Paris (29 July–2 August 2026, 4–2 over Twisted Minds, MVP shu), and the OWCS World Finals take place 2–6 December 2026 in Guangzhou with 12 teams (esports.gg, n.d.).

## Rules

OWCS rules: open qualification into regional circuits (North America, EMEA, Asia, China); matches are best-of-3, best-of-5 in finals, on the current live patch with the standard 2-2-2 role lock in place since 2022; no ties — control maps go to extra rounds. Men's and women's players compete in the same open rosters — there are no gender divisions (Liquipedia, n.d.).

## History

Overwatch launched in 2016 and its esport peaked with the Overwatch League (2018–2023, four champions: London Spitfire 2018, San Francisco Shock 2019–2020, Dallas Fuel 2021, Houston Outlaws 2023 at the Toronto grand final 4–0 over Florida Mayhem); the league folded after Microsoft's acquisition of Activision Blizzard and the OWCS open circuit began in 2024, won at the world finals by Team Falcons (2024) and Twisted Minds (2025) (Wikipedia, n.d.).
