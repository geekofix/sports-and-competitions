## Basic Information

Sittuyin (Burmese chess) is the chess of Myanmar: like western chess on 8x8, but the pieces (called by Burmese names) have a distinctive initial setup and the 'feuda' (ferz-like queen move) distinguishes it from international chess in some accounts. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Chess](chess.html), [Chaturaji](chaturaji.html), [Banqi](banqi.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

8x8 board; pieces mirror chess (king, queen/ferz, rooks, bishops, knights, pawns) with Burmese names (e.g. 'min' king, 'sit' general); some rule sets allow the special 'feuda' move. Played widely in Myanmar today (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Sittuyin descends from chaturaṅga via India/Burma; it is documented from the Konbaung period and remains the national board game of Myanmar, played in tea shops and at festivals (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
