## Basic Information

The King of Fighters (KOF) is SNK's crossover fighting series; KOF XV (2022) is the current title and an EVO main-stage game (Wikipedia, n.d.) (SNK, n.d.). Matches: 3v3 team battles, best-of-3 rounds, best-of-3 — sets (best-of-5 finals); open to all genders.

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are [Street Fighter](street-fighter.html), [Samurai Shodown](samurai-shodown.html), [Mortal Kombat](mortal-kombat.html), [Tekken](tekken.html) and the other [fighting games](super-smash-bros.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

Competitive rules: 3v3 team order — team order select, best-of-3 rounds, best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 grand finals), max — power gauge mechanics per patch; double-elimination at EVO and SNK cups; open brackets (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

KOF began in 1994 (SNK's '94) combining Fatal Fury and Art of Fighting characters; KOF '98 is regarded as one of the greatest fighting games ever, and the series has been an EVO staple since the 2000s (the KOF-98 arcade cabinet is preserved (Wikipedia, n.d.).
