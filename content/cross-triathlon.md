## Basic Information

Cross triathlon (off-road triathlon) is the multisport race combining open-water swimming, mountain biking and trail running, governed by World Triathlon. Standard cross triathlon distances are roughly 1-1.5 km of swimming, 20-30 km of mountain biking and 5-10 km of off-road running, though long-course variants go up to 3 km / 80 km / 20 km. The XTERRA brand runs a global series with a world championship in Maui, Hawaii, while World Triathlon holds its own cross triathlon world championships.

## Rules

Under World Triathlon rules, the race starts with a mass swim start, transitions to a point-to-point or looped mountain bike course, then a trail run, with transition areas between disciplines. Athletes must wear the same race number throughout, helmets are mandatory on the bike leg, and drafting is prohibited on the bike. The first athlete across the finish line wins; team and relay formats also exist. Course marking, aid stations and safety follow World Triathlon event regulations.

## History

Off-road triathlon emerged in the 1990s in Tahiti and Hawaii, where the XTERRA series (founded 1996, world championship in Maui since the 2000s) popularized the format. World Triathlon (formerly ITU) added cross triathlon to its multisport program and holds world championships, and the discipline appeared at the 2025 World Games program discussions. Cross triathlon is now practiced on every continent, from alpine lakes to tropical islands.
