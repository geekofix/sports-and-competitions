## Basic Information

Pente is a connection-and-capture game invented in 1977 by Tom Braunlich and sold by Parker Brothers: players place stones on a 19x19 go board aiming for five in a row OR capturing five pairs of opponent stones. It has a world championship and a professional league history (1980s). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Gomoku](gomoku.html), [Connect6](connect6.html), [Go](go.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pente.com, n.d.).

## Rules

First to make five in a row or to capture five pairs (ten stones) wins; captures happen by sandwiching an enemy pair between two of your stones, which are then removed. Tournament play on 19x19; Pente World Championship since 1978 (Pente.com, n.d.).

## History

Invented by Tom Braunlich in 1977 in Minnesota; the Pente World Championship ran from 1978; the game spawned the 'Pente shape' strategy diagrams (Pente.com, n.d.).
