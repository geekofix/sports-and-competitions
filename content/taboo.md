## Basic Information

Taboo (1989, Parker Brothers) is the party guessing game: one player describes a target word without using the listed 'taboo' words while teammates guess within a timer. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Codenames](codenames.html), [Avalon](avalon.html), [Mafia (Werewolf)](mafia-werewolf.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

Cards with a target word and 5 forbidden words; sand timer (60 s); correct guess = point; taboo word used = penalty; most points wins (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Invented in 1989 (patented in 1989); published by Parker Brothers; sold worldwide; the game box photo (2019) documents it (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
