## Basic Information

Pole push is a Dene strength event: two athletes sit on the snow/ice facing each other, feet braced, and push a wooden pole (about 1 m) with both hands; the first to push the opponent back over the line or lose footing loses (AWG, n.d.). Contested at the Arctic Winter Games Dene Games in men's, women's and youth categories. Within the Arctic and Indigenous sports on this site, its closest relatives are [Blanket Toss](blanket-toss.html), [Snow Snake](snow-snake.html), [Two-Foot High Kick](two-foot-high-kick.html), [Finger Pull](finger-pull.html), [Hand Games](hand-games.html), [World Eskimo Indian Olympics](world-eskimo-indian-olympics.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

Athletes sit with soles together or braced, gripping the pole at chest height; the referee starts the push on command. The bout is best of three; no standing or lifting off the seat is allowed (AWG, n.d.).

## History

Pole push reflects traditional tests of strength in Dene communities and has been a Dene Games discipline at the Arctic Winter Games since 1990 (Wikipedia, n.d.).
