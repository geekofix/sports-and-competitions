## Basic Information

Boulette is a traditional game of the Avesnes region (Nord, France) and Provence: played with a wooden ball (boulette) and cards — the boules-style throwing game with cards; the wooden boulettes of Avesnes are preserved. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Pétanque](petanque.html), [Pétanque](petanque.html), [Belote](belote.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

Played on a boule alley (boulodrome) with a small wooden ball and larger 'boulettes'; card variant assigns roles; regional rules of Avesnes/Provence; the Boulodrome at Lac du Bel Air (Priziac) shows the venue type (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Boulette is documented in northern France (Avesnes) and Brittany/Provence from the 19th century; it combines boules throwing with card play in some versions (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
