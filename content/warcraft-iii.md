## Basic Information

Warcraft III (and its 2020 remake Reforged) is Blizzard's fantasy RTS; its esport lived on through the World Cyber Games era — medal ceremonies at WCG 2006, 2009 and 2011 are documented — and today through Warcraft III Champions Cup events and community cups, often alongside StarCraft II at BlizzCon-era festivals (Wikipedia, n.d.; Liquipedia, n.d.). Standard 1v1 best-of-3/5 on ladder maps, heroes and creeping; open brackets, all genders.

## Rules

1v1 rules: fixed starting heroes per race, neutral creeps, hero levels cap; standard victory by destroying all enemy buildings; veto map pool; no pausing in tournaments; best-of-3 (bo5 finals); race mirror or draft by ruleset (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos (2002) defined modern esport RTS alongside StarCraft; WCG ran Warcraft III as a medal title from 2005 to 2011 — Sky (Li Xiaofeng) won WCG 2005 and 2006, and Sky again reached the WCG 2011 grand final — before StarCraft II took over at WCG from 2011; the 2020 Reforged remake revived ladder and community competition (Wikipedia, n.d.).
