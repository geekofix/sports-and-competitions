## Basic Information

Para badminton (also called parabadminton or G-badminton) is the Paralympic version of [badminton](badminton.html), governed by the Badminton World Federation (BWF) since 2011. Players are classified into six sport classes: WH1 and WH2 for wheelchair players, SL3 and SL4 for standing players with lower-limb impairments, SU5 for players with upper-limb impairments and SH6 (formerly SS6) for players of short stature. Badminton made its Paralympic debut at Tokyo 2020 with 14 medal events, becoming the third racket sport on the Paralympic program after wheelchair tennis and para table tennis, and it will also be contested at Paris 2024 and Los Angeles 2028.

## Rules

Para badminton follows the standard BWF laws of badminton with court adaptations for the wheelchair classes, which play on a half court in singles. Events are offered as men's and women's singles, men's and women's doubles and mixed doubles within each sport class or a combined class, such as WH1-WH2 doubles and SL3-SU5 doubles. Serving, scoring to 21 points and the rally rules are those of ordinary badminton, with modifications only where the class requires them. At the BWF Para Badminton World Championships and the Paralympics, athletes compete in their classified events, and classification is handled by BWF-certified classifiers through medical and functional evaluation.

## History

The first governing body, the International Badminton Association for Disabled (IBAD), was founded in 1995 in Stoke Mandeville, the birthplace of the Paralympic movement. The Para Badminton World Federation organized biennial world championships from 1998 and merged into the BWF at a unanimous vote in Dortmund in June 2011, making the BWF the first international federation to govern both Olympic and Paralympic badminton. The decision to include badminton in the Paralympic program was taken in Sochi in 2014, and the sport debuted at Tokyo 2020. Notable players include India's Parul Parmar, a singles world champion, and South Korea has dominated the early Paralympic medal tables.
