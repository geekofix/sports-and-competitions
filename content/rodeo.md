## Basic Information

Professional rodeo is the competitive form of the ranch skills of the American West — riding bucking horses, roping cattle, wrestling steers — governed in the US by the Professional Rodeo Cowboys Association (PRCA, founded 1936 as the Cowboys' Turtle Association, renamed RCA in 1945, PRCA since 1975) and, for women's events, the Women's Professional Rodeo Association (WPRA) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a; Wikipedia, n.d.-b).

The season culminates in the Wrangler National Finals Rodeo (NFR) in Las Vegas — held at the Thomas & Mack Center through 2035 under a $264 million agreement — where the top 15 per event compete over ten rounds for world championships and average titles (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The 67th NFR ran 4–13 December 2025 and crowned Stetson Wright as all-around world champion (a single-season earnings record $817,088 and his tenth gold buckle, adding the bull riding title), with Rocker Steiner (bareback), Statler Wright (saddle bronc), Tucker Allen (steer wrestling), Riley Webb (tie-down roping), Andrew Ward and Jake Long (team roping) and, in the WPRA, Kassie Mowry (barrel racing, her second world title) (PRCA, 2025; WPRA, 2025).

Women have been central to professional rodeo since the WPRA's founding: barrel racing and breakaway roping are women's headline events at the NFR, and Kassie Mowry is the 2025 WPRA world champion (WPRA, 2025).

## Rules

The PRCA sanctions seven championship events: bareback riding, saddle bronc riding and bull riding (the "roughstock", judged 0–100 per ride with an 8-second qualified ride), steer wrestling (hazer + wrestler, fastest time), tie-down roping, team roping (header and heeler) and breakaway roping; women compete in barrel racing (a timed cloverleaf pattern around three barrels), breakaway roping and team roping as heeler (Wikipedia, n.d.-a; Wikipedia, n.d.-b).

Riders must stay aboard 8 seconds with one hand in the rigging/handhold; scores combine rider performance and animal performance, max 100 (50+50). Timed events are decided by the fastest clean time; penalties (broken barrier, lost hat in bronc riding) add seconds (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Prize money is paid per go-round and for the average across the NFR's ten rounds; world champions are the season-end leaders in each event (PRCA, 2025).

The top 15 in each event qualify for the NFR in Las Vegas; the ProRodeo Hall of Fame (opened 1979 in Colorado Springs) honors the sport's legends, and the separate PBR (Professional Bull Riders) league runs its own world finals (Wikipedia, n.d.-b).

## History

Rodeo grew from cattle-ranch contests in the 1880s–1910s (wild cow milking, bronc riding); the Cowboys' Turtle Association was founded in 1936 to standardize rules and insurance, became the Rodeo Cowboys Association in 1945 and the PRCA in 1975, and created the NFR in 1959 to crown world champions in the seven main events (Wikipedia, n.d.-b).

Legends include Jim Shoulders (16 world championships), Ty Murray (seven all-around and two bull-riding titles), Don Gay (eight bull-riding titles), Trevor Brazile (26 world championships, the most of any PRCA contestant), Lane Frost (1987 bull-riding champion, rode the undefeated Red Rock), and on the women's side Charmayne James (11 WPRA barrel-racing world titles and seven NFR average titles); famous animals include the broncs Steamboat, Midnight and War Paint and the bull Bodacious (Wikipedia, n.d.-c). The 2025 NFR added another Wright-family sweep, with brothers Stetson and Statler taking all-around/bull riding and saddle bronc (PRCA, 2025).
