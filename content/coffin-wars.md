## Basic Information

Coffin Wars is a novelty combat sport staged by the Pro League Network (PLN): two fighters compete to escape from inside a coffin, starting from a grappling position at the bottom. It is part of PLN's lineup of unusual combat sports alongside Slap Poker and Gorilla Wars. The name also refers to the older American custom of coffin races, in which teams push a coffin down a street, most famously the Emma Crawford Coffin Races in Manitou Springs, Colorado, since 1994.

## Rules

In the PLN Coffin Wars format, two fighters take turns starting at the bottom of a grappling position inside the coffin and must escape the coffin to win the round. If the first two rounds are tied, the competitors start a third round locked up on their knees, and each must try to escape. Matches are refereed and medical staff are present. In the Emma Crawford coffin race, a team of five (Emma plus four costumed mourners) pushes a coffin 195 yards through the streets in heats, with awards for fastest time and best costumes; the travelling Coffin Cup is contested between Manitou Springs and Nederland, Colorado's Frozen Dead Guy Days.

## History

Manitou Springs invented the Emma Crawford Memorial Coffin Races in 1994 around the local legend of Emma Crawford, who wished to be buried on Red Mountain; the event runs on the weekend before Halloween with a parade. The Frozen Dead Guy Days festival in Nederland, Colorado, has held coffin races since 2002, and the two towns contest the Coffin Cup. The Pro League Network added Coffin Wars to its combat-sport shows in the 2020s, filming fighters escaping a coffin from grappling positions.
