## Basic Information

The Game of the Amazons is a chess-queens-and-arrow game invented in 1988 by Walter Zamkauskas (Argentina): on a 10x10 board each player moves one of four amazons (queens) like a chess queen and then shoots an arrow that blocks a square; the last player able to move wins. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Chess](chess.html), [Quarto](quarto.html), [Havannah](havannah.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

10x10 board, 4 amazons per side on fixed starting squares; a turn = move one amazon as a queen (any distance, straight/diagonal, no jumping) + shoot an arrow from it (same queen lines) which blocks the target square. No captures. Last to move wins. World championship exists (Computer Olympiad) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Invented by Walter Zamkauskas in 1988 in Buenos Aires; it became a classic abstract game and a computer-game benchmark (the Amazons world championship at the Computer Olympiad) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
