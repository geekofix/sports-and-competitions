## Basic Information

Onewheel racing is competitive racing on the Onewheel, a self-balancing electric skateboard with a single large wheel in the center, invented by Kyle Doerksen and launched by his company Future Motion through a Kickstarter campaign in January 2014 that raised about 630,000 US dollars. Riders stand on the board and control speed and direction by shifting their weight, and races are held on closed dirt or asphalt courses. The Onewheel Racing League (ORL), formed in 2021, organizes the premier series, including the Race for the Rail at the GoPro Mountain Games, with prize money of 10,000 US dollars since 2020.

## Rules

Onewheel races are timed runs over closed off-road or paved courses, with riders grouped into divisions such as Pro Men, Pro Women, Legends (age 50 and over) and Clydesdale (riders of 215 pounds and more), and a minimum age of 14. Only Onewheel boards manufactured by Future Motion are allowed, and modifications are limited to tire pressure, digital shaping profiles and the rear footpad; helmets and protective gear are mandatory. The winner is the rider with the fastest time over the course, and the season standings are decided by points accumulated across the ORL events. The official rules are published by Future Motion for the Onewheel Racing League and the Race for the Rail.

## History

The Onewheel was created by Kyle Doerksen of Future Motion, who developed the single-wheeled self-balancing board and brought it to market through a Kickstarter campaign in January 2014 that raised about 630,000 US dollars, one of the most successful hardware campaigns of its time. The first official Onewheel race was held in 2016 at Vail, Colorado, as the Race for the Rail at the GoPro Mountain Games, and informal racing grew through the community site The Float Life. The Onewheel Racing League was formed in 2021 to structure the sport, with prize money of 10,000 US dollars per event from 2020 onward; major races moved to Sky Tavern in Reno, Nevada, in 2022 and 2023, broadcast on ESPN, and to Hollister Hills SVRA in California from 2024 onward.
