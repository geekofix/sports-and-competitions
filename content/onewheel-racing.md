## Basic Information

Onewheel racing is competitive racing on the Onewheel, a self-balancing electric skateboard with a single large wheel in the center, invented by Kyle Doerksen and launched by his company Future Motion through a Kickstarter campaign in January 2014 that raised about 630,000 US dollars (Wikipedia, n.d.). Riders stand on the board and control speed and direction by shifting their weight, and races are held on closed dirt or asphalt courses; women and men compete in separate Pro divisions as well as open classes (Onewheel Racing League, n.d.). The Onewheel Racing League (ORL), formed in 2021, organizes the premier series, including the Race for the Rail at the GoPro Mountain Games, with prize money of 10,000 US dollars since 2020 (Onewheel Racing League, n.d.).

## Rules

Onewheel races are timed runs over closed off-road or paved courses, with riders grouped into divisions such as Pro Men, Pro Women, Legends (age 50 and over) and Clydesdale (riders of 215 pounds / about 98 kg and more), and a minimum age of 14 (Onewheel Racing League, n.d.). Only Onewheel boards manufactured by Future Motion are allowed, and modifications are limited to tire pressure, digital shaping profiles and the rear footpad; helmets and protective gear are mandatory (Onewheel Racing League, n.d.). The winner is the rider with the fastest time over the course, and the season standings are decided by points accumulated across events (Onewheel Racing League, n.d.). Age and gender classifications are explicit: Pro Men and Pro Women race separately, while Legends and weight classes are open to all genders (Onewheel Racing League, n.d.).

## History

The Onewheel was invented by Kyle Doerksen, who launched Future Motion with a Kickstarter campaign in January 2014 that raised about 630,000 US dollars, and the first boards shipped in 2015 (Wikipedia, n.d.). Competitive racing emerged as riders gathered at events such as the GoPro Mountain Games in Vail, Colorado, where the Race for the Rail became the showcase race with a 10,000 US dollar prize from 2020 (Wikipedia, n.d.). The Onewheel Racing League (ORL) was formed in 2021 to standardize divisions, safety rules and the season standings, growing the sport into a series with races across the United States (Onewheel Racing League, n.d.).
