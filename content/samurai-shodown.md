## Basic Information

Samurai Shodown is SNK's weapon-based samurai fighter; Samurai Shodown (2019) is the current reboot and an EVO title (Wikipedia, n.d.) (SNK, n.d.). Matches: weapon clashes, rage — rage gauge, best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 finals); open to all genders.

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are [King of Fighters](king-of-fighters.html), [Street Fighter](street-fighter.html), [Soulcalibur](soulcalibur.html) and the other [fighting games](tekken.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

Competitive rules: best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 grand finals), 40-second — round timer, weapon — rage mechanics per patch; double-elimination at EVO; open brackets, all genders (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Samurai Shodown launched in 1993 (SNK) with Nakoruru and Haohmaru; the 2019 reboot by SNK revived the series at EVO 2019 onward (Wikipedia, n.d.).
