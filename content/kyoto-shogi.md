## Basic Information

Kyoto shogi is a 5x5 shogi variant of the Kyoto/Osaka region with unusual pieces (including the 'Kyoto piece' that with locally defined moves); it survives in regional play. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Shogi](shogi.html), [Minishogi](minishogi.html), [Dobutsu Shogi](dobutsu-shogi.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

5x5 board with Kyoto-specific piece set; drops as in shogi in some rule sets; mate wins (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Kyoto shogi belongs to the family of small-board shogi variants (5x5) that flourished in the Kansai region from the Edo period; modern rule sheets document its initial position (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
