## Basic Information

Schnapsen (Zwicken) is the Austrian two-player trick-taking game with a 20-card deck (A,K,Q,J,10 of each suit): the trump jack ('Unter') and trump queen are key; marriages score; the game is played to 66 points; trick points with A=11,K=4,Q=3,J=2,10=10; first to 66 wins; 'Schneider'/'Schwarz' if loser has 0. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Sixty-Six](sixty-six.html), [Sheepshead](sheepshead.html), [Belote](belote.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## Rules

20-card German-suited deck; dealer deals ten cards each in two rounds; trump is declared by turning a card; Unter (trump jack) beats Ober (trump queen); marriages (Ober+Unter of same suit) score 2/4; trick-taking; 66 points wins (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## History

Schnapsen derives from the German Schafkopf/Skat family of the 18th–19th century and is the national game of Austria; the PD card images (Roland Scheicher) document the deck (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
