## Basic Information

Crazyhouse is a chess variant: whenever you capture an opponent piece you may place a piece of the same color and type (your own) on any empty square as part of the same move — captured pieces 're-enter' the game. It is one of the most popular online chess variants (played on lichess and chess.com). Within the chess variants and mind games on this site, its closest relatives are [Bughouse](bughouse.html), [Antichess](antichess.html), [Chess](chess.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Lichess, n.d.).

## Rules

Standard chess moves plus the drop rule: a captured piece becomes yours and may be dropped on any vacant square (not on the 1st/8th rank for pawns, and never giving immediate check). Dropping and moving are alternatives on your turn. Checkmate or stalemate ends the game as usual (Lichess, n.d.).

## History

Invented in the 19th century and popularized as 'crazyhouse' in the US; it became the dominant online variant after chess.com/lichess added it in the 2000s. World championships exist (e.g. the Crazyhouse World Championship) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
