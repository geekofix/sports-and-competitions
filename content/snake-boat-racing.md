## Basic Information

Snake boat racing (vallam kali in Malayalam) is the spectacular team rowing race of Kerala, India, in which long, narrow chundan vallam — "snake boats" of 100–140 feet carrying 100–120 rowers, plus helmsmen and a crew of singers chanting the vanchippattu boat songs — sprint down a course of about 1.4 km in parallel tracks (Kerala Tourism, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The Nehru Trophy Boat Race, the premier event, is held on Punnamada Lake near Alappuzha; the 72nd edition on 22 August 2026 was won by the Aroma Chundan of the Aroma Boat Club in its debut season in 4:23.328, ahead of the defending Nadubhagam (4:23.448) and Veeyapuram (4:23.770), with 71 boats including 22 chundans competing, and the 2026 programme included a women's Thekkanodi category (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The 132nd Aranmula Uthrattathi Vallamkali, the ritual race of 30 August 2026, is rowed without a winner as an offering to the Parthasarathy temple (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). A professional Champions Boat League has run since 2019, launched by Kerala tourism minister Kadakampally Surendran (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). Women race in the Thekkanodi (southern-style) women's boat category at the Nehru Trophy, and women's crews have their own events at the major regattas (Wikipedia, n.d.-b).

## Rules

There is no single world federation: the races are organised by the participating boat clubs (palliyodam committees) under the Kerala state government and the Nehru Trophy Boat Race committee, which sets the event regulations (Kerala Tourism, n.d.). In a chundan vallam race, the 100–120 rowers sit in pairs along the 100–140 ft hull and row in perfect synchrony to the vanchippattu songs led by the singers at the bow, while four to five helmsmen steer with long oars at the stern and midship; the boat that crosses the finish line first wins, with heats feeding a final at the Nehru Trophy (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The course is about 1.4 km with boats racing in parallel lanes; false starts are penalised, and contact between boats is penalised by the race committee (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Categories include the chundan (snake boat) class, the thekkanodi class — which at the 2026 Nehru Trophy included a women's category — and smaller iruttukuthy and other traditional classes, each with its own heats and final (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The Aranmula race is a ritual: the boats row to the temple and back with no winner declared (Wikipedia, n.d.-b).

## History

Snake boats descend from the war boats of the 13th century, when the rulers of Kayamkulam and the Chembakassery kingdom on the Kerala coast maintained fleets of long war canoes; legend credits King Devanarayana of Chembakassery with commissioning the first chundan vallam for war, and the oldest surviving boat, the Parthasarathi Chundan, is still in use (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). When peace came, the war boats turned to racing. The Nehru Trophy Boat Race traces to 22 December 1952, when Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru, visiting Alappuzha, watched an impromptu race of nine boats on Punnamada Lake — won by Nadubhagam — and donated a silver trophy; the first formal Nehru Trophy race was held in 1954 at Kainakary, and the event has been held on Punnamada Lake since (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The Karichal Chundan, launched on 8 September 1970, won the Nehru Trophy 16 times and is known as the "Emperor of the water", while the UBC Kainakary holds the record for the most overall trophies including hat-tricks (Wikipedia, n.d.-b).
