## Basic Information

Counter-Strike is a team-based first-person shooter developed by Valve, played competitively as Counter-Strike 2 (CS2) since 2023. Two teams of five (counter-terrorists vs terrorists) play on bomb-defusal and hostage maps; the most prestigious tournaments are the Valve Majors, supported by the game's premier circuit and ranked by HLTV.org. Professional play is governed by the tournament rules of the event organizers under Valve's game rules at counter-strike.net.

## Rules

In official competitive play, a match on a single map is best-of-one in group stages and best-of-three in playoffs at Majors. A map is played under MR12 rules: the first team to win 13 rounds wins the map (win by two), with a maximum of 24 rounds before overtime (MR3 overtime rounds, first to 4 round wins, repeating). Teams switch sides after 12 rounds. The in-game economy (money earned per round) drives buy rounds, and standard Major rules cover pauses, technical issues and player substitutions. Team rankings and the world title are determined at the Majors.

## History

Counter-Strike began in 1999 as a Half-Life mod by Minh Le and Jess Cliffe, became a standalone game in 2000, and grew into the world's largest competitive FPS esport through versions 1.6 and Global Offensive (CS:GO, 2012). Valve released CS2 in September 2023, replacing CS:GO on the professional circuit. Legendary players and teams include s1mple, ZywOo, device and NAVI's 2021 Major-winning roster; Majors are held roughly twice a year and draw millions of viewers.
