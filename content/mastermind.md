## Basic Information

Mastermind is the code-breaking game invented in 1970 by Mordecai Meirowitz (Israel) and sold by Invicta: one player sets a secret code of 4 colored pegs from 6 colors; the other guesses, getting black pegs for right color+position and white for right color only; 12 guesses to crack it. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Set](set.html), [Codenames](codenames.html), [Battleship](battleship.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

4-hole code, 6 colors (classic); feedback pegs: black = correct color+place, white = correct color wrong place; duplicates handled by standard rules; solve in 12 tries. World championships— a Mastermind world championship exists (mental calculation) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Invented by Mordecai Meirowitz in 1970 (inspired by the earlier bulls-and-cows code game); Invicta sold 50+ million copies; the 2005 sample game photo and the 2014 pieces photo document it (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
