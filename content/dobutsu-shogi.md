## Basic Information

Dobutsu shogi ('animal shogi') is the 3x4 children's game invented by professional shogi player Madoka Kitao in 2008; players move animal pieces (lion, giraffe, chick, hen, elephant) and win by capturing the enemy lion or moving their own lion to the far rank. It is the standard entry game to shogi worldwide. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Shogi](shogi.html), [Minishogi](minishogi.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Kitao, M, 2008).

## Rules

3x4 board; the chick promotes to hen; you win by capturing the opponent's lion or by moving your lion to the last rank ('try rule' in some rule sets). No drops, no checks (Kitao, M, 2008).

## History

Madoka Kitao designed dobutsu shogi in 2008 to teach children shogi; it is now used in schools across Japan and internationally, with tournaments (e.g. the Dobutsu Shogi World Championship for children) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
