## Basic Information

Dobble (Spot it! in the US) is the speed observation card game invented by Denis Blanchot: 55 round cards each with 8 symbols; any two cards share exactly one symbol; players race to spot it. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Set](set.html), [Halli Galli](halli-galli.html), [Uno](uno.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Asmodée, n.d.).

## Rules

55 cards, 8 symbols each from 57; any pair shares exactly one symbol; five game modes (e.g. 'Spot it', 'Hot Potato'); fastest to name the shared symbol scores (Asmodée, n.d.).

## History

Invented by Denis Blanchot in 2009 (published by Play Factory, Asmodée); sold 10+ million copies; the 'Playing Dobble' photo (2013) shows play (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
