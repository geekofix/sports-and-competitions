## Basic Information

Arm pull is a seated Arctic strength event: two competitors sit facing each other and pull a stick over a centre line with one arm, like a one-armed tug of war; the first to pull the opponent's hand across the line wins (AWG, n.d.; WEIO, n.d.). Men's, women's and youth categories at AWG and WEIO. Within the Arctic and Indigenous sports on this site, its closest relatives are [Blanket Toss](blanket-toss.html), [Snow Snake](snow-snake.html), [Two-Foot High Kick](two-foot-high-kick.html), [Finger Pull](finger-pull.html), [Hand Games](hand-games.html), [World Eskimo Indian Olympics](world-eskimo-indian-olympics.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

Seated with feet braced, competitors grip the stick at arm's length; on the referee's signal they pull. Best of three; the bout ends when a hand crosses the centre board (WEIO, n.d.).

## History

Arm pull derives from hauling and paddling strength tests in Arctic communities and has been a WEIO/AWG event since the 1970s (Wikipedia, n.d.).
