## Basic Information

Konane ('Hawaiian checkers') is the traditional jumping game of Hawaii played on an 8x8 (or larger) board filled with alternating black/white stones; players jump over an adjacent enemy stone into an empty space, removing it; the player left without a move loses. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Draughts](draughts.html), [Alquerque](alquerque.html), [Senet](senet.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Hawai'i Volcanoes NPS, n.d.).

## Rules

Board starts full alternating; multiple jumps in one turn are allowed (and often compulsory in some rule sets); no diagonal moves. The player unable to move loses. Played at Puʻuhonua o Hōnaunau and taught in Hawaiian schools (NPS photos) (Hawai'i Volcanoes NPS, n.d.).

## History

Konane is ancient in Hawaii (documented in 19th-century accounts); it is a cousin of draughts/checkers spread by Polynesians; the NPS teaches it at Hōnaunau (2008 photo) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
