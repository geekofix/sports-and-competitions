## Basic Information

Bodybuilding is a competitive sport in which athletes are judged on muscular size, symmetry, proportion and definition through a series of poses. The international governing body is the International Federation of Bodybuilding & Fitness (IFBB), which runs the IFBB Pro League and the amateur World Championships; the Mr. Olympia contest is the most prestigious professional title.

## Rules

Under IFBB rules, competitors perform mandatory poses (front double biceps, side chest, back double biceps, etc.) and a free posing routine to music in their division — men's bodybuilding, classic physique, men's physique, women's bodybuilding, bikini, fitness, wellness. Judges rank athletes in each round; at the Mr. Olympia and IFBB Pro League events, the winner of each division is crowned, with the overall Mr. Olympia title decided in the open bodybuilding division. Amateur athletes qualify for the IFBB World Championships and can earn a pro card. Anti-doping rules follow the World Anti-Doping Code.

## History

Modern bodybuilding was popularized by Eugen Sandow in the late 19th century, who staged the first physique contests. The Mr. Olympia contest began in 1965, and the IFBB was founded in 1946 by brothers Joe and Ben Weider. The 1970s film "Pumping Iron" made Arnold Schwarzenegger a global star; the sport's golden era included Lee Haney, Dorian Yates and Ronnie Coleman, and today Chris Bumstead dominates classic physique while Mr. Olympia crowns the open division champion.
