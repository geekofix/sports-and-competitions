## Basic Information

3x3 basketball (officially FIBA 3x3) is played on a half court with one basket: teams of four, with three players on court and one substitute. FIBA formalized the format in 2010, and it became an Olympic sport at Tokyo 2020. The FIBA 3x3 World Cup is the flagship event.

## Rules

Under FIBA 3x3 rules the shot clock is 12 seconds. A game lasts 10 minutes or ends as soon as a team reaches 21 points, whichever comes first; if the game is tied, overtime is first to 2 points. After a defensive rebound the ball must be taken behind the arc before a shot attempt; after a made basket, play resumes without a check-ball. Team fouls 6 through 9 award the opponent two free throws; from the 10th foul the opponent also receives possession. Shots from inside the arc count 1 point, from beyond it 2 points. Variants: streetball played in parks often uses house rules — games to 11 or 15 points, "1s and 2s", no free throws — so local formats vary widely.

## History

FIBA debuted 3x3 at the 2010 Youth Olympic Games in Singapore and held the first FIBA 3x3 World Championship in Athens in 2012. At Tokyo 2020, Latvia won the first Olympic men's gold (Miezis, Čavars, Lasmanis, Krūmiņš) and the United States won the women's gold (Dolson, Gray, Plum, Young). The Serbian men's team has dominated the World Cup era.
