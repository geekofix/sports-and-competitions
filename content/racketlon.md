## Basic Information

Racketlon is a combination sport in which the same two players — or doubles pairs — contest one match across all four major racket sports, [table tennis](table-tennis.html), [badminton](badminton.html), [squash](squash.html) and [tennis](tennis.html), playing one set in each sport and adding up every rally point. It is governed worldwide by the FIR — Federation of International Racketlon (founded 15 September 2002 as the International Racketlon Federation and renamed FIR), which counted 36 national member federations in March 2025 (Wikipedia, n.d.). Women and men compete in separate elite categories as well as in mixed national teams, and at the 2026 FIR World Championships (Singles & Teams), held 5–9 August 2026 at Victoria in Rotterdam, Netherlands, with about 500 players and over 80 teams, the women's elite title went first to 25-year-old Anaïs Ben Mansour of France, who beat Anna-Klara Ahlmer of Sweden in the final, while Koen Hageraats of the Netherlands won his first men's elite world title over Germany's Joerg Kanonenberg (FIR, n.d.; DWS Events, n.d.).

## Rules

Under the FIR Rules of Racketlon, a match consists of four sets played in the order table tennis, badminton, squash, tennis — from the smallest to the biggest racket — each with running score to 21 points and a two-point margin required (Wikipedia, n.d.). The winner of the match is whoever collects the most total points across all four sports, so a player can lose three sets and still win the match; if the total is tied, a single "gummiarm" (rubber arm) point is played in tennis with one serve only, and the winner of that rally takes the match (Wikipedia, n.d.). Each player serves two serves at a time — one from the right and one from the left, except in table tennis — and a single coin toss fixes the serve-and-receive order across all four sets: the table-tennis server receives in badminton, serves in squash and receives in tennis (Wikipedia, n.d.). In team competitions the individual matches are played to 11 points, and in doubles the squash set is played individually, one player from each pair until someone reaches 11, then the remaining players finish the set (Wikipedia, n.d.). Otherwise the rules of the four sports apply, with feather shuttlecocks, double-yellow-dot squash balls and ITTF/ITF-approved balls required at FIR events (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Racketlon grew out of "four-racket" experiments in Finland (mailapelit) and Sweden, where Peter Landberg's national tournament coined the word racketlon in 1989 and the format of four 21-point sets was settled by 1994 (Wikipedia, n.d.). The first international tournament was held in Gothenburg in 2001 as an unofficial world championship between Finland and Sweden, and the International Racketlon Federation was founded on 15 September 2002, later renamed FIR, running world championships for elite, seniors and junior classes plus doubles and national teams since 2003 (Wikipedia, n.d.). Among the icons of the sport are Denmark's Jesper Ratzer, unbeaten in well over a hundred elite singles matches through the 2010s, Britain's Luke Griffiths, a three-time world champion, and Austria's Christine Seehofer, the most decorated female player in the sport's history (Wikipedia, n.d.).
