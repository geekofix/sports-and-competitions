## Basic Information

Assetto Corsa (Kunos Simulazioni, 2014) and its sequel Assetto Corsa Competizione (2019) are PC/console sim racers famous for mods and league racing; ACC carries the GT World Challenge esports series run with SRO, alongside Kunos and league events, while the original AC lives on in drift and touge cups (Wikipedia, n.d.; Liquipedia, n.d.) (Kunos Simulazioni, n.d.).

## Rules

ACC competition rules: GT3/GT4 car classes with balance of performance, fixed or open setups by series; sprint (45 min) and endurance formats; stewards review incidents from replays; points per race; open to all genders (Liquipedia, n.d.).

## History

Assetto Corsa's esport roots are the drift scene and European league racing on the original AC; ACC became an official esport platform with the GT World Challenge esports series from 2020 (Wikipedia, n.d.).
