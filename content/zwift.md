## Basic Information

Zwift is the subscription virtual-world platform where cyclists and runners ride and race online on real-world-inspired courses (Watopia, London, Makuri) with smart trainers and power meters; Zwift hosted the first UCI Cycling Esports World Championships (2020, 2022, 2023) and remains the biggest mass-participation racing platform with its Zwift Racing League and category racing by w/kg (Wikipedia, n.d.; UCI, n.d.) (Zwift, n.d.). The 2026 UCI Cycling Esports World Championships are on MyWhoosh in Abu Dhabi (final 5 December 2026); past UCI champions include Jason Osborne (men, 2020/2024/2025) and Mary Kate McCarthy (women, 2024/2025) (UCI, n.d.).

## Rules

Zwift racing rules: riders are categorized by functional threshold power per kilogram (categories A+ down to E); mass-start races with drafting, sprint and king-of-the-mountain segments and power-ups; at UCI level, verified hardware data (dual power recording, height and weight checks) is required; men's and women's races in every category (UCI, n.d.).

## History

Zwift launched in 2015 (founded by Jon Mayfield and Eric Min) and grew from a training app into the world's largest online racing scene with the Zwift Racing League, Zwift Academy talent ID and the UCI partnership from 2020 (Wikipedia, n.d.).
