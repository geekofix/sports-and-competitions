## Basic Information

Rugby league sevens is a seven-a-side derivative of rugby league, played under substantially the same laws with shorter matches, and is popular with social, workplace and pub teams because a full squad of 13 is not needed; it is used by the International Rugby League as a development tool for emerging nations (Wikipedia, n.d.). Teams are composed of three forwards, one half and three backs, with squads of up to 12 and unlimited substitutions (Wikipedia, n.d.). The historic premier event was the World Sevens in Sydney, played before the NRL season until it was suspended in 2005; today the format thrives at festivals worldwide, including the annual Latino Rugby League Sevens in Cabramatta, Sydney, founded in 2015 for Latin American community teams (Wikipedia, n.d.). Women play league sevens in growing numbers at club and festival level, with women's sevens squads fielded in several countries alongside the men's events (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## Rules

Sevens follows the IRL International Laws of the Game with exceptions: matches have two halves of seven minutes (the World Sevens final was played over two ten-minute halves; at the 2004 World Sevens the tackle limit was reduced from six to four) (Wikipedia, n.d.). Scrums are reduced to three forwards per side, all kicks for goal are drop kicks, and after points are scored the non-scoring side restarts with a tap kick (Wikipedia, n.d.). Scoring is unchanged from league: try 4, conversion 2, penalty goal 2, drop goal 1 (International Rugby League, n.d.). The game is played on a full-size rugby league field, and drawn matches go to extra time, first score wins (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

The sevens format itself was invented by Ned Haig in Melrose, Scotland, in 1883 — over a decade before the 1895 schism that created rugby league — but the first rugby league sevens tournament was only played in Australia in 1961 (Wikipedia, n.d.). A landmark match was the 1933 Australia–England game at Roundhay Park, Leeds, which drew a record 80,000 for rugby league sevens, attended by royalty (Wikipedia, n.d.). The Sydney World Sevens, staged annually before the NRL season from 1988, was suspended in 2005 as NRL clubs withheld star players, but play resumed at other levels, and the Orara Valley Axemen Sevens at Coffs Harbour, hosted by actor Russell Crowe, became another major festival (Wikipedia, n.d.).
