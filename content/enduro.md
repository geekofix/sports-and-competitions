## Basic Information

Enduro is an off-road motorcycle sport in which riders race against the clock over long courses that combine timed special tests — specials — with untimed liaison sections on public roads (FIM, n.d.-b). It is governed worldwide by the Fédération Internationale de Motocyclisme (FIM), which runs the FIM Enduro World Championship (EnduroGP), the premier series with an overall EnduroGP title and the Enduro 1, Enduro 2 and Enduro 3 classes plus junior and women's categories (FIM, n.d.-b). The 2026 FIM Enduro World Championship was the 37th season, with seven rounds from Custonaci in Italy (10–12 April) through Oliana in Spain, Vierumäki in Finland, two rounds at Fafe in Portugal, Saint-Agrève in France and Rhayader in Wales (7–9 August); Spain's Josep García of Red Bull KTM Factory Racing defended both the EnduroGP and Enduro 1 titles — his eighth and ninth world championships — while Italy's Andrea Verona defended Enduro 2 and Britain's Steve Holcombe took Enduro 3 (FIM, n.d.-a). At the end of 2026, the leading current riders are — in the women's class — the FIM Women's Enduro World Championship contenders headed by the 2026 class champion, and — in the men's classes — EnduroGP champion Josep García, Andrea Verona and Steve Holcombe (FIM, n.d.-a).

## Rules

The FIM Enduro World Championship is run under the FIM Enduro Technical Regulations: each round combines a number of special tests — timed sections on closed circuits, extreme tests such as hill climbs and rock gardens, and motocross-style tests — with road liaison sections that must be ridden within the road-legal time allowances (FIM, n.d.-b). Riders start at set intervals and are ranked by the total of their special-test times plus any time penalties; liaison penalties, missed checkpoints and exceeding time in road sections add time, and exceeding the maximum lateness at a check causes elimination (FIM, n.d.-b). Bikes must be road-legal — lights, horn, registration — and are grouped into the Enduro 1, Enduro 2 and Enduro 3 classes by engine capacity and preparation, with the EnduroGP overall title across all three, plus junior (under-23) and women's world championships (FIM, n.d.-b). Riders must wear FIM-approved helmets, boots, gloves and body protection, and doping control applies (FIM, n.d.-b). Men's and women's classes use the same courses and rules, with the women's title awarded separately (FIM, n.d.-b).

## History

Enduro grew out of early twentieth-century reliability trials, in which motorcyclists rode long distances over poor roads to prove their machines — events such as the International Six Days Trial (ISDT), first held in 1913 and the forerunner of today's International Six Days Enduro (ISDE) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The FIM Enduro World Championship has run since the 1980s as the successor to the European Championship, and classes have shifted over the decades between 125 cc two-strokes and today's 450 cc four-strokes (FIM, n.d.-b). Icons of the sport include Spain's six-time world trials champion Carles Mas (1934–2020) — his works Montesa is pictured in the Commons collection — Britain's five-time world enduro champion David Knight and Australia's Stefan Merriman, while the women's class has grown into a full world championship with its own title (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
