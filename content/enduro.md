## Basic Information

Enduro is a motorcycle off-road racing discipline in which riders race against the clock over a long loop of special test stages linked by untimed liaison sections on public or private roads, testing speed, navigation and stamina. The world championship is the FIM Enduro World Championship (formerly the FIM Enduro World Championship / ISDE-era), organized by the Fédération Internationale de Motocyclisme (FIM); the historic flagship is the International Six Days Enduro (ISDE), now the FIM ISDE World Trophy.

## Rules

At FIM Enduro World Championship events, riders complete several timed special tests per day over 2-3 days, with a total of around 200-300 km per day including liaison sections; the rider with the lowest accumulated time wins. Time checks ("time controls") on the liaison route enforce the road-legal pace: arriving early or late at a check costs time penalties. Tests include special stages on enduro terrain (rock sections, hill climbs, motocross-style sections) and extreme enduro elements at some rounds. Riders must be FIM-licensed, bikes must be road-legal with lights, and a strict parc fermé applies. At the FIM ISDE, national teams of six riders compete for the World Trophy (the main classification) and the Women's Trophy, with medals awarded by the percentage of riders finishing within time.

## History

Enduro evolved from the early 20th-century reliability trials in Europe, where motorcyclists rode long distances on unreliable machines; the first International Six Days Trial was held in 1913 in the UK (Carlisle). The ISDE became the de facto world championship from 1980 and was rebranded as the FIM ISDE World Trophy. The FIM, founded in 1904 in Paris, runs the Enduro World Championship as a separate series since 2016 (previously the EnduroGP). Nations such as Finland, Sweden, Italy, Spain, Australia and the UK have dominated; legendary riders include Stefan Merriman, Johnny Walker, Tadeusz Błażusiak (multiple Six Days gold medalist) and modern stars like Josep Garcia and Zachary Osher. The sport also includes hard enduro events (Erzbergrodeo, Red Bull Romaniacs) as an extreme variant.
