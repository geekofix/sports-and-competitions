## Basic Information

Sudoku is the logic number-placement puzzle on a 9x9 grid: fill each row, column and 3x3 box with 1–9; the World Sudoku Championship (WSC) has been held since 2006 (e.g. Sofia 2015, photographed). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Crossword](crossword.html), [Kakuro](kakuro.html), [Nonogram](nonogram.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

9x9 grid with given clues; each row/column/box contains 1–9 exactly once; unique solution required in competition puzzles; solving speed and accuracy are judged at the WSC (individual + team) (World Sudoku Championship, n.d.).

## History

Sudoku was popularized by Nikoli in Japan from the 1980s (name from 'sūji wa dokushin ni kagiru') and by Wayne Gould's Times puzzles from 2004; the World Sudoku Championship since 2006 (Prague 2018 photo) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
