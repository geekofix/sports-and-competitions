## Basic Information

Uno is the shedding card game invented by Merle Robbins in 1971 (Cincinnati) and sold by Mattel from 1972: players match the top discard by color or number/symbol; action cards (Skip, Reverse, Draw Two, Wild, Wild Draw Four) change play; first to shed all cards wins (others score penalty points). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Halli Galli](halli-galli.html), [Dobble](dobble.html), [Dou Dizhu](dou-dizhu.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Mattel, n.d.).

## Rules

108-card deck (colors red/yellow/green/blue, 0–9, action cards, wilds); match color/number/symbol; call 'Uno' with one card left (penalty if caught); Wild Draw Four challenge rules; scoring to 500 in match play. World championships— Uno world championships are held (Mattel, n.d.).

## History

Invented by Merle Robbins in 1971 (the name from Spanish 'one', (after the Spanish word for 'one'); Mattel bought the rights in 1972; 150+ million decks sold; the PD Uno card image (2005) documents the deck (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
