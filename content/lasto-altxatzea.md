## Basic Information

Lasto Altxatzea is the Basque hay-bale (lasto fardoa) lifting contest: competitors raise a straw bale (typically 20–40 kg) from the ground onto a raised platform, as many times as possible within the time limit. It is a common herri kirolak event at harvest festivals in the Aiaraldea and Lea-Artibai valleys (Euskal Herri Kirolak Elkartea, n.d.).

## Rules

The bale is weighed in advance; a repetition counts when the bale is placed stably on the platform. The time limit is usually 2–5 minutes; chalk is allowed, no extra grip aids. Men's and women's categories exist at modern championships (Euskal Herri Kirolak Elkartea, n.d.).

## History

Lifting hay and straw bales was everyday farm work; competitive bale lifting at harvest fêtes is documented from the early 20th century. The 2012 photograph from Larrinbeko shows a bale being moved at Aiaraldea; today the event is part of the Herri Kirolak circuit (Wikipedia, n.d.).
