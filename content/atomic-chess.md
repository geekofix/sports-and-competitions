## Basic Information

Atomic chess is a chess variant in which every capture also 'explodes': the captured piece and all pieces on adjacent squares (except kings) are removed from the board. Checkmate still wins; a king can also be 'mated' by explosion; kings are immune to capture but can be checkmated. Within the chess variants and mind games on this site, its closest relatives are [Antichess](antichess.html), [King of the Hill Chess](king-of-the-hill-chess.html), [Chess](chess.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Lichess, n.d.).

## Rules

Standard moves; on a capture, the captured piece and every piece (own or enemy) on the eight surrounding squares are removed, except kings. Checkmate of the enemy king wins; stalemate counts as a win for the stalemating side in most rule sets (lichess). Pawns capture-explode like other pieces but do not promote by explosion (Lichess, n.d.).

## History

Atomic chess appeared in the 1980s and became a standard online variant in the 2000s; lichess runs an annual atomic world championship (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
