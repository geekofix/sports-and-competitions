## Basic Information

Virtua Fighter is Sega AM2's 3D arcade fighting game and the first 3D fighter (1993); Virtua Fighter 5 Ultimate Showdown (2021) is the current competitive version and an EVO Japan slot (Wikipedia, n.d.) (Sega, n.d.). Matches: VF-specific evade — evade and sabaki parry systems; best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 finals), 45-second ; open to all genders.

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are [Tekken](tekken.html), [Dead or Alive](dead-or-alive.html), [Soulcalibur](soulcalibur.html) and the other [fighting games](street-fighter.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

Competitive rules: best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 grand finals), 45-second rounds, ring-outs, evade — the evade system; Virtua Fighter 5 REVO World Stage tour; open brackets, all genders (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Virtua Fighter debuted in 1993 at arcades (Yu Suzuki's AM2) and defined 3D fighting; VF5 ran at EVO from 2012, and the REVO World Stage circuit runs the current game (Wikipedia, n.d.).
