## Basic Information

Onyx is a 19x19 go-like alignment game invented in 2004 in Brussels: players place black and white rings; a player wins by connecting two opposite edges with a chain of their color or by capturing— the goal is to form a connected path of your color across the board. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Go](go.html), [Hex](hex.html), [Havannah](havannah.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

19x19 board; black and white stones form rings (a ring is two adjacent same-color stones?); connecting opposite sides with your color wins; capture rules apply for surrounded groups (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Invented in 2004 (Brussels) as a go-like connection game; it is played competitively online (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
