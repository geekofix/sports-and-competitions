## Basic Information

The Excel World Championship (Financial Modeling World Cup, FMWC) is the world championship of financial modeling in Microsoft Excel, organized by the Financial Modeling World Cup organization. Competitors solve timed case models — building forecasts, valuations and merger or LBO models — and are ranked on speed and accuracy, with a Grand Finals in Las Vegas each December.

## Rules

The FMWC season runs in monthly online stages (Division 1, 2 and 3 plus a Qualifier) and regional championships; each stage is a timed exam (typically 30-60 minutes per case) in Excel where competitors must replicate a given model from a case description and answer output questions. Scoring is based on correct answers (accuracy) with time as a tiebreaker; only the top performers advance from each stage. The annual Grand Finals bring the best modelers to Las Vegas for a multi-round, in-person competition with a large prize pool (the 2024 champion won US$200,000 from a US$1 million total prize pool). Microsoft sponsors the event and provides the platform; the Model Inspecta / FMWC rules govern permissible functions and external resources.

## History

Financial modeling competitions grew out of university investment clubs and the CFA Institute's Research Challenge in the 2000s. The Financial Modeling World Cup was founded in 2019 by Andrew Hollingworth and has become the premier global competition for Excel modelers, attracting tens of thousands of participants from over 100 countries each season, with strong participation from investment banking and private equity. Microsoft became a partner, and the event is now a recognized pathway into finance careers; past champions include Andrew Hollingworth-adjacent talents such as the 2020-2023 winners who went on to roles in banking and funds. The FMWC also runs a school and university program to promote modeling skills.
