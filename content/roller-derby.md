## Basic Information

Roller derby is a full-contact team sport played on quad roller skates around a flat oval track, in which each team fields up to five skaters per jam — one jammer (star helmet cover) and four blockers, one of whom may be the pivot (stripe) — and the jammer scores one point for each opposing blocker passed after the initial trip through the pack (WFTDA, n.d.). The modern flat-track game exploded from the United States after the Women's Flat Track Derby Association (WFTDA) was founded in 2005 and codified the Rules of Flat Track Roller Derby, now published in yearly editions — the current edition is dated 1 January 2025 (WFTDA, n.d.). The WFTDA counts 397 full and 48 apprentice member leagues; alongside it, the Men's Roller Derby Association (MRDA) governs men's play and co-ed leagues exist too, so women and men both compete at the highest level, in separate or mixed structures (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). World Skate also sanctions roller derby internationally and included it in the World Skate Games, with rulebook changes announced for January 2026 (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). Safety gear — helmet, wrist guards, elbow pads, knee pads and mouth guard — is mandatory, and players skate on quad skates (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

Under the WFTDA Rules of Flat Track Roller Derby (edition of 1 January 2025), a bout consists of two 30-minute periods, divided into jams of up to two minutes; the lead jammer — the first to legally pass all opposing blockers — may call the jam off early, and a star pass lets the pivot take over the jammer role (WFTDA, n.d.). Each team fields up to five skaters per jam: one jammer and at most four blockers, at least one of whom must not be serving a penalty (WFTDA, n.d.). The jammer's first pass through the pack scores nothing; on every subsequent pass one point is earned per opposing blocker passed legally, including skaters in the penalty box (WFTDA, n.d.). The pack is the largest group of in-bounds, upright blockers from both teams in proximity, and blockers must stay within 20 ft (6 m) of the pack to block legally; contact is allowed within the rules of the game but illegal hits — hits to the head, back, below the knee, or with elbows — draw penalties (WFTDA, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-a). A skater who commits seven penalties is expelled, and a major penalty costs 30 seconds in the penalty box (WFTDA, n.d.). The team with the most points at the end of the bout wins; ties go to overtime jams (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The game is played on a flat oval track roughly 88–110 ft (27–33 m) around, marked with an inside boundary line; leagues play women's, men's (MRDA) and co-ed divisions under the same core rules (Wikipedia, n.d.-a; Wikipedia, n.d.-b).

## History

Roller derby originated in the United States in the 1930s as a endurance racing spectacle on banked tracks, televised in the 1960s–1970s, before the game was reimagined in 2001 in Austin, Texas, as a grassroots, women-led flat-track game; the Women's Flat Track Derby Association was founded in 2005 and grew to hundreds of leagues (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The first Roller Derby World Cup was held in 2011 in Toronto, won by Team USA (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Men's flat-track derby organized early through the Men's Derby Coalition, founded 2007 and renamed the Men's Roller Derby Association (MRDA), with the first MRDA Championship in 2010 (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). Since 2017 roller derby has also been part of the World Skate Games under World Skate, which published its own roller derby rulebook changes for January 2026 (Wikipedia, n.d.-b).
