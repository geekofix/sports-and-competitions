## Basic Information

Belote (and its cousin coinche) is the national card game of France and very popular in Eastern Europe: four players in two partnerships with a 32-card deck (7–A); the declarer's team must score more than half of the 162 card points with melds (declarations) and tricks in the trump suit. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Whist](whist.html), [Durak](durak.html), [Sixty-Six](sixty-six.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## Rules

32-card deck; bidding for the trump suit (no-trump 'tout' or capot bids); declarations: four aces, four kings, four queens, four jacks, belote-roi (K-Q of trump); tricks follow suit, trump beats; the declaring side needs more than half of the 162 points (82 or more). Capot (all tricks) scores extra (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## History

Belote was invented in France in 1921 commonly dated to 1921, possibly derived from the older klaverjassen/jass; it spread through France and the Balkans (Croatia, Serbia); the example card layouts (perso, 2007, PD) show typical plays (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
