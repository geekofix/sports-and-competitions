## Basic Information

Chu shogi (middle shogi) is the 12x12 medieval Japanese chess variant from the Heian period with 46 piece types, including the powerful dragon king and dragon horse; it is the direct ancestor of modern shogi. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Shogi](shogi.html), [Dai Shogi](dai-shogi.html), [Tenjiku Shogi](tenjiku-shogi.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

12x12 board; 46 piece types with promotions; drops allowed as in shogi; checkmate wins. Played at the imperial court from the 10th–12th centuries (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Chu shogi flourished in the Heian period (10th–12th c.) at the Japanese court; it was superseded by dai shogi and then modern shogi in the 16th century. Modern enthusiasts play reconstructions; historic boards are preserved (e.g. the Shutter shogi board photo in Kyoto) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
