## Basic Information

Roller hockey — rink hockey in American English, quad hockey — is a fast team sport played on quad roller skates, in which two teams of five (four skaters plus a goalkeeper) drive a hard ball with curved sticks into the opponent's goal (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). It is governed by World Skate, successor of the FIRS founded on 21 April 1924; the first Roller Hockey World Championship was held in Stuttgart in 1936, and since 1989 the men's World Cup has run biennially on odd years, with a women's World Cup since 1998 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Traditional powers are Spain (18 men's world titles), Portugal (16), Argentina (6, including the 2022 title and the 1992 Olympic demonstration event in Barcelona) and Italy (4) (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). Since 2017 the world championship has been part of the World Skate Games (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Both women and men play at the highest level — Argentina's women have won six World Cups, most recently in 2022 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

Rink hockey is played on a rectangular rink (standard about 40 × 20 m) with a hard round ball and curved sticks similar to bandy and shinty; each team has four skaters and one goalkeeper on the rink at a time (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). Senior matches last two 25-minute halves with a 15-minute halftime; if tied, up to two 5-minute golden-goal periods with the clock stopped when the ball is dead follow, and a penalty shootout decides if still level (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The rink markings include a halfway line, an "anti-play" line 22 m from each end wall, a penalty area 9 × 5.4 m in front of each goal and a 1.5 m-radius goalkeeper protection area (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). Excessive contact is forbidden — unlike inline hockey, bodychecking is not allowed — and fouls are punished with free hits, penalty strokes from the penalty spot, and blue and red cards for serious or repeated offences (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The goalkeeper wears extensive padding (shin guards up to 75 cm), a neck guard, forearm gloves and a helmet with grid or visor, and uses a flat batting glove rather than a catcher (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The World Skate Rink Hockey Technical Commission publishes the current rulebook (2026 edition) covering all categories, with U-19 matches played as two 20-minute periods (World Skate, n.d.). Women's and men's World Cups and European championships (CERH) run in parallel, and since 2017 the elite world title is decided at the World Skate Games (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

The first recorded hard-ball roller hockey game was played in 1878 at the Denmark Rink in London, where skaters adapted polo on wheels and called it "roller polo"; the National Roller Polo League formed in Dayton, Ohio, in 1882 (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The international federation was founded in 1924, the first World Championship followed in Stuttgart in 1936, and after World War II the event resumed in 1947 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Rink hockey was a demonstration sport at the 1992 Barcelona Olympics, won by Argentina (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The 2007 World Championship in Montreux saw Argentina triumph again, and the 2022 World Cup was won by Argentina in both the men's and women's tournaments (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
