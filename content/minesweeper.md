## Basic Information

Minesweeper is the single-player logic game shipped with Windows since 1990: clear a minefield using number clues (adjacent mine counts) without detonating a mine; speedrunning (beginner/intermediate/expert) has world records (Minesweeper.info). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Sudoku](sudoku.html), [Nonogram](nonogram.html), [Kakuro](kakuro.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Minesweeper.info, n.d.).

## Rules

Grid with hidden mines (30 in 16x16 expert); click a cell: number shows adjacent mines; flag mines; clear all non-mine cells to win; 3BV speed metric determines speedrun records (world record under two seconds on expert) (Minesweeper.info, n.d.).

## History

Written by Curt Johnson for Windows 2.0 (written in 1989, shipped with Windows in 1990); became a global classic; competitive speedrunning with official world rankings (minesweeper.info) since the 2000s (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
