## Basic Information

Jenga is the stacking/dexterity game invented by Leslie Scott in 1983 (name from Swahili 'kujenga', to build) and sold by Parker Brothers: players pull one block from a 54-block tower (3 per level) and place it on top; the tower must not fall. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Twister](twister.html), [Connect Four](connect-four.html), [Blokus](blokus.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

54 blocks (18 levels of 3); on your turn remove one block from any level below the top and place it on top crosswise; the tower must stand after your turn; first to topple loses. World records (tallest tower) exist (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Invented by Leslie Scott in the 1970s–83 in Africa (she played with wooden blocks), trademarked 1983; Parker Brothers sold it; the distorted-tower photo (2012) shows a game (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
