## Basic Information

Codenames (Vlaada Chvátil, 2015, Czech Games Edition) is the word-association party game for 2+ teams: spymasters give one-word clues + a number to identify their agents on a 5x5 grid; avoid the assassin. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Taboo](taboo.html), [Mastermind](mastermind.html), [Avalon](avalon.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Czech Games Edition, n.d.).

## Rules

25 word cards (5x5); two spymasters know the key; clue = one word + number; guessing wrong can hit the opponent's agent or the assassin (instant loss); first team to find all their agents wins. World Codenames championship exists (Czech Games Edition, n.d.).

## History

Designed by Vlaada Chvátil, published 2015 by Czech Games Edition; won the 2016 Spiel des Jahres; the Finland board-game-fair photos (JIP, 2015–2020) show play (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
