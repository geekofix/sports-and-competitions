## Basic Information

Tennis is the racket sport played on a rectangular court divided by a net: two or four players trade strokes with strung rackets, scoring points as 15, 30, 40 and game in the sport's unique counting, on surfaces from grass and clay to hardcourt. An Olympic sport since the first modern Games and a global professional circuit of four Grand Slam tournaments, it is one of the oldest and richest of all ball games.

## Rules

Serves alternate between court sides each point, must land in the diagonally opposite service box, and offer two attempts with the whole racket behind the baseline. Play continues with rallies in which the ball must return over the net inside the court's lines after one bounce; scoring runs love, 15, 30, 40, game (with deuce and advantage resolving ties at 40-40), games to 6 for a set (with a tiebreak at 6-6), and matches to the best of three or five sets. Singles plays the narrow court's inner lines, doubles the wider alleys with a defined serving rotation, while equipment rules govern racket dimensions, ball specs and hawk-eye electronic line calling, which professional events use on every line.

## History

Tennis grew out of the medieval French jeu de paume, a handball game of monastery courtyards and royal halls; rackets arrived by the sixteenth century and the indoor "real tennis" courts of Europe survive as the sport's oldest venues. Major Walter Wingfield's 1873 lawn tennis patent and the All England Club's 1877 championships at Wimbledon created the modern outdoor game, and the four majors soon followed as institutions (Wimbledon, the US, French and Australian championships). The Open era from 1968 admitted professionals to the grand slams and built the world tour; the sport's stars from Suzanne Lenglen through the amateur-age legends of the 1920s and 1970s to the Open era's changing of the guard have made tennis' eras a century-long argument over who played best, while the Grand Slam calendar, rankings and women's and men's tours govern the professional sport today.
