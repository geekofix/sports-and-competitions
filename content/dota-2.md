## Basic Information

Dota 2 is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) esports game developed and published by Valve Corporation, released in 2013, in which two teams of five players each destroy the opposing team's Ancient — the central structure in their base — while controlling heroes with unique abilities (Valve Corporation, n.d.). The competitive scene centers on Valve's annual world championship, The International (TI), whose prize pool is crowdfunded by the in-game Battle Pass. The International 2026 (TI 15) ran 13–23 August 2026 at the Shanghai Oriental Sports Center in China with 16 teams: a Swiss-stage group phase 13–16 August and playoffs 20–23 August (HLTV, 2026). In the grand final on 23 August 2026, Team Spirit beat Team Vision 3–2 to lift the Aegis of Champions for the third time, after 2021 and 2023; Team Falcons had won TI 2025 in Hamburg (HLTV, 2026). At the end of 2026, the leading current professional players are the members of Team Spirit's 2026 championship roster — Yaroslav "Yatoro" Naidenov, Stanislav "Larl" Sivtsev, Magomed "Collapse" Khalilov, Danil "not_me" Skutin and Daler "rue" Minakov — and the runners-up of Team Vision (HLTV, 2026; Liquipedia, n.d.) — the sections below cover rules, history and more.

Women compete alongside men in open qualifiers, and dedicated women's teams and tournaments exist in the Dota 2 scene.

## Rules

A Dota 2 match is played on a square map with two bases connected by three lanes; each of the ten players controls one hero, and the team that destroys the enemy Ancient first wins (Valve Corporation, n.d.). Matches at The International use a best-of-five grand-final format and a double-elimination playoff bracket after the group stage, with Valve's official rules governing drafts, pauses and remakes (HLTV, 2026). The competitive season is organized into the Dota Pro Circuit, with regional leagues feeding the Major tournaments and The International; standings and results are tracked by HLTV and Liquipedia (HLTV, n.d.; Liquipedia, n.d.). There are no gender-separated leagues: men and women compete together on the same rosters, and Valve requires all professional players to meet the age-of-majority requirements of their region (Valve Corporation, n.d.). The 2026 event featured 16 invited teams, a base prize pool of 1.6 million US dollars plus crowdfunding, and the Swiss-stage format introduced in recent years (HLTV, 2026).

## History

Dota 2 grew out of the Warcraft III mod Defense of the Ancients (DotA); Valve hired the mod's lead developer, IceFrog, and released Dota 2 as a free-to-play game in July 2013 (Wikipedia, n.d.). Valve staged the first The International in 2011 at Gamescom in Cologne with a one-million-dollar prize for the champions, setting the template for crowdfunded esports prize pools (Wikipedia, n.d.). The International became an annual event with rotating host cities — Seattle, Vancouver, Beijing, Bucharest, Los Angeles, and Hamburg in 2025 — and iconic moments such as OG's comeback victory at TI8 in 2018 and Team Spirit's back-to-back titles in 2021 and 2023 (HLTV, 2026). The 2026 edition in Shanghai marked TI 15 and Team Spirit's record third Aegis (HLTV, 2026).
