## Basic Information

Latrunculi ('game of the soldiers') is the Roman board game played on a grid (often 8x8 or 7x8): soldiers move orthogonally any distance like a rook until blocked, and capture by custodial enclosure (surrounding an enemy on two opposite sides). It is a possible ancestor of tafl games. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Hnefatafl](hnefatafl.html), [Tablut](tablut.html), [Alquerque](alquerque.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

Grid board; pieces move like rooks but cannot jump; capture by surrounding an enemy piece on opposite sides (or against the board edge in some versions). Last player with pieces wins (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Played throughout the Roman Empire (boards and pieces found at Complutum, Spain, 2024 excavation; British Museum set 2024); described by Roman writers; possibly influenced medieval tafl games (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
