## Basic Information

Sledge jump is an Arctic Winter Games event: the athlete starts on a small wooden sledge, pushes off and jumps forward, landing on both feet on the snow; distance from the sledge's start to the heels is measured (AWG, n.d.). Men's and women's divisions are contested. Within the Arctic and Indigenous sports on this site, its closest relatives are [Blanket Toss](blanket-toss.html), [Snow Snake](snow-snake.html), [Two-Foot High Kick](two-foot-high-kick.html), [Finger Pull](finger-pull.html), [Hand Games](hand-games.html), [World Eskimo Indian Olympics](world-eskimo-indian-olympics.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

The competitor sits or kneels on the sledge at the start line, pushes off with the hands and jumps clear of the sledge, landing on both feet. Three attempts; best jump counts (AWG, n.d.).

## History

Sledge jump mimics leaping from a sledge while travelling — a practical Inuit skill — and has been an AWG Arctic sport since the 1970s (Wikipedia, n.d.).
