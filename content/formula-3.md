## Basic Information

The FIA Formula 3 Championship is the FIA's second feeder series below Formula One, contested as support to selected Formula 1 and Formula 2 events; the 2026 season is the 8th under the F3 name, with all cars built by Dallara (F3 2025 chassis) and powered by a Mecachrome 3.4-litre naturally aspirated V6 on Pirelli tyres (FIA, n.d.-b; FIA, n.d.-a). The 2026 drivers' champion is Ugo Ugochukwu of the USA, driving for Campos Racing, who clinched the title with a second place in the final race at the new Madring circuit in Madrid, while Campos Racing also defended the teams' championship; the 2025 champion was Rafael Câmara of Brazil, who won as a rookie for Trident before graduating to Formula 2 (FIA, n.d.-a). The 2026 calendar included the new Madrid round at Madring alongside classic venues (FIA, n.d.-a) (FIA, n.d.-b).

On the women's side, the leading young female single-seater talents come through the F1 Academy and national F4 series, which feed into FIA F3 — the championship itself is open to all genders (F1 Academy, n.d.).

## Rules

The FIA Formula 3 Sporting Regulations govern the series (FIA, n.d.-c). A race weekend consists of free practice and qualifying on Friday, a sprint race on Saturday (reverse top-12 grid from qualifying) and the feature race on Sunday; the sprint awards points 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1 and the feature 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1, with 2 points for pole position and 1 for the fastest lap in each race (FIA, n.d.-c). All teams use the identical Dallara F3 2025 chassis with the Mecachrome V6 and Pirelli control tyres; aerodynamic development is frozen, keeping costs low. Points scored count toward the FIA Super Licence (40 points needed; the F3 champion receives 40), the mandatory qualification for Formula 1; a rookie classification (best freshman) runs alongside the overall standings, and the teams' championship is decided by the combined scores of each team's two cars (FIA, n.d.-c). The series is open to all drivers aged 16 and over holding the required licence, and women compete on equal terms — several female drivers have raced in FIA F3 in recent seasons (FIA, n.d.-c).

## History

The modern FIA Formula 3 Championship was created in 2019 when the GP3 Series (founded 2010) merged with the old FIA Formula 3 European Championship; its early champions were Robert Shwartzman (2019), Oscar Piastri (2020), Dennis Hauger (2021), Victor Martins (2022) and Gabriel Bortoleto's generation followed, with Rafael Câmara winning the 2025 title as a rookie before moving to Formula 2 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a; FIA, n.d.-a). The feeder ladder as a whole has shaped generations of stars: Ayrton Senna won the 1983 British Formula 3 title, Max Verstappen dominated the 2014 European Formula 3 championship as a 16-year-old, and Mick Schumacher was the 2018 European F3 champion before reaching F1 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Ugo Ugochukwu's 2026 title with Campos Racing continues that tradition of producing future Formula 1 drivers (FIA, n.d.-a).
