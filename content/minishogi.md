## Basic Information

Minishogi is the 5x5 variant of shogi (Japanese chess): no piece drops, the king starts on the board, and the goal is checkmate; it is the most popular shogi variant for beginners and quick games. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Shogi](shogi.html), [Dobutsu Shogi](dobutsu-shogi.html), [Kyoto Shogi](kyoto-shogi.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Shogi Association, n.d.).

## Rules

5x5 board; pieces: king, rook, bishop, gold generals, silver generals, knights, lances, pawns (five pawns per side, with the lance/knight/gold/silver/rook/bishop/king formation). Captured pieces are NOT dropped (unlike shogi); mate wins (Shogi Association, n.d.).

## History

Minishogi was formalized in Japan in the 20th century as an introduction to shogi; it is now a standard variant on shogi servers and in schools (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
