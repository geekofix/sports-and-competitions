## Basic Information

The squat is the first of the three lifts in competitive [powerlifting](powerlifting.html): the barbell is taken on the back, the lifter descends until the hip crease is below the top of the knee, then stands erect again under the referee's commands (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). It opens every full powerlifting competition and is widely regarded as the most technically demanding lift to judge, because depth and control are assessed visually by three referees. Among the current leading women, the American Bonica Brown holds classic and equipped world records in the 84+ kg class — including an equipped 310.5 kg at the 2017 World Games and a 280 kg classic squat world record in 2023 — with women's squat records contested in every bodyweight class; among the men, the open classic record stands above 360 kg (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

Under IPF technical rules, the lifter takes the bar from the uprights, steps back and stands upright and motionless with knees locked; the chief referee then gives the "squat" command and the lifter descends, bending the knees until the top surface of the legs at the hip joint is lower than the top of the knees, then recovers to an upright position with knees locked and stands motionless before the "rack" command (International Powerlifting Federation, 2026). Faults that void the attempt include double-bouncing out of the bottom, any downward movement during recovery, failure to reach depth, shifting the feet, contacting the bar or supports with the plates, or dumping the bar. Equipped lifters wear a supportive squat suit and knee wraps; classic lifters may use only knee sleeves, a belt and wrist wraps. Each lifter gets three attempts, single-lift squat world championships run on the same technical basis, and men's and women's classes exist across all bodyweight categories plus junior and masters age groups (Wikipedia, n.d.-b).

## History

Squatting with a barbell on the shoulders descends from 19th- and early-20th-century strongman "deep knee bends"; the lift assumed its modern form as racks and standardized barbells spread through mid-century gyms, and in the odd-lift contests it was fixed as the opening discipline of powerlifting (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). In the equipped era, squat suits and knee wraps allowed totals far beyond raw lifting; in the IPF's classic era from 2012, raw squat world records have been reset repeatedly by women such as Bonica Brown and men in the heaviest classes (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
