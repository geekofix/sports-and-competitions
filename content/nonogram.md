## Basic Information

Nonogram (Picross in Nintendo games, 1990s) is the logic picture puzzle: row/column clue numbers give the lengths of consecutive filled blocks; shading cells according to clues reveals a hidden picture. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Kakuro](kakuro.html), [Sudoku](sudoku.html), [Crossword](crossword.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

Grid with row/column run-length clues; fill or leave blank cells so runs match clues exactly; unique solution; competitions (e.g. the World Puzzle Championship's nonogram events) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Nonograms were popularized by Japanese publisher Nikoli in the 1980s–90s; Nintendo's Picross (1994+) made them mainstream; the Android 10 nonogram Easter egg (PD) shows a solved grid (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
