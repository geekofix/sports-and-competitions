## Basic Information

Bughouse (also bughouse chess) is a four-player team variant played on two boards: partners sit opposite each other; captured pieces are passed to the partner, who may drop them on their own board (crazyhouse-style). It is played in parks (notably in the US) and online, with world championships. Within the chess variants and mind games on this site, its closest relatives are [Crazyhouse](crazyhouse.html), [Antichess](antichess.html), [Chess](chess.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

Two standard chess games played simultaneously by teams of two; a capture on one board lets the partner drop a piece of the same type on their board. A player may also spend their move just passing a piece without capturing. The team that checkmates first wins both boards (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Originated in the early 20th century and popularized in the US; famous park play at Washington Square Park and Bughouse Square in Chicago (the debating soapbox, photographed 2013–2015). Online play and the World Bughouse Championship are organized since the 1960s–70s (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
