## Basic Information

Set is the real-time card game invented by Marsha Falco in 1988 (popularized 1991, Mensa Select): 81 cards with 4 attributes (number, shape, shading, color); players race to find 'sets' of 3 cards where each attribute is all-same or all-different. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Dobble](dobble.html), [Halli Galli](halli-galli.html), [Uno](uno.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Set Enterprises, n.d.).

## Rules

81-card deck; 12 cards laid out, +3 if no set; a set = 3 cards with each attribute identical or all different across the three; most sets; most sets wins. World championship— Set world championships are held (Set Enterprises, n.d.).

## History

Designed by Marsha Falco (geneticist) in 1988, published by Set Enterprises 1991; the PD 'Set-game-cards' image (2005) shows an example set; the custom 'Projective Set' deck (2015) shows its math depth (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
