## Basic Information

Minixiangqi is the 7x7 compact variant of xiangqi (Chinese chess) for quick games: the same pieces on a smaller board with a smaller palace; it is popular in China and Vietnam as a casual park game. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Xiangqi](xiangqi.html), [Banqi](banqi.html), [Manchu Chess](manchu-chess.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

7x7 board (7x7, with the river removed in some versions); standard xiangqi pieces and rules scaled down; mate of the general wins (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Minixiangqi emerged in the 20th century as a faster casual form of xiangqi; it is sold as travel sets and played in parks (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
