## Basic Information

Steel Challenge is a pure speed-shooting sport: competitors race the clock through eight standardized stages of five steel plates each, and only time counts — the fastest total wins (Wikipedia, n.d.). It is governed by the Steel Challenge Shooting Association (SCSA), an organization of USPSA, and is part of the action-shooting family alongside multigun and IPSC (SCSA, n.d.).

The eight official stages — SC-101 "Five to Go" through SC-108 "Outer Limits" — are identical at every sanctioned match worldwide, which is what makes world records comparable; each stage is shot in five strings (Outer Limits in four), and the stage time is the sum of the best four strings (best three on Outer Limits) (Wikipedia, n.d.; SCSA, n.d.). The annual World Speed Shooting Championship (WSSC), held at the CMP Talladega Marksmanship Park in Alabama since 2018, crowns the world champion; the 2025 edition drew 247 competitors shooting 665 guns, with Maximus White setting a division world record of 84.17 seconds and Olivia Robertson a Lady world record of 131.10 (SSUSA, 2025).

## Rules

Competitors start with the firearm in a low-ready position, shoot the five plates in any order until the stop plate is hit, and the timer stops on the last shot; any missed plate adds a 5-second penalty, and a shooter who cannot finish a stage is scored as a DNF for that stage (SCSA, n.d.). Divisions (16 as of 2026) cover centerfire pistol, pistol-caliber carbine, rimfire pistol and rimfire rifle, each with Open, Limited, Production, Single Stack, Optics, Carry Optics and Lady variants; classification is based on percentages of "peak times" derived from world-championship stage results (SCSA, n.d.). Men and women compete in the same divisions with Lady categories recognized separately.

## History

The competition was founded in 1981 by Mike Dalton and Mike Fichman; John Shaw won the first "World’s Fastest Shooter" title that year with a share of a $20,000 purse, and 70 shooters took part (Wikipedia, n.d.). The format grew into one of America’s largest professional pistol matches — by 2007 more than 220 competitors shared a $390,000 purse — and Dalton and Fichman sold Steel Challenge to USPSA in the winter of 2007 (Wikipedia, n.d.). The world championship moved from Piru, California, to Frostproof, St. George, San Luis Obispo and finally Talladega, where it has been held every year since 2018 (Wikipedia, n.d.).
