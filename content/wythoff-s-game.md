## Basic Information

Wythoff's game is a two-pile Nim variant invented by the Dutch mathematician W. A. Wythoff in 1907: players remove any number from one pile or the same number from both; the player taking the last counter wins; losing positions are the Wythoff pairs (Beatty sequences of φ). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Nim](nim.html), [Chomp](chomp.html), [Sprouts](sprouts.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

Two piles; a move takes k>0 from one pile or k from both; last counter wins (normal play); P-positions are (⌊nφ⌋, ⌊nφ²⌋) pairs (Wythoff pairs) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Described by W. A. Wythoff in 1907 ('Ein Spiel mit zwei Haufen'); the TwoPile diagram (2025, CC0) shows a position; it is a classic combinatorial game theory example (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
