## Basic Information

Call of Duty is Activision's military first-person shooter franchise; its top esport is the Call of Duty League (CDL), a city-franchised league playing the current annual title (Black Ops 7 in 2026) in 4v4 objective modes (hardpoint, search & destroy, control) (Activision, n.d.). The 2026 CDL Championship was won by FaZe Vegas, who beat defending champions OpTic Texas 5–2 in the grand final on 19 July 2026 at Michelob ULTRA Arena in Las Vegas; Simp was finals MVP — OpTic had won the back-to-back titles in 2024 and 2025 (Wikipedia, n.d.; Checkmate Gaming, n.d.).

## Rules

CDL rules: 12 franchised teams, four Majors per season plus the Championship; matches are best-of-5 with a best-of-9 grand final; map-mode veto into the current CDL map pool (hardpoint, search & destroy, control); loadouts and general-availability rules per the CDL rulebook; roster of four plus substitutes; open to all genders — women compete in the same open league (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Competitive Call of Duty dates to MLG's 2010s circuits and the Call of Duty World League (2016–2019, with Evil Geniuses, OpTic and Complexity among the champions); the city-based CDL launched in 2020 with franchise slots sold for about $25 million, won that year by the Dallas Empire; OpTic Texas (formerly Dallas Empire) won 2024 and 2025 before FaZe Vegas ended their three-peat bid in 2026 (Wikipedia, n.d.).
