## Basic Information

eFootball (Konami, successor of Pro Evolution Soccer, launched 2021/2022 as eFootball 2022 and rebranded 2023) is Konami's football simulation; its esports are the eFootball World Cup (1v1 and pro-club team events) and the eFootball Open national cups, also featured at the Esports World Cup (Wikipedia, n.d.) (Konami, n.d.). Matches use 5-minute halves with standard teams; open to all genders with women's cups alongside.

## Rules

eFootball esports rules: 1v1 knockout brackets (best-of-3) at the World Cup plus team pro-club events; standard match settings, no custom teams in official events; disconnect rules with admin replay review; national eFootball Open cups qualify players for the World Cup (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Pro Evolution Soccer became eFootball with the 2022 free-to-play relaunch; Konami has run the eFootball Championship series since 2022 and the eFootball Open since 2023, with the game at the Esports World Cup since 2024 (Wikipedia, n.d.).
