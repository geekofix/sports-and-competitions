## Basic Information

Bed racing is a charity team sport in which crews push decorated beds on wheels over a road course that includes hills, obstacles and, at Knaresborough, a final swim across a river; the flagship event is the Great Knaresborough Bed Race in North Yorkshire, organised by the Knaresborough Lions Club and held every year since 1966 (except 2020 and 2021) on the second Saturday of June (Great Knaresborough Bed Race, n.d.). The 60th edition took place on Saturday, June 13, 2026, under the theme "The Swinging Sixties" with 90 teams: the Knaresborough Striders Men won in 12 minutes 33 seconds, ratified by Guinness World Records as a new modern-era record (12:50.1 against the 2014 mark of the Harrogate Harriers at 12:51), while GH Brooks — winners of seven consecutive races from 2018 to 2025 — finished second (Knaresborough Lions Club, n.d.). The fastest female team was the Squashed Hearts in seventh place in 15:33, and the Best Dressed Bed award went to bed number 44, "The Rocket Men". The 2.4-mile course runs through Conyngham, the Nidd Gorge, Castle Ings, the High Street, Bond End and Mackintosh Park, finishing with a swim across the River Nidd (Great Knaresborough Bed Race, n.d.). Other well-known bed races include the Great Bed Races at the Kentucky Derby Festival in Louisville, USA (Kentucky Derby Festival, n.d.). Teams are usually mixed, with separate awards for the fastest men's, women's and mixed beds.

## Rules

Each team pushes a bed — complete with mattress, frame and four wheels — carrying its riders over the published course, which must be completed in the fastest time; beds must stay on the road except where the course allows shortcuts, and crews typically consist of up to six pushers plus riders (Great Knaresborough Bed Race, n.d.). At Knaresborough the course includes the notorious Bland's Hill and ends with all riders swimming across the River Nidd to the finish. Awards are given for the fastest overall bed, the fastest men's, women's and mixed teams, the best dressed bed and the best fancy dress, and all proceeds go to charity — at Knaresborough the Lions Club has raised millions for local causes since 1966. Men's and women's teams compete under identical rules, and family and junior beds race in separate categories at many events (Kentucky Derby Festival, n.d.).

## History

The Great Knaresborough Bed Race was first held in 1966 and has run every year since except 2020 and 2021, always on the second Saturday of June, becoming one of Britain's best-loved charity spectacles; the 2026 race was the 60th edition (Knaresborough Lions Club, n.d.). The GH Brooks team established a dynasty by winning seven consecutive races from 2018 to 2025 before finishing second in 2026, when the Knaresborough Striders Men set the new modern-era record. The women's side has always been part of the event, with a dedicated fastest female team award — the Squashed Hearts of 2026 — and mixed family teams are a fixture of the parade. Similar bed races are held at festivals around the world, notably the Great Bed Races during the Kentucky Derby Festival in Louisville, Kentucky, where decorated beds race through the streets each spring (Kentucky Derby Festival, n.d.).
