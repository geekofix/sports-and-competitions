## Basic Information

Age of Empires is Microsoft/Ensemble Studios' historical real-time strategy series (villager economy, ages, civilization bonuses); its esport scene centers on Age of Empires II (Definitive Edition) and IV at S-tier events such as Red Bull Wololo — the 2026 edition 'Londinium' was held at London's Royal Albert Hall and Odeon Luxe Leicester Square with separate AoE II and AoE IV tournaments — and the ESOC Global Championship (Wikipedia, n.d.; Liquipedia, n.d.). Matches are best-of-5 (best-of-7 finals) on a map pool with civilization draft; men and women compete in the same open brackets.

## Rules

RTS rules: standard random-map settings on the current patch, fixed map pool with veto, no pausing (limited tactical pause in some leagues); civilizations drafted or mirrored by ruleset; in AoE II a player wins by eliminating all enemy buildings and units (typically starting with the Town Center); best-of series decided by map wins. Open to all genders (Liquipedia, n.d.).

## History

The series launched in 1997 (Age of Empires, designed by Bruce Shelley at Ensemble Studios); AoE II (1999) became the competitive esport in the 2000s via the Zone and Voobly, with early big LANs at WCG and ESWC; Red Bull Wololo has been the flagship since 2021, with players such as TheViper, Hera (multiple Wololo titles) and Liereyy dominating (Wikipedia, n.d.).
