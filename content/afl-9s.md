## Basic Information

AFL 9s is the nine-a-side version of Australian rules football, run by the Australian Football League (AFL) mainly as a schools and community program. It is played on a smaller field with modified rules so that more players get involved in every play.

## Rules

AFL 9s is played with nine players per side on a reduced-size oval, with shorter quarters than the full game. The AFL's modified rules keep the ball moving and give every player a touch — for example, restrictions on how far the ball can be carried and quicker restarts. Full AFL laws apply otherwise, including tackling, marking and goal scoring.

## History

The AFL launched AFL 9s in the 2010s to introduce Australian rules football to primary-school children and community groups; it is now played by thousands of school teams across Australia.
