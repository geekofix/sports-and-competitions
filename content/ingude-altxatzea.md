## Basic Information

Ingude Altxatzea is the Basque anvil-lifting contest (ingude altxatzea): competitors lift a heavy anvil (ingudea, typically 50–100 kg and more) from the ground to chest or shoulder height repeatedly within a time limit; the most lifts wins. The 1928 Ojanguren photograph of Biktor Zabala shows the classic event (Euskal Herri Kirolak Elkartea, n.d.).

## Rules

A lift counts when the anvil is held stably at shoulder height with one hand; chalk allowed. Time limits of 2–5 minutes; weight classes at modern championships; men's and women's categories. Safety: steel-toe boots, clear zone (Euskal Herri Kirolak Elkartea, n.d.).

## History

Anvils were the blacksmith's core tool in every Basque village; lifting contests at the forge are documented from the 19th century. The historic 1928 photograph shows Biktor Zabala in competition; today the event is part of the Herri Kirolak circuit with standardized anvils (Wikipedia, n.d.).
