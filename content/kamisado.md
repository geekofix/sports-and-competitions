## Basic Information

Kamisado is an 8x8 diagonal-movement game (invented in France, commercialized in the 2000s): each player has 8 colored towers on the back rank; on your turn you move a tower forward diagonally as far as you like until blocked; when two enemy towers meet on a diagonal, the higher— the tower whose color matches the squthe tower whose color matches the square it lands on wins the diagonal and may turn; landing on a square of the opponent's tower color activates it. First to reach the opponent's back rank wins. World Kamisado championship exists. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Quoridor](quoridor.html), [Chess](chess.html), [Quarto](quarto.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

8x8 board with colored rows; towers move only diagonally forward any distance; a tower landing on a square of its own color activates; 'kamisado' towers (hit head-on) get extra powers. First tower to the far rank wins (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Kamisado was designed in the 2000s (France) and has a world championship; the sumo tower piece is pictured in the standard set (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
