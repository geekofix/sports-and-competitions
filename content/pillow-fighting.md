## Basic Information

Pillow fighting, once considered only a children's game, has been developed into professional combat sport by the Pillow Fight Championship (PFC), founded by Paul and Steve Williams in 2021 in the United States. Fighters wear boxing-style gear and swing specialized combat pillows, combining elements of boxing and mixed martial arts, on the stated premise that "nobody gets hurt." The inaugural professional event, "Pound Down", was staged in Miami on 29 January 2022 and streamed on pay-per-view.

## Rules

PFC bouts are contested by gender-separated weight classes (under 225 lbs and 225 lbs and over) with adult and youth divisions. Fights consist of rounds of 90 seconds each with 60-second intervals, scored by three judges on a 10-point system like boxing. Fighters must use an official PFC combat pillow, which is inspected before each match. Contestants typically wear headgear and mouthguards, with gloves prohibited because the pillow is the striking instrument. Legal techniques include swings to the head and body with the pillow. Fouls include grabbing the opponent's pillow, wrestling, holding, striking with any body part, or leaving the competition area during the bout. A tied score goes to an extension round; a bout which is still tied is scored a draw, with the champion retaining the title in championship matches. Judges reward clean pillow strikes to the head and body, and a fighter may also lose by knockout, technical stoppage or referee stoppage, as in boxing.

## History

Organized pillow fighting predates PFC in several forms: a World Pillow Fighting Championship has been staged for decades in northern California, a team championship for schoolchildren has been held annually since 2013 in Ito, Japan, and since 2008 the Newmindspace group has organized international pillow-fight flash-mob days in cities around the world. Professional pillow fighting was developed from about 2018, with rule development and equipment design taking several years: the current 2 lb combat pillow, with handle and sailcloth case, was chosen after almost a hundred prototypes, since the original 3.5 lb feather pillows proved too heavy for three two-minute rounds. PFC streamed its first tournament on 29 January 2022, with 16 men and 8 women competing for $5,000 first prizes in a Florida arena; Hauley Tillman won the men's title and Istela Nunes, an MMA fighter, the women's. PFC events have since aired on ESPN's The Ocho, DAZN and Brazilian national television, and a sanctioned championship was held in Ghana in 2023.
