## Basic Information

Connect6 is a connection game invented in 2008 by Professor I-Chen Wu at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, Taiwan: black places one stone on the first move and two stones on every later move; the first to connect six wins on a 19x19 board. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Gomoku](gomoku.html), [Pente](pente.html), [Go](go.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

19x19 go board; first move one stone (black), then two stones per turn alternating; six or more in a row wins. World championships are held (World Connect6 Championship) (Wu, I.-C, n.d.).

## History

Designed in 2008 in Taiwan to balance the first-move advantage of gomoku/renju by giving the second player two stones per move; the Connect6 world championship is organized since 2009 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
