## Basic Information

Subbuteo ('flick football') is the English table game of the 1920s–50s: players flick disc-shaped counters (players) across a cloth pitch on a hinged board, trying to score goals; it had clubs and championships in the mid-20th century. Within the table and arcade games on this site, its closest relatives are [Table Football](table-football.html), [Table Football](table-football.html), [Air Hockey](air-hockey.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

Cloth pitch on a board (~90x60 cm); 11 disc players per side (one is the goalkeeper); flick discs with a finger (via a cue); goals by getting the disc into the opponent's goal; matches timed (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Invented in England in the 1920s (London), boomed in the 1940s–50s with the Subbuteo Association; the 2008 PD photo shows players; equipment photo (2010) shows the discs (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
