## Basic Information

League of Legends is a 5-versus-5 multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) developed and published by Riot Games, founded in 2006 by Brandon Beck and Marc Merrill (Wikipedia, n.d.-a; Riot Games, n.d.). Two teams of five players each control a unique champion on the map Summoner's Rift, gaining experience and gold to buy items while pushing lanes, defeating neutral monsters and destroying the enemy's turrets, dragons and ultimately the Nexus at the heart of the enemy base to win (Riot Games, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The game supports a ranked ladder from Iron to Challenger and one of the largest professional esports ecosystems in the world: regional leagues such as the LEC (Europe), LCK (Korea), LPL (China) and LCS (Americas) feed the Mid-Season Invitational and the annual World Championship, first held in 2011 at Jönköping, Sweden (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The 2024 World Championship at the O2 Arena in London was won by the Korean organization T1, who defeated Bilibili Gaming 3–2 in the final to give Lee "Faker" Sang-hyeok a record fifth world title (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). Women's competitive play is organized through dedicated circuits: the League of Legends Game Changers: Rising (LGC Rising) returned for a second edition in 2026 as the official EMEA competition for players who identify as women, run with licensing partner N.E.O from May to October with playoffs on 27 October 2026, and Eterna won the inaugural 2025 edition by defeating G2 Hel 3–1, while G2 Hel had earlier become the first all-women team to earn promotion in a tier-two European regional league (Liga Nexo) (Wikipedia, n.d.-c; Insider Gaming, n.d.).

## Rules

A standard match is played 5 versus 5 on Summoner's Rift, a symmetrical map with three lanes, a jungle and two bases (Riot Games, n.d.). After a draft phase in which each team bans and picks champions, players buy items with gold earned from minions, monsters and objectives, level up to 18 and try to destroy the eleven turrets defending the enemy Nexus; the first team to destroy the Nexus wins (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Neutral objectives — the dragon pit, Rift Herald, Baron Nashor and the Elder Dragon — grant team-wide bonuses, and the ancient ruins give a powerful buff, so map control around these objectives decides most professional games (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). In ranked play, players are placed in tiers from Iron to Challenger and earn or lose league points per win or defeat (Riot Games, n.d.). Professional matches are played on a fixed patch with a best-of series format — best-of-one in early rounds, best-of-three and best-of-five later — with side selection decided by coin flip and no champion repeats within a series are enforced only where the tournament format states them (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The women's Game Changers: Rising circuit uses the same game rules and standard best-of formats as the open leagues, with its own EMEA stage calendar and playoffs (Wikipedia, n.d.-c). Gender does not change the game rules anywhere in League of Legends; the women's competitions exist as dedicated pathway circuits alongside the open leagues (Insider Gaming, n.d.).

## History

Riot Games was founded in Los Angeles in 2006 by Brandon Beck and Marc Merrill with the goal of building a player-focused games company, and League of Legends launched in October 2009 after a beta (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The first World Championship was held in June 2011 at DreamHack Jönköping, won by Fnatic, and the event has grown into the most-watched esports final in the world, with the 2024 London final peaking at over 6.9 million concurrent viewers (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). T1 — originally SK Telecom T1 — has been the defining dynasty, and its mid-laner Lee "Faker" Sang-hyeok is widely regarded as the greatest esports player of all time after his fifth world title in 2024 (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). On the women's side, Riot launched dedicated inclusive competitions with the Game Changers program, and the EMEA League of Legends Game Changers: Rising debuted in 2025 before its expanded second edition in 2026, with Eterna as the first champion and G2 Hel as the first all-women team to reach a tier-two regional league final tier (Wikipedia, n.d.-c).
