## Basic Information

League of Legends (LoL) is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) video game developed and published by Riot Games, released in October 2009. Two teams of five champions battle on a map called Summoner's Rift with three lanes, a jungle and two bases; the objective is to destroy the enemy Nexus. It is the world's most-played PC game and the flagship esport of professional gaming, with the annual World Championship (Worlds) awarding the Summoner's Cup.

## Rules

In a standard match, each of the ten players picks a unique champion with abilities and roles (top, jungle, mid, bot carry, support); players farm minions for gold, buy items, and push lanes toward the enemy base, destroying turrets and ultimately the Nexus. A match is won when a team destroys the opponents' Nexus; there is no single-elimination in the game itself — professional series use best-of-three or best-of-five formats. In the World Championship, teams from the regional leagues (LCK in Korea, LPL in China, LEC in Europe, LCS in the Americas and others) qualify through their domestic splits and play a group stage followed by a knockout bracket to decide the world champion.

## History

Riot Games was founded in 2006 in Los Angeles by Brandon Beck and Marc Merrill, and League of Legends launched in October 2009, inspired by the Defense of the Ancients (DotA) mod. The first World Championship (Season 1) was held in June 2011 in Jönköping, Sweden, and was won by the European team Fnatic, who lifted the Summoner's Cup. The Korean team SK Telecom T1's Lee "Faker" Sang-hyeok became the most successful player in esports history with five world titles (2013, 2015, 2016, 2023, 2024). The Mid-Season Invitational has been held since 2015, and League of Legends was a demonstration/medal-adjacent event at the 2018 Asian Games and a medal event at the 2022 Hangzhou Asian Games (mobile version). Esports as a whole were showcased at the Olympic Esports Week in Singapore in 2023.
