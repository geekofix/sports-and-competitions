## Basic Information

Guilty Gear is Arc System Works' anime-style fighting game series; Guilty Gear Strive (2021, with the Dual Rulers 2025 expansion) is the current competitive title and a permanent EVO main-stage game with its own Guilty Gear Strive World Tour (Wikipedia, n.d.; Liquipedia, n.d.) (Arc System Works, n.d.). Matches: best-of-3, 99-second rounds, tension/roman-cancel mechanics; best-of-5 grand finals; open to all genders.

## Rules

Competitive rules: best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 finals), 99-second rounds, burst and Roman cancels per the current patch; character — stage veto; double-elimination brackets at World Tour events; open brackets, all genders (Liquipedia, n.d.).

## History

Daisuke Ishiwatari created Guilty Gear in 1998; the series became known for its rock soundtrack and technical depth, with Xrd and Strive at EVO from the 2010s onward; icons include Daigo Umehara's Guilty Gear play and Strive's world tour champions (Wikipedia, n.d.).
