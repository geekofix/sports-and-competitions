## Basic Information

Donkey Kong is Nintendo's 1981 arcade platformer — Mario's first appearance as 'Jumpman', climbing ladders and dodging barrels; its esport is the high-score world championship on original arcade hardware, run from 2007 with famous finals featuring Steve Wiebe and Billy Mitchell (Wikipedia, n.d.; Twin Galaxies, n.d.). Tournaments use original ROMs with standard dipswitch settings, no pause; open to all genders.

## Rules

High-score rules: original Donkey Kong arcade PCB/ROM with standard difficulty dipswitches, no pausing, no save states; the highest verified score wins; scores verified by recording and referees (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Shigeru Miyamoto designed Donkey Kong in 1981 (named after the stubborn ape); the game defined the platformer genre and launched the Mario franchise; the competitive high-score scene peaked with the 2009 world championship final between Steve Wiebe and Billy Mitchell, documented in the film The King of Kong (2007) (Wikipedia, n.d.).
