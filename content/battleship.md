## Basic Information

Battleship (the paper-and-pencil guessing game, 'Battleships') is played by two players on hidden grids: each secretly places ships (lengths 5,4,3,3,2 in the classic set) and calls shots ('B4'); hits/misses are marked; the first to sink all enemy ships wins. The board game was commercialized in 1931. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Mastermind](mastermind.html), [Tic Tac Toe](tic-tac-toe.html), [Stratego](stratego.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

10x10 grid (classic); ships: 1x5, 1x4, 2x3, 1x2; no adjacent ships (in some rule sets); call shots; 'hit'/'miss'/'sunk'; first to sink all wins. World championships (paper battleship) are held (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Paper battleship predates the 1931 Milton Bradley board game (a similar paper game appeared in the 1890s); the early-20th-century board (shakko, 2011 photo) and the 2016 Navy photo show play (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
