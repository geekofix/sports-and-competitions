## Basic Information

Taekwondo is the Korean martial art and Olympic combat sport built around kicking: fast, jumping and spinning kicks above the waist, with punches, blocks and forms practice completing the art. One of the world's most widely practiced martial arts with millions of students, it has two major international branches "+E+" the International Taekwon-Do Federation (ITF) and the World Taekwondo (WT) Olympic system "+E+" and has been an Olympic sport since Sydney 2000.

## Rules

Training spans fundamentals (kicks and strikes executed with the ball and instep of the foot), patterns or forms (hyeong, tul or poomsae, choreographed sequences of blocks and attacks), breaking (gyeokpa, the art of splitting boards), self-defence and sparring. WT-style kyorugi sparring "+E+" the Olympic format "+E+" is contested on a mat over rounds, with points scored by electronic body protectors and judges for kicks, with head kicks, spinning techniques and higher kicks earning more, and penalties enforcing safe, legal contact in weight categories for men and women. ITF sparring uses semi-contact rules that permit hand techniques to the head with protective gear; both branches use belt ranks (geup then dan degrees) certified through examination and standardized curricula by their governing bodies.

## History

Taekwondo was formed in Korea in the late 1940s and 1950s as masters of the original kwans (martial arts schools teaching karate-derived and indigenous Korean fighting arts) sought a unified national art; in 1955 the name taekwondo was adopted for the unified style, drawing on the traditional term taekkyeon for nationalist resonance, and the Korea Taekwondo Association followed in 1959. General Choi Hong-hi founded the International Taekwon-Do Federation in 1966, while the Kukkiwon (the world headquarters, 1972) and World Taekwondo (founded 1973 as the World Taekwondo Federation, renamed in 2017) built the sport-oriented Olympic branch. Taekwondo appeared as an Olympic demonstration sport in 1988 and 1992 and became a full medal sport at Sydney 2000; today WT governs Olympic and Paralympic taekwondo worldwide, ITF schools flourish internationally, and Korea officially designated taekwondo its national martial art in 2018.
