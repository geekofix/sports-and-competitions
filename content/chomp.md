## Basic Information

Chomp is the mathematical 'poisoned chocolate' game: players take turns choosing a square of a rectangular chocolate bar and 'eating' it plus everything below and to the right; the player forced to eat the poisoned top-left square loses. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Nim](nim.html), [Wythoff's Game](wythoff-s-game.html), [Sprouts](sprouts.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Gale, D, 1974).

## Rules

m x n grid; a move removes the chosen cell and all cells below/right; the (1,1) cell is poisoned; normal play (loser = eats poison); the first-player win is proven by strategy-stealing (no explicit strategy known for all sizes) (Gale, D, 1974).

## History

Invented by David Gale in the 1970s ; the chomp position diagram shows a move; the Australian chomp candy bar photo (Evan-Amos, CC0) shows the chocolate metaphor (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
