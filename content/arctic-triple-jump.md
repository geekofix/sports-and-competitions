## Basic Information

The Arctic triple jump is a three-bound jump event: the athlete performs a hop, a step and a jump with alternating take-off feet (like the athletics triple jump) from a standing or short run-up on snow, measured to the final heel mark (AWG, n.d.). Contested at the Arctic Winter Games in men's and women's categories. Within the Arctic and Indigenous sports on this site, its closest relatives are [Blanket Toss](blanket-toss.html), [Snow Snake](snow-snake.html), [Two-Foot High Kick](two-foot-high-kick.html), [Finger Pull](finger-pull.html), [Hand Games](hand-games.html), [World Eskimo Indian Olympics](world-eskimo-indian-olympics.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

Three consecutive bounds — hop on the take-off foot, step onto the other foot, jump into the pit — measured from the board to the nearest heel mark of the final landing. Three attempts, best counts (AWG, n.d.).

## History

Derived from Inuit jumping games and the athletics triple jump, the Arctic triple jump has been an AWG event since the 1970s (Wikipedia, n.d.).
