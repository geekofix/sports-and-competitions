## Basic Information

Abalone is a marble-pushing game invented in 1987 by Michel Lalet and Laurent Levi (France): two to four players maneuver 14 marbles each on a hexagonal board, pushing single enemy marbles or lines (up to 3) off the edge; the first to push six opponent marbles off wins. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [ZERTZ](zertz.html), [GIPF](gipf.html), [Go](go.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Abalone Games, n.d.).

## Rules

Hexagonal board with 61 spaces; a group of 1–3 touching marbles can move together if the line is shorter than the pushed group; a pushed marble that leaves the board is captured. Six captures win. World championships (World Abalone Championship) since the 1990s (Abalone Games, n.d.).

## History

Invented in 1987 by Lalet and Levi (originally 'Sumito'); published by Abalone Games; became a Mensa Select game and has world championships (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
