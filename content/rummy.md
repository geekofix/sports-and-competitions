## Basic Information

Rummy is the family of melding games: players draw and discard, aiming to form melds — sets (3–4 of a rank) or runs (3+ consecutive of a suit) — and 'go out' by melding all cards; the opponents score penalty points for unmelded cards. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Gin Rummy](gin-rummy.html), [Canasta](canasta.html), [Cribbage](cribbage.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## Rules

Standard 52-card deck + jokers; draw from stock or discard; meld sets/runs of 3+; may lay off on others' melds; going out ends the hand; scoring by card values (face cards count 10). Many variants: gin rummy, canasta, etc (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## History

Rummy descends from the Mexican game Conquian (19th century) and spread worldwide in the early 20th century; the 2010 Rummy 500 photo (David Lazich) and the 1947 Navy gin-rummy photo show its social spread (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
