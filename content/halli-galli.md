## Basic Information

Halli Galli is the speed card game by Haim Shafir: 56 cards with fruit symbols; when exactly five of one fruit are visible, the first to ring the bell takes the cards. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Dobble](dobble.html), [Set](set.html), [Uno](uno.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Amigo Spiele, n.d.).

## Rules

Deck of 56 cards (fruits: strawberry, banana, lemon, plum); cards turned in a pile; when the top cards show exactly five of one fruit, ring the bell first to win the pile; most cards at the end wins (Amigo Spiele, n.d.).

## History

Invented by Haim Shafir and published by Amigo Spiele in 1983; one of the best-selling children's card games in Germany (the Halli Galli card photo, 2010, and the 2011 game photo document it) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
