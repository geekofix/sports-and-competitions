## Basic Information

TZAAR (2005) is the third GIPF-project game by Kris Burm: on a triangular board players stack their pieces to make them taller; you must either move a stack onto another (capturing it, making yours taller) or pass — but you may not pass; the first unable to move loses. TZAAR won the— it is the 2006— it won the Golden Geek— and has a world championship. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [GIPF](gipf.html), [DVONN](dvonn.html), [ZERTZ](zertz.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (GIPF project, n.d.).

## Rules

Triangular board (triangular board, side 6, 21 spaces); each turn: either stack one of your pieces onto another (yours or opponent's) making a taller tower, or pass is forbidden — you must move; a player who cannot move loses. World TZAAR Championship (played at Essen Spiel 2007, photographed) (GIPF project, n.d.).

## History

Designed by Kris Burm in 2005; won the 2006 International Gamers Award; it is a GIPF-project game with a world championship; the designer Kris Burm was photographed at Essen Spiel'07 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
