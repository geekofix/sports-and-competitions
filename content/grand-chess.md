## Basic Information

Grand chess is the 10x10 variant (with chancellor and archbishop) that Capablanca preferred from 1929 and that Christian Freeling refined in 2002; the 10x10 board is used in modern grand chess with world championships. Within the chess variants and mind games on this site, its closest relatives are [Capablanca Chess](capablanca-chess.html), [Chess](chess.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

10x10 board; pieces as in Capablanca chess (chancellor, archbishop); pawns promote on the 10th rank; castling along the back rank with corner rooks (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Capablanca introduced Grand Chess on a 10x10 board in 1929; Christian Freeling's 2002 rules are the modern standard, played at international grand chess tournaments (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
