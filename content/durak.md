## Basic Information

Durak ('fool') is the most popular Russian card game: an attacker plays cards of the same rank as cards on the table; the defender must beat each with a higher card of the same suit or a trump; undefended cards are taken up; the player left holding cards at the end is the fool (durak). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Preferans](preferans.html), [Belote](belote.html), [Euchre](euchre.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## Rules

36-card deck (6–A) in the classic form; trump suit from the bottom card; defender beats by higher rank of same suit or any trump; attacker may add cards up to the number of cards the defender holds (up to six initially, more in perevodnoy durak); first to empty hand wins, last with cards is the fool (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## History

Durak developed in Russia in the 18th–19th centuries; it spread across the former USSR and is now played worldwide online; the 2021 photo shows a three-player game with a 1974 Atlasnye deck (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
