## Basic Information

Sand hockey is field hockey played on sand by nomadic communities of southern Morocco, showcased at the annual Nomads Festival (Moussem des Nomades) near Erg Chebbi/M'Hamid; players use curved sticks and a ball on a marked sand pitch, with men's, women's and children's matches (Wikipedia, n.d.). It is one of the traditional games of the Moroccan desert festivals alongside camel racing and fantazia. Within the hockey family on this site, its closest relatives are [Genna](genna.html), [Field Hockey](field-hockey.html), [Indoor Field Hockey](indoor-field-hockey.html), [Hockey5s](hockey5s.html), [Floorball](floorball.html), [Bandy](bandy.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

Played on a rectangular sand pitch with goals at each end, using field-hockey-style curved sticks and a ball; teams of 6–11 per side depending on the informal local format. Matches are short, festival-format games; men's, women's and junior categories are organised at the Moussem (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Desert hockey at the Nomads Festival documents how field-hockey-like stick games spread along trans-Saharan routes; the Moussem des Nomades near M'Hamid el Ghizlane gathers nomadic tribes each spring for games, music and trade (Wikipedia, n.d.).
