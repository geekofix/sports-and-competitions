## Basic Information

Cycling is the sport of racing human-powered bicycles and is governed worldwide by the Union Cycliste Internationale (UCI), founded on 14 April 1900 in Paris by five national federations (Union Cycliste Internationale [UCI], n.d.-a). The UCI sanctions the major disciplines — road, track, mountain bike, BMX, cyclo-cross and indoor cycling — the UCI WorldTour and the annual UCI World Championships (UCI, n.d.-a). The 2026 men's road season was defined by Tadej Pogačar's fifth Tour de France title: the 113th edition started in Barcelona on 4 July and finished on the Champs-Élysées on 26 July 2026, with Pogačar winning by 6'26" over Remco Evenepoel, Isaac del Toro third and taking the best young rider's white jersey, Mads Pedersen the points classification and Richard Carapaz the mountains classification, at an average speed of 43.328 km/h (UCI, n.d.-b). Pogačar also led the 2026 UCI WorldTour standings for the sixth consecutive year, while Jonas Vingegaard won the 2026 Giro d'Italia — becoming only the eighth rider to win all three Grand Tours (UCI, n.d.-b). On the women's side, Demi Vollering completed the set of all three women's Grand Tours by winning the 2026 Giro Donne and then the 2026 UCI Road World Championship road race in Montreal, where Marlen Reusser won the women's time trial; Kim Le Court won the 2026 Tour of Britain Women (UCI, n.d.-b). ## Rules

The UCI Constitution and Cycling Regulations are the official rulebook for all disciplines (UCI, n.d.-a). Road racing comprises one-day classics and stage races with team time trials, individual time trials and bunch sprints; the bike must weigh at least 6.8 kg and meet UCI dimensional limits (UCI, n.d.-a). Track cycling is raced in the velodrome in sprint, endurance and omnium formats; mountain biking covers cross-country Olympic (XCO), short track (XCC) and marathon; BMX includes racing and freestyle; cyclo-cross is covered on [Cyclo-cross](cyclo-cross.html) on this site (UCI, n.d.-a). Races are officiated by UCI commissaires; infringements such as drafting, holding or dangerous riding are penalized with relegation, time penalties or disqualification (UCI, n.d.-a). The UCI publishes official world rankings and the WorldTour standings (UCI, n.d.-b). Men's and women's events run under the same regulations, with women's professional racing organized on the UCI Women's WorldTour; age classifications distinguish junior (17–18), under-23 and elite riders, and para-cycling fields C, T, B and H classes under the UCI (UCI, n.d.-a).

## History

The UCI was founded in Paris on 14 April 1900 and the first UCI Road World Championships followed in 1927 at the Nürburgring (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The three Grand Tours anchor the road calendar: the Tour de France began in 1903 and was won by Maurice Garin, the Giro d'Italia began in 1909 and the Vuelta a España in 1935 (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The most celebrated rider of all time is Belgium's Eddy Merckx, pictured below — winner of a record 525 professional races, five Tours de France, three Giros and one Vuelta, nicknamed "Le Cannibale" (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Other icons include Jacques Anquetil, Bernard Hinault, Miguel Induráin and, on the women's side, the French champion Jeannie Longo and Britain's Beryl Burton (Wikipedia, n.d.-b).
