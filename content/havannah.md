## Basic Information

Havannah is a hexagonal connection game invented in 1981 by Christian Freeling: played on a hexagon of 54 cells (side 10), a player wins by forming a ring, a bridge (connecting two opposite sides) or a fork (three corners). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Hex](hex.html), [Go](go.html), [Amazons](amazons.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## Rules

Hex board shaped as a hexagon with 10 cells per side; win by ring around a cell/group, bridge connecting two opposite sides, or fork touching three corners. World championships exist (Havannah world championship) (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Invented by Christian Freeling (Netherlands) in 1981; it belongs to the connection-game family with Hex and Y. The Havannah world championship is held regularly (Wikipedia, n.d.).
