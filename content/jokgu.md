## Basic Information

Jokgu (족구, Korean-style footvolley) is a Korean sport that fuses association football and volleyball: two teams of four play over a low net on a small court, and the ball may be played only with the feet (below the knee) and the head (above the chin). It is hugely popular in South Korea, with more than a thousand teams in professional, school and military leagues, and the Korea Jokgu Association is working toward an International Jokgu Federation, having staged the first World Jokgu Championship in 2023.

## Rules

The court measures 16 m x 7 m with a net about 1.1 m high, similar to tennis. Each team fields four starting players plus relievers, and each side may contact the ball at most three times, with at most one bounce of the ball between contacts. Only the feet below the knee and the head above the chin may touch the ball; hands and arms are forbidden. A match consists of three sets of 15 points under rally scoring, and a set requires a two-point advantage. The official ball is about 20 cm in diameter. Serves are made from behind the end line, and the ball may be played off the net on a serve.

## History

Jokgu was invented in 1960 by members of the Republic of Korea Air Force as a way to keep fit on military bases, and it quickly became one of the most common pastimes in the Korean military and schools. It was among the sports premiered at the first Annual Leisure Games in Chuncheon in 2010. As the game spread abroad, teams in the United States formed a jokgu league in 2011, and in 2023 South Korea hosted the first World Jokgu Championship, a step toward the planned International Jokgu Federation. Skilled players combine football-style volleys with acrobatic overhead and bicycle kicks.
