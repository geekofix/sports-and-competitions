## Basic Information

Truco is the South American bluffing trick-taking game (Argentina, Brazil, Uruguay, Chile) played with a 40-card Spanish deck (or 52 without 8s/9s): players bet 'truco' (and 'retruco', 'vale quatro') on winning two of three tricks; the highest single card is the ace of swords (espadas). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Scopa](scopa.html), [Briscola](briscola.html), [Belote](belote.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## Rules

40-card Spanish deck; ranks: 1 of swords highest, then 7 of swords, 3, 2, 1 of other suits; order: 1♠ > 7♠ > 3 > 2 > 1(other) > 12— standard: 1espadas, 7espadas, 7oros, 3, 2, 1, 12, 11, 10, 7, 6, 5, 4. Bidding envido/truco; bluffing central (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## History

Truco originated in the Río de la Plata region in the 19th century (from Spanish trick-taking games); it is the national game of Argentina (the 1811 Spanish deck from Madrid is preserved as the ancestor deck) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
