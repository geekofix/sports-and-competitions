## Basic Information

Arena Football is the indoor variant of American football, played on a small padded field (about 200 by 85 feet) surrounded by padded walls that keep the ball in play. The Arena Football League (AFL), founded by Jim Foster in 1987, made it a major indoor sport until 2019; today the Indoor Football League (IFL) continues professional indoor football in the US.

## Rules

Arena football is played 8v8 on a 50-yard padded field with rebound boards instead of out of bounds. Four 15-minute quarters; the offense has four downs as in the NFL; scoring is the same (touchdown 6, field goal 3, safety 2). Because the walls are in play, the ball rarely goes out, producing continuous, high-scoring action. Kicking is limited: the kickoff is from the 20-yard line and field goals are worth 3 points; variants exist between the AFL and IFL rulebooks, such as drop-kick kickoffs and goal-post width.

## History

Jim Foster created arena football in 1987 to make football playable indoors year-round; the AFL grew to over 20 teams and was broadcast on ESPN in the 1990s and 2000s before folding in 2019. The IFL, founded in 2009, now runs the top indoor league.
