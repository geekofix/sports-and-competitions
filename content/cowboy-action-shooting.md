## Basic Information

Cowboy action shooting (CAS) is a timed, accuracy-based multigun sport dressed in the Old West: competitors wear period costume and use pre-1899 firearms — two single-action revolvers, a lever-action rifle chambered in a pistol calibre, and a side-by-side double-barrel shotgun — to engage steel targets on themed stages (Wikipedia, n.d.). It belongs to the action-shooting family together with 3-gun, Steel Challenge and IPSC practical shooting.

The main sanctioning body worldwide is the Single Action Shooting Society (SASS), an international membership organization formed in 1987 that sanctions State, Regional, National and World Championship matches; other bodies include the Western Action Shootists Association (WASA), the National Congress of Old West Shooters (NCOWS) and Single Action Shooting Australia (SASA) (Wikipedia, n.d.; SASS, 2026). The annual world championship is "End of Trail", held at the Founders Ranch in Eunice, Louisiana (SASS, n.d.).

## Rules

A match consists of several stages of 10 targets each; the shooter’s raw time is stopped by the last shot and penalties are added — 5 seconds per miss, 10 seconds for procedural errors and additional penalties for hitting "no-shoot" targets; the lowest total time in each category wins (SASS, 2026). All handguns must be single-action (the hammer cocked by hand before each shot); rifles must be lever-action in a centerfire revolver calibre of a type in use before 1899; shotguns are double-barrel coach guns, with external-hammer Winchester 1897 pumps allowed (Wikipedia, n.d.). Categories include age-based groups (Junior, Senior, Elder, etc.) and ladies’ categories such as LOCAS (Ladies of Cowboy Action Shooting), and minimum entry mandates apply at championship level (SASS, 2026).

## History

Cowboy action shooting originated in 1981 at the Coto de Caza Shooting Range in Orange County, California, when a group of shooters led by Larry Harris — the "Wild Bunch" — created the format; SASS was formed in 1987 to preserve and promote the sport and today counts tens of thousands of members worldwide (Wikipedia, n.d.; SASS, 2026). The sport spread quickly through affiliated clubs in more than ten countries (3-Gun Nation affiliates also run CAS), and the TV-era popularity of Westerns made the costumes a fixture of the game (Wikipedia, n.d.).
