## Basic Information

Dots and boxes (timbiriche in Latin America) is the paper game: players alternately draw a line between adjacent dots on a grid; completing the fourth side of a 1x1 box scores it and grants another turn. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Tic Tac Toe](tic-tac-toe.html), [Sprouts](sprouts.html), [Chomp](chomp.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Lucas, É, n.d.).

## Rules

Grid of dots (5x5 typical); one line per turn; a completed box scores a point and an extra turn; most boxes at the end wins; the 'double-cross' strategy decides close games (Lucas, É, n.d.).

## History

Published as a game by Édouard Lucas in the 1880s (France); known worldwide; the timbiriche photo (2022, Brazil) shows a game between an adult and a child (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
