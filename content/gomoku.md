## Basic Information

Gomoku ('five in a row') is played on a 15x15 go board (intersections): players alternate placing black/white stones; the first to make an unbroken line of exactly five (freestyle: five or more) wins. The Renju International Federation (RIF) organizes world championships since 1989. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Go](go.html), [Renju](renju.html), [Omok](omok.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Renju International Federation, n.d.).

## Rules

15x15 board, black moves first; overline (six or more) wins in freestyle gomoku, counts as a foul for black in renju; no handicap in official play. World championships (individual and team) are held by the RIF (Renju International Federation, n.d.).

## History

Gomoku is documented in China (wuziqi) from the 17th century and in Japan from the Edo period; the RIF was founded in 1989 (Tournament: World Gomoku Championship). RenjuNote records professional openings (2025) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
