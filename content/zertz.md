## Basic Information

ZERTZ (1998) is a marble game by Kris Burm on a board that shrinks as marbles are removed: players place rings on a hexagonal board and remove their own marbles; a player unable to place a ring loses; the goal is to force the opponent into a losing position (zugzwang). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [GIPF](gipf.html), [Abalone](abalone.html), [DVONN](dvonn.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (GIPF project, n.d.).

## Rules

Hexagonal board starting with 37 rings and 5/6/7 marbles per color (white/grey/black); each turn place a ring on an empty space and optionally remove one of your marbles by jumping over others; a player who cannot place a ring loses. World championship exists (GIPF project, n.d.).

## History

Designed by Kris Burm in 1998 as part of the GIPF project; it is known for its elegant shrinking-board zugzwang mechanics (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
