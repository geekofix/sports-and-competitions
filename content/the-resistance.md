## Basic Information

The Resistance (2009, Don Eskridge) is the original social-deduction game: 5–10 resistance members with 3 hidden spies; mission teams vote; spies can sabotage; three successful missions win for the resistance. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Avalon](avalon.html), [Mafia (Werewolf)](mafia-werewolf.html), [Codenames](codenames.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

5–10 players; 3 spies (more with more players); five missions of 2–3 agents; success/fail cards played secretly; majority decides; if 3 missions fail, spies win; the resistance wins after 3 successes (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Designed by Don Eskridge and published in 2009; it created the modern social-deduction genre and has a world championship; Avalon (2012) is its Arthurian successor (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
