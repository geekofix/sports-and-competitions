## Basic Information

The Yu-Gi-Oh! video games (Konami's Master Duel, 2022, and older simulators) carry the digital Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG esport: the Yu-Gi-Oh! World Championship on Master Duel with ladder and cup qualifiers into the world final, played best-of-3 matches with a side deck; open to all genders (Wikipedia, n.d.) (Konami, n.d.). The physical TCG World Championship has run since 2003 alongside.

## Rules

Master Duel competitive rules: best-of-3 matches (best-of-5 in finals), 40-card decks with a side deck, sideboarding between games; ranked ladder plus cup qualifiers; World Championship with regional qualifiers; all genders in open play (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Kazuki Takahashi created Yu-Gi-Oh! (manga 1996, TCG 1999); Konami's handheld World Championship games ran in the 2000s, and Master Duel (2022) became the main digital esport platform with the first digital world championship in 2022 (Wikipedia, n.d.).
