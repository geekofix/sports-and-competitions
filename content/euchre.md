## Basic Information

Euchre is a four-player partnership trick-taking game played with a 24-card deck (9–A) or 28/32-card variants: one suit is trump (called by the dealer's partner after picking up the 'upcard'); the 'right bower' (J of trump) and 'left bower' (J of same color) are the top trumps. It is the classic Midwestern American card game. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Whist](whist.html), [Pinochle](pinochle.html), [Sheepshead](sheepshead.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## Rules

24-card deck (9,A,K,Q,J,10 of each suit); 5 cards each; bidding to 'order up' the trump or pass; the maker must take 3 of 5 tricks (5 if alone); going alone is allowed. Scoring: 1 point for 3–4 tricks, 2 for all 5 (the opponents score 2 if the maker is euchred) (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## History

Euchre descends from the German game jücker/euchre it arrived in North America with German immigrants in the 18th–19th centuries and became the dominant American partnership game; the scorekeeping counters (2s/3s) are photographed (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
