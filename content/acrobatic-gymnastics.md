## Basic Information

Acrobatic Gymnastics is a gymnastics discipline in which pairs or groups perform acrobatic balances, pyramids, throws and catches to music. The Fédération Internationale de Gymnastique (FIG) recognizes five partnership categories: women's pair, men's pair, mixed pair, women's group (three) and men's group (four). It is contested at FIG World Championships but is not an Olympic event.

## Rules

Under the FIG Code of Points, partnerships perform routines — men's groups perform three routines (balance, dynamic and combined), the other categories perform one or two — within a time limit. Panels of judges score difficulty, execution and artistic impression, with deductions for falls, uncontrolled catches and time violations. The highest total wins. Equipment categories define what is allowed, and catches must be fully controlled to score.

## History

Acrobatic gymnastics grew out of circus acrobatics and the acrobatic schools of Eastern Europe and the Soviet Union in the mid-20th century. The FIG has governed the discipline since 1999, and World Championships have been held since the 1970s under earlier international bodies.
