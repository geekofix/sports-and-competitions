## Basic Information

Soulcalibur (Soul Blade, 1996) is Bandai Namco's weapon-based 3D fighting game; Soulcalibur VI (2018) is the latest main title and a regular EVO slot (Wikipedia, n.d.) (Bandai Namco, n.d.). Matches: 8-direction movement, weapon pickups, ring-outs; best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 finals), 60-second — 60-second rounds ; open to all genders.

## Rules

Competitive rules: 80-second — round timer per ruleset, best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 finals), soul charge and critical edge mechanics per patch; arena ring-outs count; double-elimination brackets; open brackets, all genders (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Soul Blade launched in 1996 (Project Soul); the series is known for 8-way run and weapon combat, and has been at EVO since the 2000s, with Soulcalibur VI at EVO 2019 and community world circuits (Wikipedia, n.d.).
