## Basic Information

Losing chess (giveaway chess) is antichess with normal starting position and no compulsory captures; like antichess the goal is to lose all your pieces, but unlike antichess captures are NOT compulsory — you may choose to give pieces away; the player who loses all pieces (or cannot move) wins. Within the chess variants and mind games on this site, its closest relatives are [Antichess](antichess.html), [Chess](chess.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Lichess, n.d.).

## Rules

Standard chess setup and movement; the winner is the first player with no pieces left; stalemate wins for the stalemated player. Kings are normal pieces (Lichess, n.d.).

## History

The giveaway game dates to the 19th century; 'losing chess' as a distinct variant is played online (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
