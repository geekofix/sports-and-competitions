## Basic Information

Mountain biking is cycling on unpaved terrain — trails, forest roads, downhill courses — governed worldwide by the Union Cycliste Internationale (UCI); its competitive disciplines are cross-country Olympic (XCO), cross-country short track (XCC), cross-country marathon, E-MTB (pedal-assist), downhill (DHI), four-cross and [trials](mountain-bike-trials.html) (UCI, n.d.-a). The 2026 UCI Mountain Bike World Championships were held 26–30 August 2026 in Val di Sole, Italy, and awarded 17 rainbow jerseys across the disciplines (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The elite XCO titles went to Tom Pidcock of Great Britain — his second elite XCO world title — and Sina Frei of Switzerland, who won her first by more than a minute from Martina Berta of Italy and Nicole Koller of Switzerland; the XCC elite titles went to Adrien Boichis of France and Laura Stigger of Austria, and the downhill elite titles to Jackson Goldstone of Canada (defending) and Valentina Höll of Austria, her fifth consecutive DHI world title from 2022 to 2026 plus the 2026 World Cup overall (UCI, n.d.-b; Wikipedia, n.d.-b). At the end of the 2026 season the leading women in the sport are Sina Frei, Valentina Höll and Laura Stigger, with Puck Pieterse of the Netherlands and Kate Courtney of the United States among the other stars (Wikipedia, n.d.-b) (UCI, n.d.-b).

## Rules

In XCO, riders race a mass-start circuit of several laps and the first across the line wins; XCC is a shorter, faster circuit race of about 20 minutes; the marathon (XCM/XCR) is a long point-to-point or loop race; downhill is an individually timed run down a dedicated course, with practice and qualifying runs before the final; four-cross pits four riders simultaneously on a berm-lined course; E-MTB races use pedal-assist bikes with a speed cutoff; trials is scored on penalty points (UCI, n.d.-a). World Cup standings are decided by points across the season and the rainbow jersey goes to the world champion in each category. Women and men compete in separate elite categories in every discipline, on the same courses and under the same technical regulations; junior (U19) and under-23 categories exist alongside the elites (UCI, n.d.-a).

## History

Mountain biking began in the late 1970s in Marin County, California, where Gary Fisher, Joe Breeze and Charlie Cunningham built the first dedicated mountain bikes and raced down the fire roads of Mount Tamalpais — the Repack race of 1976 is regarded as the first mountain bike race (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). NORBA was founded in the United States in 1983, and the UCI held the first Mountain Bike World Championships in Durango, Colorado in 1990 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The icons of the sport include Switzerland's Nino Schurter, with ten XCO world titles from 2009 to 2022 and Olympic gold at Rio 2016; France's Julien Absalon, with five rainbow jerseys and Olympic gold at Athens 2004 and Beijing 2008; Pauline Ferrand-Prévot, five-time XCO world champion; Loïc Bruni, five DHI titles from 2015 to 2022; Rachel Atherton, six DHI world titles from 2008 to 2016; Sweden's Jenny Rissveds, the Rio 2016 Olympic women's champion; and Kate Courtney of the United States, the 2018 XCO world champion (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
