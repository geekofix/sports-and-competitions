## Basic Information

Brandubh ('black prince') is the 7x7 Celtic tafl variant of Ireland, documented in medieval Irish texts: the king with 8 defenders faces 16 attackers on a 7x7 board; the king must reach the edge to win. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Hnefatafl](hnefatafl.html), [Tablut](tablut.html), [Konane](konane.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

7x7 board; king starts in the center with 8 defenders vs 16 attackers; king escapes to the edge to win; attackers capture the king by four-side surround (two on the throne) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Brandubh is described in medieval Irish literature (e.g. the 12th–14th century tales); the board diagram shows the 7x7 layout; it is the Irish cousin of hnefatafl (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
