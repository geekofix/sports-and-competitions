## Basic Information

Skullgirls is Lab Zero Games' 2011 indie fighting game (Skullgirls 2nd Encore current), a long-running EVO main stage title known for its hand-drawn art and team — 3v3 matches (Wikipedia, n.d.) (Lab Zero Games, n.d.). Best-of-3 sets; open to all genders.

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are [Street Fighter](street-fighter.html), [Brawlhalla](brawlhalla.html), [MultiVersus](multiversus.html) and the other [fighting/platform fighters](super-smash-bros.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

Competitive rules: 3v3 teams, best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 finals), assists and IPS — infinite-prevention systems per patch; undying — lifebars; double-elimination at majors; open brackets, all genders (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Skullgirls began as a fan — indie project funded via crowdfunding — launched 2011 after Mike Zaimont's Lab Zero won ; it became an EVO staple from 2012 with its hand-drawn animation and rollback netcode (Wikipedia, n.d.).
