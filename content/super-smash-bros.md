## Basic Information

Competitive Super Smash Bros. is the esports scene of Nintendo's crossover platform-fighting series, above all Super Smash Bros. Ultimate (2018, Switch) and Super Smash Bros. Melee (2001, GameCube) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Matches are 1v1 (singles, the most prestigious form) or 2v2 doubles, played as best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 in finals) under the community tournament ruleset: stock battle with three stocks, a 7-minute time limit, items off, pause off, on tournament-legal stages (SmashWiki, n.d.).

The largest events are community majors: Supernova (formerly Super Smash Con, rebranded 2024 after Nintendo's 2023 tournament guidelines) ran 6–9 August 2026 in Baltimore with 1,581 Ultimate entrants and was won by Sparg0; the 2025 edition was won by Miya (Supernova/Wikipedia, n.d.-b). Japan's Kagaribi and the US LVL UP EXPO are other premier events, and rankings are published on UltRank/LumiRank (Liquipedia, n.d.).

All genders compete in the same open brackets — there are no gender divisions in Smash — and top players include MkLeo (widely considered the greatest Ultimate player), Sparg0, acola, Miya, Doramigi, Hurt and Carmelo (Wikipedia, n.d.-a; Liquipedia, n.d.).

## Rules

The tournament-legal ruleset for Ultimate: best-of-3 games (best-of-5 in grand finals of majors), 3 stocks, 7:00 time limit, items off, stage hazards as on the legal stage list, pause off, with a stage ban/pick procedure (counterpick stages). A game ends when a player loses all stocks, is knocked out of the blast zones, or has the higher damage percentage when time expires; Sudden Death resolves ties (SmashWiki, n.d.). Singles (1v1) is the most prestigious format; doubles (2v2) and Squad Strike (3-character rotation) also exist (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

EVO included Melee from 2013 and Ultimate from 2019 until Nintendo withdrew its license in 2022, so Smash now runs its own circuit (Smash World Tour 2024–2025 under Panda, then independent majors); anti-collusion and conduct rules are enforced by organizers (Wikipedia, n.d.-a; Wikipedia, n.d.-c). Men's and women's players compete in the same open brackets (SmashWiki, n.d.).

## History

Organized Smash competition began in 2002 with Melee, and Melee's inclusion at EVO 2013 was the turning point that brought competitive Smash into the mainstream (Wikipedia, n.d.-c). Nintendo ran the official Smash World Tour from 2019 and cancelled the 2022 championship after disputes with Panda Global; since then the community has run Supernova (the renamed Super Smash Con, founded 2015), LVL UP EXPO, Kagaribi and many other majors (Wikipedia, n.d.-b).

MkLeo (Leonardo Perez) dominated the 2019–2021 era and is widely considered the best Ultimate player of all time, alongside rivals Sparg0, Tweek, Glutonny and the Japanese Steve prodigy acola, who was ranked world #1 in 2023; Miya won Supernova 2025 and Sparg0 won the 2026 edition in Baltimore (Wikipedia, n.d.-a; Liquipedia, n.d.). Melee's own history at EVO (2014 photo below) features legends such as Armada, Mang0, Hungrybox and Zain (Wikipedia, n.d.-c).
