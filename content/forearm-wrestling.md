## Basic Information

Armwrestling (forearm wrestling) is a strength sport in which two competitors, seated or standing at a specialized table with elbow pads and a hand peg, try to pin each other's hand to the touch pad; the winner is the first to get the opponent's pin. The amateur world is governed by the World Armwrestling Federation (WAF, founded 1977, 80+ member nations, WADA-compliant), which runs the annual World Armwrestling Championships by weight class, right and left arm, men and women. The professional scene is led by supermatch promotions such as East vs West (EvW) and King of the Table (KOTT), and the former World Armwrestling League (WAL) brought the sport to ESPN in the 2010s.

## Rules

Under the 2025 WAF Rules, competitors strap their hands together in a strap and grip the peg; the referee aligns the shoulders and elbows over the center, calls "ready, go," and the match begins. A foul (false start, moving the shoulder, crossing the center line with the elbow, or touching the peg improperly) gives a warning, and two fouls lose the match; the match is won when any part of the opponent's hand from the wrist up touches the touch pad, or when the opponent's elbow leaves the elbow pad. Matches are best-of-three or single pulls in some divisions; weight classes, right/left arm events and para divisions are contested. Table dimensions, elbow pad size and strap specs are certified by the WAF. Anti-doping follows the WADA Code.

## History

Armwrestling has ancient roots (depicted in Egyptian tomb art and Greek pottery) and was a popular pub and fair contest in 19th-century Britain and America. The first recognized world championship was held in 1962 in Petaluma, California. The WAF was founded in 1977 and has held annual World Championships since (the 42nd was in Bucharest 2021). Professional armwrestling boomed in the 2010s with the WAL on ESPN (stars like Devon Larratt, "The Beast"), and since 2021 East vs West (Engin Terzi) and King of the Table (Neil Pickup) stage supermatches, with Devon Larratt, Vitaly Laletin, Levan Saginashvili and Ermes Gasparini among the biggest names.
