## Basic Information

Gran Turismo is Polyphony Digital's driving simulation (Gran Turismo 7 since 2022); its global esport is the Gran Turismo World Series (GTWS), with two concurrent championships — the Nations Cup (players represent their country) and the Manufacturers Cup (players represent a car brand) — running online qualifiers into live World Tour rounds and the December World Finals (Wikipedia, n.d.). The 2026 season (9th official) opened at Round 1 in Milan on 23 May 2026, where Samuel Moreno took a debut live-event win; the World Finals are in Tokyo in December 2026 (gran-turismo.com, n.d.).

## Rules

GTWS rules: 12 drivers per cup qualify from online qualifiers plus the 2025 top-3; a sprint race sets the grand-final grid; points are awarded for the top finishers; the best five of six online rounds count; live events run at venues such as Hangar-7 Salzburg, Milan, Tokyo and Singapore; men and women compete in the same open qualifiers (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Gran Turismo's esport began in 2014 with the FIA partnership (FIA Gran Turismo Championships on GT Sport, 2015–2021, with the FIA Prize Giving honoring champions); since 2022 the series runs on GT7 as the GTWS. Record champions include Igor Fraga (inaugural 2018 Nations Cup), Takuma Miyazono (five-time champion, real-world Nürburgring VLN and 2026 24h Nürburgring entrant) and Kanata Kawakami (Wikipedia, n.d.).
