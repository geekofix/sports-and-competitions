## Basic Information

"Gléma" is an alternate spelling of glima, the traditional Icelandic upright wrestling sport. This entry cross-references the main article: see [Glima](glima.html), which covers the rules, styles and history in full.

## Rules

The rules are those of Icelandic glima: standing grappling above the waist, first fall wins, belt and sleeve grips, no leg holds. Full details are in the [Glima](glima.html) entry on this site.

## History

Glima is documented in the medieval Icelandic sagas from the 12th century and was the sport of the Viking Age and the Icelandic Commonwealth; see [Glima](glima.html) for the complete history, including the revival in the 20th century and today's national championships.
