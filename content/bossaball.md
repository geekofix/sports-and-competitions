## Basic Information

Bossaball is a team ball sport invented in 2004 by Belgian Filip Eyckmans that combines volleyball, football, gymnastics and capoeira — with music. It is played on an inflatable court with a round trampoline on each side of an adjustable net; a "samba referee" with a microphone and DJ set runs the show. Bossaball Sports S.L. organizes international championships, including World and European Cups.

## Rules

Under the official Bossaball rules, teams of three to five players (mixed gender allowed) play on the inflatable court. One player per side stands on the trampoline ("attacker") while teammates surround it. The serve is thrown by hand or foot over the net. A rally allows up to eight ball contacts, with limits on consecutive contacts with head/feet (max 2) and hands (max 1). Points: the attacking team scores 3 points if the ball lands on the opponent's trampoline and 1 point on the inflatable floor; touching the bossawall (the ring around the trampoline) keeps the rally alive. A set is won at 25 points (two-point lead), the third set at 15; matches are best of three sets.

## History

Bossaball was conceptualized between 2003 and 2005 by Filip Eyckmans, a Belgian former national-team tennis player and DJ living in Spain. The first World Cup was held in 2009 in Turkey, and European Cups have run since 2010, with Brazil, Belgium and the Netherlands among the top nations. The portable court inflates in about 45 minutes and the sport is played on beaches, in schools and at music festivals across Europe and South America.
