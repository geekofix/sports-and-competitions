## Basic Information

Beach handball is handball played on sand: smaller teams, a smaller goal and faster, more spectacular play than the indoor game, with diving and aerial shots. It is governed by the International Handball Federation (IHF), which organizes the Beach Handball World Championship for men and women, and it was a Youth Olympic sport at Buenos Aires 2018 and Paris 2024.

## Rules

Under IHF beach handball rules, two teams of four players (three court players plus a goalkeeper) play on a 27 x 12 m sand court with a 2 x 1.6 m goal. A match has two 10-minute halves with a running clock. Goals scored by a "specialist" (spin shots, kung-fu), goalkeeper saves or in-flight goals count double. Throw-offs after goals are taken by the goalkeeper, and players may be substituted freely. At the World Championships, teams play a group stage followed by knockout rounds, with shootouts to decide ties.

## History

Beach handball grew out of indoor handball in Italy and Denmark in the 1990s, where players trained on sand in summer. The IHF codified the beach rules and held the first Beach Handball World Championship in 2004 in Egypt. The sport debuted at the Youth Olympic Games in Buenos Aires 2018 and returned at Paris 2024, and the IHF continues to run world championships for both sexes every two years.
