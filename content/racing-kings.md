## Basic Information

Racing Kings is a chess variant where the goal is to race your king to the eighth rank first; check and checkmate do not exist — you may not give check, and a king may never move into check. Within the chess variants and mind games on this site, its closest relatives are [King of the Hill Chess](king-of-the-hill-chess.html), [Three-Check Chess](three-check-chess.html), [Chess](chess.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Lichess, n.d.).

## Rules

Pieces move as in chess, but giving check is illegal (a move that attacks the enemy king is forbidden). The first king to reach the back rank (8th for White, 1st for Black) wins; if both would arrive, the one arriving first wins; touching rules apply (Lichess, n.d.).

## History

Racing Kings is a modern variant (late 2000s) that became a standard lichess variant with its own online world championship (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
