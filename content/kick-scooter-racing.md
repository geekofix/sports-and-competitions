## Basic Information

Kick scooter racing is racing on non-motorized kick scooters (trottinettes) on closed urban circuits, kart tracks or park loops; the World Scooter Championship crowns champions — 2017 world champion was the American Dante Hutchinson — and national federations (e.g., in France) run city race series (Wikipedia, n.d.). Formats are mass-start heats and finals over 200 m–5 km; men's and women's categories; helmets mandatory.

## Rules

Race rules: mass-start heats of six to eight riders advancing to a final; distances from 200 m sprints to several-kilometre courses; standing start; scooters with 110–200 mm wheels, no motors; helmet and gloves mandatory; categories by gender and age group; false starts penalized (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Kick-scooter racing formalized in the 2000s–2010s alongside the freestyle boom, with the World Scooter Championship (Dante Hutchinson, USA, 2017 champion) and French/European city series; relay formats (Tretroller-Staffel) also exist (Wikipedia, n.d.).
