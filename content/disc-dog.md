## Basic Information

Disc dog (also called canine disc) is a competitive sport in which handlers throw flying discs and their dogs catch them, judged on distance, accuracy and choreographed freestyle routines. The premier international event is the World Canine Disc Championship organized by Skyhoundz, alongside long-running series such as the Ashley Whippet Invitational, the UpDog Challenge and the UFO World Cup. The sport grew out of Frisbee-catching dogs in the 1970s and is now played in more than a dozen countries.

## Rules

Competitions are divided into distance/accuracy events and freestyle routines. In distance events such as DiscDogathon Bullseye, Spot Landing or Time Trial, the handler throws the disc and the dog scores points for catching it in marked zones; in freestyle, a routine of 60 to 90 seconds is performed to music and judged on catches, vaults (the dog using the handler's body as a launch platform), choreography and difficulty. Major events mandate certified discs such as Hyperflite, because disc hardness and flight stability affect both fairness and dog safety. In pairs events, two handlers alternate throws while their dogs compete together. At the Skyhoundz World Championship, the field is whittled down through qualifying rounds to a final freestyle and distance competition.

## History

Disc dog began in the mid-1970s when Ashley Whippet (Alex Stein) and his dog Ashley Whippet performed disc catches during a televised baseball rain delay in 1974 and again on The Tonight Show, turning a bar trick into a spectator sport. Competitive canine disc events spread across the United States in the late 1970s and 1980s, with the first organized tournaments held in the early 1980s. Skyhoundz, founded in 1996 by Irv Lander, grew into the largest organization and crowned the first World Canine Disc Champions. The UpDog Challenge (founded 1999 by Todd Carlson and others) and the UFO (United Frisbee Operators) World Cup added standardized rules and international circuits. Today the sport combines athletic dog training with showmanship, and top teams tour internationally year-round.
