## Basic Information

Scopa is the classic Italian 'broom' game: with a 40-card deck, players capture table cards by matching rank (sum combinations in scopone), and score for cards captured (especially sevens of— diamonds); the national Italian card game with championships. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Briscola](briscola.html), [Truco](truco.html), [Canasta](canasta.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (FIS, n.d.).

## Rules

40-card Italian deck; 3 cards each (4 in scopone) + 4 on table; play a hand card to capture table cards of equal value or summing (in scopone) to its value; scopa = clearing the table; scoring: most cards, most diamonds, seven of diamonds ('sette bello'), prime; game to 11/16/21 points (FIS, n.d.).

## History

Scopa is documented in Italy from the 18th century and is the national game (the Italian federation organizes championships); the Modiano 'napoletane' deck photo shows the cards; Scopa 02.png shows a trick (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
