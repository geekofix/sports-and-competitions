## Basic Information

Krav Maga (Hebrew for "contact combat") is the Israeli self-defense and fighting system developed by Imi Lichtenfeld (1910-1998), combining techniques from boxing, wrestling, judo and jujutsu into simple, instinctive movements. It is the official hand-to-hand combat system of the Israel Defense Forces, Israeli police and security services, and is taught to civilians worldwide by organizations such as the International Krav Maga Federation (IKMF), founded in 1995/96 with Lichtenfeld's support out of the Israeli Krav Maga Association (IKMA, 1978).

## Rules

Krav Maga is a self-defense system, not a sport, and has no kata or competition rules — it is governed by the law, not by a rulebook. Its core principles are: neutralize the threat as quickly as possible, attack and defend simultaneously, target the opponent's most vulnerable points (eyes, throat, groin, knees), use any available object as a weapon, and control the situation until help arrives. Training covers defenses against strikes, kicks, grabs, chokes, multiple attackers, and knife, stick and gun threats, in standing, sitting and ground positions, and in the dark or confined spaces. Practitioners progress through a belt/level system from white to black, with specialized programs for military, police and security personnel.

## History

Imi Lichtenfeld was born in Bratislava, excelled as a wrestler, boxer and gymnast, and fled anti-Semitic riots to Mandatory Palestine in the 1930s. He trained Jewish resistance units (Haganah, Palmach, Pal-yam) in fighting, swimming and self-defense, and from 1948 served as Chief Instructor for Physical Fitness and Krav Maga at the Israel Defense Forces' combat fitness school for 20 years, refining the system for soldiers of all fitness levels. After retiring from the IDF in 1964, he taught civilians and law enforcement with the motto "so that one may walk in peace", and founded the IKMA in 1978. Today Krav Maga is taught to millions of people in more than 100 countries and to numerous armies and police forces around the world.
