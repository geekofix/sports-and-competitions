## Basic Information

Renju is the strictly regulated form of gomoku: black's double-threes, double-fours and overlines are forbidden, which balances the first-move advantage; the Renju International Federation runs world championships since 1989 and a professional league. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Gomoku](gomoku.html), [Omok](omok.html), [Go](go.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Renju International Federation, n.d.).

## Rules

All gomoku rules plus forbidden-move rules for black (overline, double-three, double-four, four-three); black wins only with exactly five. World Renju Championship and team events are held annually; top players include Okazaki (Japan, pictured 2025) (Renju International Federation, n.d.).

## History

Renju rules were codified in Japan in the 20th century to make gomoku fair; the RIF (founded 1989) governs international play; the 2025 Renju-ji Okazaki memorial tournament in Okazaki shows its Japanese heartland (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
