## Basic Information

Trail running is off-road running on unpaved trails and mountain paths over distances from about 10 km to ultra distances, governed for international competition by World Athletics through the World Mountain and Trail Running Championships, with the global race calendar and ITRA ranking points run by the International Trail Running Association (World Athletics, n.d.-a; International Trail Running Association, n.d.). At the end of the 2026 season the women's trail scene was headlined by Blandine L'Hirondel of France (pictured), who won the 2026 UTMB in 21:54:49 and Transvulcania in a course record of 7:43:47, ahead of Rachel Entrekin and Emily Schmitz, while the men's classics were led by Kilian Jornet of Spain and the 2026 UTMB winner (World Athletics, n.d.-b; UTMB World Series, n.d.). The flagship races are the UTMB around Mont Blanc (171 km), Zegama-Aizkorri, the World Mountain and Trail Running Championships and the Skyrunning series (UTMB World Series, n.d.-a).

## Rules

Trail races are point-to-point or loop courses on natural terrain with marked routes and aid stations; time cut-offs apply, poles are allowed in most races, and the start/finish area must allow chip timing (World Athletics, n.d.-a; International Trail Running Association, n.d.). The World Mountain and Trail Running Championships contest the short trail, long trail, mountain classic and uphill disciplines in senior and U20 categories; races are scored by ITRA points for the world rankings, and World Athletics ratifies world bests on certified courses (World Athletics, n.d.-a; World Athletics, n.d.-b). Men and women race separately with equal categories, and vertical kilometer (uphill) and skyrunning (high-altitude) formats have their own championships (International Trail Running Association, n.d.-a).

## History

Competitive trail running grew from the fell races of northern England and the Scottish hill races of the 19th century, and from mountain races such as the early 20th-century hill runs in Britain (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Zegama-Aizkorri in the Basque Country, first held in 2002, became the spring classic of the Skyrunner World Series — its 2016 women's podium is pictured (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Kilian Jornet of Spain won UTMB, Hardrock 100 and the Grand Raid on Réunion as a teenager, and the UTMB, started in 2003, grew into a 10,000-runner festival around Mont Blanc; the World Mountain and Trail Running Championships, first held in 1985, are now a biennial World Athletics event (UTMB World Series, n.d.; World Athletics, n.d.-a).
