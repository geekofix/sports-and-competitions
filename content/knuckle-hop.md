## Basic Information

Knuckle hop (seal hop) is a WEIO and Arctic event: the athlete hops across a measured course on the knuckles of both fists (fists closed, walking on knuckles), and the fastest time or greatest distance wins (WEIO, n.d.). At the 2026 WEIO Kyle Worl (Juneau) set the record at 152 ft 6 in (ICT News, 2026). Men's and women's categories. Within the Arctic and Indigenous sports on this site, its closest relatives are [Blanket Toss](blanket-toss.html), [Snow Snake](snow-snake.html), [Two-Foot High Kick](two-foot-high-kick.html), [Finger Pull](finger-pull.html), [Hand Games](hand-games.html), [World Eskimo Indian Olympics](world-eskimo-indian-olympics.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

Starting behind a line, the athlete moves forward on closed fists only; any use of open palms or knees resets the attempt. Timed over a set distance (WEIO, n.d.).

## History

The knuckle hop mimics the movement of seals on ice and has been a WEIO staple since the 1960s (Wikipedia, n.d.).
