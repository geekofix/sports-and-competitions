## Basic Information

Twister is the physical party game invented by Charles Foley and Harold Funtime in 1966 and sold by Milton Bradley: a spinner calls 'left hand red' etc. on a mat of colored dots; players place limbs without falling. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Jenga](jenga.html), [Connect Four](connect-four.html), [Taboo](taboo.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Hasbro/Milton Bradley, n.d.).

## Rules

A giant mat with 24 circles in 4 colors (red, yellow, green, blue); spinner with 16 combinations (limb + color); players place the called limb on the called color; elbows/knees must stay down; first to fall loses. Guinness-record group games exist (Hasbro/Milton Bradley, n.d.).

## History

Invented by Charles Foley in 1966 (with Harold Funtime); Milton Bradley published 1966; the 1972 PD photo shows a family game; twister parties photographed in Italy (2021) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
