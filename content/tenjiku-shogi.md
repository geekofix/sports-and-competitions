## Basic Information

Tenjiku shogi ('astronomical shogi') is the largest historical shogi variant on a 16x16 board, named after the tenjiku (heavenly) theme of its pieces; it is played today by enthusiasts. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Shogi](shogi.html), [Dai Shogi](dai-shogi.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## Rules

16x16 board with astronomical-themed pieces and complex movement; drops as in shogi; mate wins (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

## History

Tenjiku shogi represents the peak of the shogi-variant tradition before the 16th-century standardization on the 9x9 board; modern handmade sets (e.g. the 2022 set by John Redford of Austin, Texas) reconstruct it (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
