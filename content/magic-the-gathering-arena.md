## Basic Information

Magic: The Gathering Arena is Daybreak Games' 2018 digital version of Wizards of the Coast's 1993 card game; its esport is the MTG World Championship on Arena plus qualifier events (Arena Opens) in the 2026 cycle — open to all genders, and women have won Pro Tour titles (Daybreak Games / Wizards, n.d.). The physical Pro Tour ran from 1997 to 2019 before moving fully digital in 2020 (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## Rules

Competitive rules: best-of-3 matches with sideboarding between games, best-of-5 in the top 8; rotating constructed and limited formats; draft and sealed qualifiers on Arena; World Championship invites via the players' ranking; open to all genders (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

Richard Garfield created Magic in 1993; Arena entered open beta in 2018 and became the platform for the Pro Tour and World Championship from 2020; the physical game's World Championship dates back to 1994 (Wikipedia, n.d.).
