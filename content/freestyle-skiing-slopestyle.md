## Basic Information

Slopestyle is freestyle skiing's terrain-park discipline: riders ski a course of rails, boxes and two or three jumps (kickers), linking technical rail tricks and big aerials, and are judged on the whole run (FIS, n.d.-b; Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Governed by FIS within the FIS Freestyle Ski World Cup, slopestyle has been an Olympic event since Sochi 2014 (FIS, n.d.-a). At the Milano Cortina 2026 Winter Olympics in Livigno (February 7–22, 2026), Birk Ruud of Norway won the men's title with 86.28 ahead of Alex Hall (USA) and Luca Harrington (NZL), and Mathilde Gremaud of Switzerland the women's gold with 86.96 ahead of Eileen Gu (CHN) and Megan Oldham (CAN) (Olympics, n.d.). In the 2025–26 FIS Freestyle Ski World Cup (November 20, 2025 – March 29, 2026), the slopestyle Crystal Globes went to Birk Ruud (NOR) on the men's side and Kirsty Muir (GBR) — who also took the women's park & pipe overall globe — on the women's side, while the park & pipe overall men's globe went to Henry Sildaru (EST) (FIS, n.d.-a).

At the end of 2026 the leading women are Mathilde Gremaud (SUI), the 2026 Olympic slopestyle champion and 2022 Olympic silver medalist, Eileen Gu (CHN), 2026 slopestyle silver medalist and big air/halfpipe champion, and Kirsty Muir (GBR), the 2025–26 slopestyle globe winner, while on the men's side Birk Ruud (NOR), 2026 Olympic champion and 2025–26 slopestyle globe winner, heads the rankings ahead of Alex Hall (USA) and Luca Harrington (NZL) (Olympics, n.d.; FIS, n.d.-a).

## Rules

Under the FIS freestyle slopestyle rules, the course is about 450–700 m long with a sequence of rail/box features followed by two or three kickers (jumps) with a landing zone each; the whole course must be used (FIS, n.d.-b). In qualification each athlete takes two runs (best counts); in the final three runs, of which the best two count (FIS, n.d.-b). Each run is scored 0–100 by judges using whole-course scoring — difficulty, execution, amplitude, variety and progression — across both the jib (rail/box) and jump sections; a fall or a stopped run scores low, and the start list runs from lowest to highest qualifier so the top seeds go last (FIS, n.d.-b). Helmets are mandatory; men and women compete in separate events on identical courses, and junior classes (U18) exist at World Cup level (FIS, n.d.-b). The FIS Freestyle Ski World Cup crowns the slopestyle Crystal Globe at season's end, and the FIS Freestyle Ski World Championships (odd years since 1986; 2025 in Engadin) award world titles (FIS, n.d.-a).

## History

Slopestyle emerged from the new-school freestyle movement of the late 1990s–2000s, combining street-style rail tricks with big-kick jumps, and joined the Olympic program at Sochi 2014, won by the USA's Sage Kotsenburg and Canada's Dara Howell (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Norway's Øystein Bråten took the men's title at PyeongChang 2018 and the USA's Alex Hall at Beijing 2022; Canada's Mark McMorris and the USA's Devin Logan are among the discipline's best-known names, with Norway's Johanne Killi and Switzerland's Sarah Höfflin (2018 Olympic champion) leading the women's era (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Birk Ruud's 2026 Olympic victory in Livigno added to his 2022 Olympic bronze, and Mathilde Gremaud followed her 2022 Olympic silver with the 2026 gold (Wikipedia, n.d.-a; Olympics, n.d.).
