## Basic Information

Baseball5 is the five-a-side, mixed-gender street version of baseball and softball, played without a bat, glove or pitcher on a small square field; it is governed worldwide by the World Baseball Softball Confederation (World Baseball Softball Confederation [WBSC], n.d.-a), which launched the format in Havana in 2017 and sanctions the Baseball5 World Cup. Because every team must field at least two women and two men at all times, Baseball5 is one of the few genuinely mixed-gender team sports on the Olympic programme — it made its Youth Olympic Games debut at Dakar 2026 (October 31–November 13, 2026), the first IOC event held in Africa, where Cuba won the gold medal over Chinese Taipei (International Olympic Committee [IOC], n.d.). Cuba also won the 2025 Youth Baseball5 World Cup in Tepic, Mexico, beating Chinese Taipei 2–0, while at the 2026 Pan American Championship in Barquisimeto, Venezuela, the hosts ended Cuba's 31-game unbeaten run with a 2–1 victory (WBSC, n.d.-a). The 2026 Baseball5 World Cup, held December 9–13, 2026 in San Juan, Puerto Rico, was the first edition to feature 16 teams, with two-time defending champion Cuba among the favourites (WBSC, n.d.-c).

## Rules

Baseball5 is played on a square field 21 m on each side with four bases set 13 m apart; there is no pitcher and no bat — the batter strikes a small rubber ball with the hand or fist from the home area, and fielders play the ball with their hands only (WBSC, n.d.-b). Each team fields five players, and at least two of them must be women and two men at all times; a game lasts five innings, each divided into a batting and a fielding half, and three outs end a half-inning. A run is scored when a batter-runner safely touches every base in order and returns to home plate; outs are recorded by a clean catch, a tag or a force out, exactly as in baseball. Matches are decided in best-of-three or best-of-five series and typically take 15–20 minutes. Youth competitions are staged in age-group categories with shortened fields and games, while the senior World Cup uses the full format for both men's and women's national teams, which compete in the same mixed-team format (WBSC, n.d.-b).

## History

Baseball5 was developed by the WBSC and first played publicly in the streets of Havana, Cuba, in 2017 as a compact, urban derivative of baseball and softball that needs no specialist equipment; the inaugural Baseball5 World Cup followed in Havana in 2018, and Cuba quickly established itself as the dominant nation of the young sport, winning consecutive senior and youth titles through 2025 (WBSC, n.d.-a). The format was designed from the outset as a mixed-gender game — the two-per-gender rule is written into the laws — so women and men have competed on equal terms since the first tournaments, including the 2025 Youth World Cup and the Youth Olympic Games debut at Dakar 2026 (IOC, n.d.).
