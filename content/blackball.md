## Basic Information

Blackball — also called English 8-ball or British pool — is the pool variant played with a 7-ball set (1–7 solids, black 8) on a 6 ft × 3 ft table with rounded pockets, governed internationally by the World Pool-Billiard Association (World Pool-Billiard Association [WPA], n.d.-a). At the 2026 WPA Blackball World Championships at the Thistle Hotel Heathrow in London (October 21–28, 2026), Gareth Hibbott of England defeated Christophe Lambert of France 6–1 in the Men's A final — Hibbott's first WPA world title, adding to his IPA world titles of 2016 and 2024, which made him the only man to win the IPA world championship twice; France were crowned Women's A team champions, and Calvin Creach of France won the Youth World Grand Prix (WPA, n.d.-b) (World Pool-Billiard Association, n.d.-a) (World Pool-Billiard Association, n.d.-b).

On the women's side, the Women's A competition at the 2026 worlds was won by France, and women compete on equal terms in open, women's and youth divisions at every WPA Blackball world championship (WPA, n.d.-b).

 Within the billiards/pool/snooker family on this site, its closest relatives are [8 Ball Pool](8-ball-pool.html), [9 Ball Pool](9-ball-pool.html), [Snooker](snooker.html), [Billiards](billiards.html), [Carom Billiards](carom-billiards.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

Blackball uses a black 8-ball instead of the American 8: after the break, the table is open until a player legally pockets a ball, which assigns them either the 1–7 group or the 9–15 group (the balls numbered 9–15 count as the stripes equivalent); the black 8 may only be pocketed after all of the player's group are cleared, and potting it early loses the game (WPA, n.d.-a). Standard pool fouls apply: scratch (cue ball pocketed) is ball-in-hand behind the baulk line, touching a ball, jumping the cue ball, or potting the 8 early all lose the frame; a legal break requires four balls to the cushions or a ball pocketed (WPA, n.d.-a). Matches are typically best-of-races in casual play and knockout rounds at world championships, with men's, women's, youth (U16/U18) and team (A/B) classifications; junior events use a smaller table or reduced race length in some federations (WPA, n.d.-b).

## History

Blackball evolved from English billiards and early pool in the United Kingdom in the early 20th century, spreading through the British Commonwealth and Ireland, where it is still played in pubs and clubs; the black 8-ball set and rounded "mushroom" pockets distinguish it from American 8-ball. The World Pool-Billiard Association formalized Blackball as an official discipline and has run the WPA Blackball World Championships since the 2000s; the 2026 edition in London was the largest yet, with 15 nations (WPA, n.d.-b). Women's world championships have been part of the event for years, and the youth programme has grown steadily, producing stars such as Calvin Creach, the 2026 Youth World Grand Prix winner (WPA, n.d.-b).
