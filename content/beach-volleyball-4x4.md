## Basic Information

Beach volleyball 4x4 is the four-player-per-team format of beach volleyball, used in youth, amateur and multi-sport events. Like 2x2 beach volleyball it is played on sand with a net, but with bigger teams, a slightly different rotation and scoring system. It is governed by the FIVB and appears at events such as the ANOC World Beach Games and youth world championships.

## Rules

Under FIVB 4x4 rules, each team has four players on a 16 x 8 m sand court with the net at 2.43 m (men) / 2.24 m (women). Teams may use up to four touches (one more than in 2x2) to return the ball, and blocking counts as a touch. Matches are usually best of three sets to 21 points (win by two, cap 30), third set to 15, with side-out scoring and end switches every 7 points. Mixed-gender 4x4 is common at youth and beach multi-sport events, where teams must field at least two players of each gender on court.

## History

The 4x4 format grew out of casual beach volleyball, where larger groups play together; it was formalized for youth and development competitions and featured at the Youth Olympic Games and the World Beach Games. The FIVB includes 4x4 in its event portfolio alongside the professional 2x2 World Tour, and national federations use it to introduce more players to beach volleyball.
