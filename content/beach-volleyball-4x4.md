## Basic Information

Beach volleyball 4x4 is a four-a-side variant of beach volleyball governed by the FIVB and played on the same 16 m × 8 m sand court, with up to six players per team of whom four are on the court at any time; it is the format contested at the World Beach Games (Fédération Internationale de Volleyball [FIVB], n.d.-b). The first World Beach Games, held at Katara Beach in Doha, Qatar, October 12–16, 2019, featured 4x4 tournaments in which the USA won both gold medals — the men beat Qatar with Indonesia bronze, and the women beat Brazil with Canada bronze (Canada's squad included Cowley, Sider, Battiston, Cockrell, Nagy and Saxton) — while the 2021 edition was cancelled and the next World Beach Games are scheduled for Da Nang, Vietnam, in 2027 (Association of National Olympic Committees [ANOC], n.d.). Because four players share the court, 4x4 is faster and more athletic than 2x2, with a maximum of four touches per side before the ball must cross the net. Men's and women's tournaments are played under identical rules, and the format is also used in university and corporate beach sports events (Fédération Internationale de Volleyball, n.d.-a) (Fédération Internationale de Volleyball, n.d.-b).

## Rules

Under the FIVB rules for the 4x4 format, a team may register up to six players but exactly four play at any time, and substitutions are limited per set; the court (16 m × 8 m), net heights (2.43 m men, 2.24 m women) and scoring (best of three sets to 21 points, 15 in the decider, win by two) are the same as in 2x2 beach volleyball (FIVB, n.d.-a). Each side may touch the ball a maximum of four times — one more than in 2x2 — before returning it, a block counts as a touch, and double contacts and open-hand tips are penalized as in 2x2. Teams switch ends every seven points (five in the deciding set) and have one timeout per set. Men's and women's matches use identical rules apart from the net height, and youth 4x4 events use reduced court sizes (FIVB, n.d.-a).

## History

The 4x4 format was developed by the FIVB as a more social, squad-based version of beach volleyball and was showcased at the inaugural World Beach Games in Doha 2019, where the USA swept both titles; the women's final saw the Americans defeat Brazil and Canada take bronze, while in the men's final Qatar took silver behind the USA and Indonesia bronze (ANOC, n.d.). The 2021 World Beach Games in Bali were cancelled, and the 2027 edition in Da Nang, Vietnam, will again feature 4x4 alongside beach soccer, beach tennis, beach wrestling and other sand sports. On the women's side, the 2019 Doha tournament confirmed that 4x4 gives women's squads a larger on-court role than in 2x2, with six-player rosters rotating through four court spots (FIVB, n.d.-b).
