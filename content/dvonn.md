## Basic Information

DVONN (2001) is the second GIPF-project game by Kris Burm: pieces are stacked on a 11x5 board and move in straight lines a number of spaces equal to the stack height; only stacks topped by a red dvonn piece can move; the largest stack wins by immobilizing all others onto itself; at the end, stacks not containing a dvonn piece are removed; the largest remaining stack wins. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [GIPF](gipf.html), [TZAAR](tzaar.html), [ZERTZ](zertz.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (GIPF project, n.d.).

## Rules

11x5 board (49 spaces); start with pieces placed in the standard setup; stacks move in straight lines exactly their height in spaces, only onto occupied spaces; only stacks with a red dvonn piece on top move in the movement phase; then all stacks without dvonn are removed; biggest stack wins (GIPF project, n.d.).

## History

Designed by Kris Burm in 2001 as part of the GIPF project; it has a world championship and deep stacking strategy (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
