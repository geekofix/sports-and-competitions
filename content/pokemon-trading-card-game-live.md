## Basic Information

The Pokémon Trading Card Game (The Pokémon Company, n.d.) has both a physical and a digital esport: Pokémon TCG Live (The Pokémon Company, n.d.) carries the digital qualifiers for the Pokémon TCG World Championships, with divisions for juniors (ages 8–12), seniors (13–15) and masters (16+) — a genuine age-class structure; genders compete together in the masters division (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## Rules

TCG rules: 60-card decks with six prize cards as a win condition (knockout or deck-out also win); single-game matches in Swiss rounds; age divisions junior/senior/masters; mixed gender in masters (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## History

The TCG was created in 1996 by Creatures Inc.; PTCGL replaced Pokémon TCG Online in 2022 and became the official digital qualifier path to the World Championships from 2023 (Wikipedia, n.d.).
