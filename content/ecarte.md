## Basic Information

Écarté (French 'pinch') is a two-player French card game with a 32-card deck (7–A): players draw and discard to improve a 5-card hand, then play tricks; the goal is to win the majority of tricks/points over several hands. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Piquet](piquet.html), [Bezique](bezique.html), [Whist](whist.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## Rules

32-card deck; 5-card hands plus a talon; at each trick both players play a card, no trump in the basic game — the higher card of the led suit wins each trick; the match is scored over several hands; it is a piquet-derived game (Pagat.com, n.d.).

## History

Écarté was invented in France in the 19th century as a simplified piquet for two players; the French 52-card pack's écarté ranking documents the card order used (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
