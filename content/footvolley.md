## Basic Information

Footvolley is a beach sport combining beach volleyball, football and tennis: two teams of two (or three) players on a sand court hit a football over a net using any body part except hands and arms. It was created in 1965 by Octavio de Moraes on Copacabana Beach, Rio de Janeiro, originally as "pevoley." There is no single official world federation, but the main international bodies are the International Footvolley Federation (FIFV), World Footvolley (thefootvolley.com, organizing the World Footvolley Federation) and the Footvolley World League (FVWL, footvolleyworldleague.com), with regional bodies such as the European Footvolley League (EFVL).

## Rules

Under the FIFV official rules, the commonly used international court is 9 m x 18 m in total with a net 2.20 m high for men and 2.10 m for women and the pro tour, and teams of two players. Each team may touch the ball a maximum of three times, and no player may touch it twice consecutively; the ball may bounce once on the ground per team. A point is scored when the ball lands in the opponent's court, the opponents fail to return within three touches, or a fault is committed. Sets are played to 15, 18 or 21 points (win by two) depending on the competition, best of three sets. Serves are underhand or overhand from behind the end line. The US Pro Footvolley Tour uses a 2.10 m net and awards up to three 2-point "air" shots per set.

## History

Footvolley was invented in 1965 by Octavio de Moraes in Rio de Janeiro, when beach footballers were banned from playing football on Copacabana sand and switched to a volleyball net, naming it "pevoley" before it became "futevôlei." It spread through Brazil in the 1970s (players lowered the team size from five to two as the game speed increased) and internationally from the 1990s-2000s, with the first international events outside Brazil in 2003 (USA Footvolley, Miami). The European Footvolley League and the Footvolley World League organize international tours and world championships; Israel's Corona Footvolley League (since 2008) is one of the few year-round leagues. Stars include Brazilian legends Dirceu and Luigi and the Helinho/Renan era of the 2000s.
