## Basic Information

Stick pull (Eskimo stick pull) is a Dene grip-strength event: two athletes sit facing each other and try to pull a greased, tapered wooden stick out of each other's grip — the first to lose grip or have the stick cross the centre line loses (AWG, n.d.; WEIO, n.d.). Contested in men's, women's and youth divisions at the Arctic Winter Games and WEIO. Within the Arctic and Indigenous sports on this site, its closest relatives are [Blanket Toss](blanket-toss.html), [Snow Snake](snow-snake.html), [Two-Foot High Kick](two-foot-high-kick.html), [Finger Pull](finger-pull.html), [Hand Games](hand-games.html), [World Eskimo Indian Olympics](world-eskimo-indian-olympics.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links.

## Rules

The stick is greased (shortening) and tapered from the centre; competitors sit with feet braced and pull the stick hand-over-hand without regripping across the centre. Best of three decides (WEIO, n.d.).

## History

Like finger pull, stick pull mimics hauling fish from a fish wheel or pulling sled ropes; it became a formal Dene/Arctic sport at the 1990 Arctic Winter Games and is a WEIO staple (Wikipedia, n.d.).
