## Basic Information

Dragon Ball FighterZ (DBFZ) is Arc System Works' 2018 anime fighter based on the Dragon Ball manga/anime; it has been an EVO main-stage game and runs the DBFZ World Tour (Wikipedia, n.d.; Liquipedia, n.d.; Bandai Namco / Arc System Works, n.d.). Matches: 3v3 assists, best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 finals); open to all genders.

## Rules

Competitive rules: 3v3 teams with assists and super dashes, best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 grand finals), 99-second — rounds per ruleset; character draft in some tourneys; open brackets, all genders (Liquipedia, n.d.).
