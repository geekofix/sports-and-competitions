## Basic Information

Quoridor is a pawn-race game invented by Mirko Marchesi in 1997: two or four players race a pawn from the back rank to the opposite side on a 9x9 board, placing up to 10 wall segments each to block the opponent — but never completely. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are [Blokus](blokus.html), [Kamisado](kamisado.html), [Chess](chess.html); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Gigamic, n.d.).

## Rules

9x9 board, one pawn per player starting mid-back-rank; each turn either move the pawn one square (orthogonally/diagonally when facing) or place a wall (2 cells long) from 10 per player; walls may not fully block a path. First pawn to reach the opposite side wins. World Quoridor Championship exists (Gigamic, n.d.).

## History

Invented in 1997 by Mirko Marchesi (published by Gigamic, France); the World Quoridor Championship is held since the 2010s (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
