## Basic Information

BlazBlue is Arc System Works' 2008 anime fighting series (drive — rapid/cancel systems, barrier bursts); BlazBlue Centralfiction is the long-running competitive version, played at EVO and ARC Revo (Wikipedia, n.d.; Liquipedia, n.d.) (Arc System Works, n.d.). Best-of-3 sets, best-of-5 finals, 99-second rounds; open to all genders.

## Rules

Competitive rules: best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 grand finals), barrier — heat systems per the current patch; stage veto; double elimination at majors; open brackets (Liquipedia, n.d.).

## History

BlazBlue launched in 2008 as the spiritual successor to Guilty Gear's — created by the same team around Toshimichi Takeuchi ; it ran at EVO from the 2010s and at the Arc System Works World Tour / ARC Revo circuit (Wikipedia, n.d.).
