Warrior Dash

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Warrior Dash is the best-known commercial obstacle course race (OCR): a 5 km course with around 12–16 obstacles — mud pits, walls, cargo nets, fire — run for time and for fun, with costumes encouraged (Wikipedia, n.d.). It belongs to the obstacle-racing family with Spartan Race, Tough Mudder and mud runs.

Figure: CAMiller62. (2012-06-14). Mud run obstacle course event [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Mudrun1.jpg

Events run all year in many countries; participants can run competitively (timed, heats) or for fun (untimed waves) (Warrior Dash, n.d.). The course is about 5 km with 12+ obstacles including mud pits and wall climbs; water obstacles are common (Wikipedia, n.d.).

Rules

Rules: participants must complete the course, may skip obstacles with a penalty (in competitive waves), and must wear the bib; safety rules require medical stations and obstacle inspection; helmets are recommended at some obstacles (Warrior Dash, n.d.). Age groups from kids’ dashes to elite waves; men and women run in separate competitive waves and mixed fun waves.

History

Warrior Dash was founded in 2011 by former US military officers and held its first race in 2012 at the Catamount Ski Resort in Massachusetts; it grew to millions of participants worldwide (Wikipedia, n.d.). The OCR boom of the 2010s also created Spartan Race (2010), Tough Mudder (2010) and the OCR World Championships (2018, Chicago) (Wikipedia, n.d.).
