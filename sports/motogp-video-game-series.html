MotoGP (video game series)

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

The official MotoGP video game (Milestone, e.g., MotoGP 25) carries the MotoGP esports World Championship (eMotoGP), with qualifiers on time attack and race events on current MotoGP circuits and bikes (Wikipedia, n.d.; Liquipedia, n.d.) (Milestone / Dorna, n.d.). The 2026 eMotoGP season runs on the current MotoGP title with a world championship finale.

Figure: ™/®MotoGP Sports Entertainment Group/Fédération Internationale de Motocyclisme, designed by Pentagram. (2024). MotoGP logo (2024) [Logo]. Wikimedia Commons. Public domain. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MotoGP_logo_(2024).svg

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are F1 (video game series), WRC (video game series), Gran Turismo, Forza, iRacing, Assetto Corsa and Zwift; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Rules

eMotoGP rules: time-attack qualifying decides the grid, then race events on official MotoGP circuits with the current bikes; points per race decide the world championship; open to all genders (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

MotoGP's game esport began in the 2000s with community cups; the official eMotoGP World Championship has run since the 2010s under Milestone and Dorna, with world finals at MotoGP events (e.g., Valencia) (Wikipedia, n.d.).
