Gomoku

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Gomoku ('five in a row') is played on a 15x15 go board (intersections): players alternate placing black/white stones; the first to make an unbroken line of exactly five (freestyle: five or more) wins. The Renju International Federation (RIF) organizes world championships since 1989. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Go, Renju, Omok; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Renju International Federation, n.d.).

Figure: Fabi Fuu 76. (2025-02-02). Gomoku professional opening (RenjuNote) [Diagram]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY 4.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gomoku_Pro_Opening_(RenjuNote).png

Rules

15x15 board, black moves first; overline (six or more) wins in freestyle gomoku, counts as a foul for black in renju; no handicap in official play. World championships (individual and team) are held by the RIF (Renju International Federation, n.d.).

History

Gomoku is documented in China (wuziqi) from the 17th century and in Japan from the Edo period; the RIF was founded in 1989 (Tournament: World Gomoku Championship). RenjuNote records professional openings (2025) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
