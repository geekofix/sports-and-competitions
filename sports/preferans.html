Preferans

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Preferans (preference) is a Russian three-player trick-taking bidding game with a 32-card deck (7–A): players bid the number of tricks they will take in a declared trump (or no-trump); the highest bidder plays the 'contract'; scoring is zero-sum among the three. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Durak, Belote, Whist; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pagat.com, n.d.).

Figure: Božidar L. (2014-01-30). A preferans game record [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Preferans_zápis.jpg

Rules

32 cards (7–A); 10 cards each + 2 in the talon (preferans) which the bidder takes and discards 2; bidding from 6 tricks up; the contract player must make the bid; 'misère' (no-trump— 'bez kozir') bids exist; penalties for failing (Pagat.com, n.d.).

History

Preferans was invented in Russia in the 19th century (mid-1800s, St. Petersburg origins) and became the dominant Soviet/Russian game; the 2014 'Preferans zázn—' photo shows a game record (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
