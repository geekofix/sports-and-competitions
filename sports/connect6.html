Connect6

≈ 1 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Connect6 is a connection game invented in 2008 by Professor I-Chen Wu at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, Taiwan: black places one stone on the first move and two stones on every later move; the first to connect six wins on a 19x19 board. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Gomoku, Pente, Go; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

Figure: Nelson Pavlosky. (2007-01-18). A Connect6 game on a large board [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 2.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Closeup_of_a_Connect_6_game.jpg

Rules

19x19 go board; first move one stone (black), then two stones per turn alternating; six or more in a row wins. World championships are held (World Connect6 Championship) (Wu, I.-C, n.d.).

History

Designed in 2008 in Taiwan to balance the first-move advantage of gomoku/renju by giving the second player two stones per move; the Connect6 world championship is organized since 2009 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
