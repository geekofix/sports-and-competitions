Baseball5

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Baseball5 is the street, urban version of baseball played with a rubber ball on a small diamond with five players per team and no gloves. It is governed by the World Baseball Softball Confederation (WBSC), which runs the Baseball5 World Cup, and was a Youth Olympic sport at Buenos Aires 2018 and Paris 2024.

Rules

Under WBSC Baseball5 rules, teams of five (mixed-gender in international play) play on a diamond with bases 13 m apart (12 m in some age groups). The batter hits the ball with the hand (a flat palm) instead of a bat; a hit must land in fair territory before the fielders can get the batter out. There are no gloves or mitts — fielders catch with bare hands. Innings last until each team has made three outs, or one minute, whichever comes first. At the World Cup and Youth Olympics, games are best of three or five innings.

History

Baseball5 was developed by the WBSC in the 2010s to bring baseball to streets, schools and beaches, especially in urban areas and developing countries. It debuted at the Youth Olympic Games in Buenos Aires 2018, where mixed national teams competed, and again at Paris 2024. The first Baseball5 World Cup was held in 2019, and the format is now played in over 80 countries.
