Dou Dizhu

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Dou dizhu ('Fight the Landlord') is the most played card game in China: three players with a 54-card deck; one 'landlord' (with 3 extra cards) against two 'peasants'; climbing game — play higher combinations (singles, pairs, triples, straights, bombs) to empty your hand first. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Rummy, Canasta, Uno; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pagat.com, n.d.).

Figure: ChokSien Hiew. (2008-12-15). The bomb cards of dou dizhu [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dou_dizhu_bombs.jpg

Rules

54 cards (incl. 2 jokers); 17 each + 3 landlord cards; landlord plays first; must beat the last play with a higher combination of the same type (or a bomb/four-with-two); first side to empty hand wins; bombs (four of a kind) and rocket (both jokers) are highest (Pagat.com, n.d.).

History

Dou dizhu was invented in Hubei, China in the late 1970s–80s in Hubei and exploded with online play (QQ Games, 2000s); the 2008 'Dou dizhu bombs' photo shows the bomb cards; street play in Nanjing is photographed (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
