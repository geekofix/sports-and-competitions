Half Marathon

≈ 4 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

The half marathon is the road race over 21.0975 km (13.1094 miles) — half the marathon distance — usually run on city streets alongside the marathon, governed and record-ratified by World Athletics (World Athletics, n.d.-e). The world records are 56:51 for men, set by Yomif Kejelcha of Ethiopia at the 2026 Buenos Aires Half Marathon, and 1:02:52 for women, set by Letesenbet Gidey of Ethiopia in Valencia in 2021; since 2011 World Athletics also keeps a women-only record, 1:05:16 by Peres Jepchirchir of Kenya (Gdynia 2020) (World Athletics, n.d.-b; World Athletics, n.d.-d). At the end of the 2026 season the women's race was led by Gidey (pictured) and Fotyen Tesfay, the 2026 Buenos Aires winner in 1:03:57, while the men's event featured Kejelcha (pictured), Jacob Kiplimo of Uganda — whose 56:42 in Barcelona 2025 was not ratified because of non-compliant pacing — and Kibiwott Kandie (World Athletics, n.d.-b; World Athletics, n.d.-d).

Figure: van Leeuwen, E. (2022, July 16). Letesenbet Gidey of Ethiopia at the 2022 World Championships in Oregon [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. GFDL. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Letesenbet_Gidey_Oregon_2022.jpg

Together with Athletics, this discipline belongs to the road-racing family — Marathon, Ultramarathon and 20km Race Walk — and the full list of athletics disciplines is under Important Links.

Rules

Road races are held on a certified, accurately measured course (Jones counter) with a start and finish area, water and refreshment stations at regular intervals, and electronic chip timing; pacemakers are allowed under strict World Athletics rules — performances with non-compliant pacing are not ratified as records, as with Kiplimo's 56:42 in 2025 (World Athletics, n.d.-a; World Athletics, n.d.-b). World records on the road are ratified by World Athletics; the top elite races carry World Athletics Label status, the World Athletics Half Marathon Championships crown world champions, and the Abbott World Marathon Majors include the leading half marathons (World Athletics, n.d.-c; World Athletics, n.d.-e). Men and women race separately or together; mixed-race and women-only records are kept separately, and age-group and wheelchair categories are offered at major races (World Athletics, n.d.-a).

History

The first recognized half marathon world records date to the 1960s — Ron Hill of England ran 65:44 in 1965 — and Moses Tanui of Kenya became the first man under one hour in 1993 with 59:47 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Brigid Kosgei ran 1:04:28 at the 2019 Great North Run, but the downhill course was not eligible for records; the women's record has stood since Gidey's 1:02:52 in Valencia 2021, and the men's record fell repeatedly in 2024-2026, ending with Kejelcha's 56:51 in Buenos Aires (pictured) (Wikipedia, n.d.; World Athletics, n.d.-b).
