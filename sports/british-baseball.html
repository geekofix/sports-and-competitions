British Baseball

≈ 6 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

British baseball is a bat-and-ball team sport played in Great Britain and Ireland, closely related to rounders: instead of a round bat and an overarm bowl, it uses a flat bat and an underarm bowl, and the four bases (posts) are arranged in a diamond; the batting team scores runs by completing circuits of the bases while the fielding team tries to record three outs. The national governing body is the British Baseball Federation (BBF), which has run the National Baseball League (NBL) and the national championships since 1987, and internationally the sport is governed by the World Baseball Softball Confederation (WBSC) (British Baseball Federation [BBF], n.d.) (World Baseball Softball Confederation [WBSC], n.d.-a). In the 2026 NBL season the London Mets were crowned national champions, and the Summer Cup was held at Farnham Park on August 29–30, 2026 (BBF, n.d.). Great Britain also competed at the 2026 World Baseball Classic in March 2026, finishing fourth in its bracket as Venezuela won the title, and fielded age-group national teams at the 2026 WBSC U23 World Cup in Managua, the U-18 European Championship in Trieste (July 13–19, 2026), the U-12 European Championship in Évry (July 7–11, 2026, where Great Britain finished eighth as Germany won) and the Women's Baseball World Cup qualifiers in Tainan (August 23–27, 2026) (WBSC, n.d.-a) (WBSC, n.d.-b) (World Baseball Softball Confederation, n.d.-a) (World Baseball Softball Confederation, n.d.-b).

On the women's side, Great Britain's women's national team — coached by Pietro Sollecito — took part in the 2026 WBSC Women's Baseball World Cup qualifiers in Tainan, Taiwan, in August 2026, continuing the rapid growth of women's baseball in Britain, where the women's league and GB women's national team are organized by the BBF alongside the men's programme (WBSC, n.d.-a) (BBF, n.d.).

Rules

Under the rules used by the British Baseball Federation and WBSC-affiliated play, a team fields nine players; the game lasts five innings at senior level (shorter at youth levels), and each innings both teams bat and field, with three outs ending a team's turn at bat. The bowler delivers the ball underarm; the batter stands at home post with a flat-sided bat and attempts to hit the ball into fair territory, then runs around the four posts; a run is scored when a batter legally touches all four posts and returns to home. Stealing bases is not allowed, and runners must wait for the ball to be hit before leaving their post. Fielders record outs by catching a batted ball before it bounces, tagging a runner with the ball, or touching a post before the runner arrives (a force out) (BBF, n.d.).

Equipment and field: the ball is a hard leather-covered ball similar to a softball; the bat is flat (rectangular in cross-section) rather than round; bases are flat posts or bags; fielders wear a fielding glove on the non-throwing hand at some levels. Fouls include throwing the bat, obstructing runners, bowling above the shoulder or below the knee (a "no ball"), and batting out of turn; penalty is a free hit or an out depending on the rule set. Men's and women's games use the same rules, with women's and girls' competitions at WBSC level often played with a slightly smaller ball; youth categories (U12, U15, U18, U23) shorten innings and adjust pitching distance (WBSC, n.d.-a). Formats include league play (NBL in Britain, April–September with a "Final 4s" championship weekend), cups (Summer Cup), and international tournaments — World Baseball Classic for national teams and age-group world cups and European championships under the WBSC (BBF, n.d.) (WBSC, n.d.-a).

History

British baseball grew out of rounders, the traditional English bat-and-ball game that was codified in the 19th century and survives today as GAA rounders in Ireland and as playground rounders in England. British baseball developed its own identity in the United Kingdom — flat bat, underarm bowl, four posts, no stealing — and the British Baseball Federation was established in 1987 to govern the NBL and national teams (BBF, n.d.). The related Irish game, GAA rounders, played by clubs such as the Wolfe Tones of Antrim, remains hugely popular within the Gaelic Athletic Association, and British baseball clubs maintain close ties with rounders and with baseball and softball communities abroad (Gaelic Athletic Association [GAA], n.d.). Great Britain first appeared at the World Baseball Classic in 2026, marking the sport's arrival on the world stage for the British national team, while women's baseball — with the WBSC Women's Baseball World Cup since 2004 — has become a major growth area, with GB women competing at the 2026 qualifiers in Tainan (WBSC, n.d.-a) (WBSC, n.d.-b).
