FIFA (video game series)

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

The FIFA video-game series (EA Sports FC since 2023, e.g., EA SPORTS FC 26 in 2026) is the world's biggest football simulation; its esport is the FIFAe World Cup (national teams, 1v1) and the FIFAe Club World Cup (pro clubs), run by EA and the FIFAe Nations Cup series (Wikipedia, n.d.) (EA Sports, n.d.). The 2026 FIFAe Club World Cup and Nations Cup continue the annual cycle, and the games also feature at the Esports World Cup (EA Sports FC 26 was in the EWC 2026 Paris lineup) (Wikipedia, n.d.).

Figure: Electronic Arts. (2024-07-25). EA FC 25 logo (EA SPORTS FC series branding) [Logo]. Wikimedia Commons. Public domain. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:EA_FC_25_logo.webp

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are eFootball, NBA 2K, Madden NFL, MLB The Show, Rocket League and E-Sports; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Rules

FIFAe rules: 1v1 brackets for the World Cup and pro-club team events; matches on the current game with standard competitive settings (short in-game halves), Ultimate Team/FUT modes excluded — competitive uses standard squads; best-of-3/5 series; men and women compete in open qualifiers, with women's FIFAe events alongside (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

FIFA's esport began with the FIFA Interactive World Cup (2004–2015), replaced in 2016 by the FIFAe World Cup; the series was renamed EA Sports FC in 2023 after the FIFA license ended, with the FIFAe competitions continuing under EA (Wikipedia, n.d.).
