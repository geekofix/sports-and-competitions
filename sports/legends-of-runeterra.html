Legends of Runeterra

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Legends of Runeterra (LoR) is Riot Games' 2020 digital card game set in the League of Legends universe; its esport ran the seasonal world championship (2022–2023) before Riot paused organized play in 2024 (Wikipedia, n.d.; Liquipedia, n.d.) (Riot Games, n.d.). Matches are best-of-3 with a two-deck ban phase; open to all genders.

Figure: White Demon. (2024-09-07). Legends of Runeterra card (Azerbaijani dissection art) [Illustration]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 4.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Legends_of_Runeterra_card_disection_az.png

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are Hearthstone, Magic: The Gathering Arena, Gwent, Yu-Gi-Oh (video game), Pokémon Trading Card Game Live, League of Legends and E-Sports; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Rules

LoR rules: two decks of 40 cards, one banned per match by the opponent; best-of-3 matches with the spell-mana system; open brackets (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

Riot launched LoR in open beta in January 2020 as the card-game companion to League of Legends; the first world championship was held in 2022 before Riot wound down the organized circuit (Wikipedia, n.d.).
