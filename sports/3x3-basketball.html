3x3 Basketball

≈ 4 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

3x3 basketball (officially FIBA 3x3) is played on a half court with one basket: teams consist of four players, three on court and one substitute. FIBA formalized the format in 2010, it debuted at the 2010 Youth Olympic Games in Singapore, and it became a full Olympic medal sport at Tokyo 2020. The FIBA 3x3 World Cup is the flagship event of the discipline (FIBA, n.d.-a). See also Basketball, 3x3 Wheelchair Basketball and Wheelchair Basketball on this site.

Among the teams shaping the game today are the United States women, who won a record fourth FIBA 3x3 World Cup title in 2026 with Mikaylah Williams named tournament MVP, and the German women's national team (Luana Greinacher, Sonja Brunckhorst, Marie Reichert, Svenja Mevius), Olympic bronze medalists at Paris 2024 — pictured below at Die Finals 2025 in Dresden — alongside the Latvian men, who claimed their first World Cup title in 2026 (FIBA, n.d.-b; FIBA, n.d.-c).

Rules

Under the official FIBA 3x3 rules the shot clock is 12 seconds. A game lasts 10 minutes of playing time or ends as soon as one team reaches 21 points, whichever comes first; if the game is tied at the end of regulation, overtime is played to first 2 points (FIBA, n.d.-a). Shots from inside the arc count 1 point, shots from beyond the arc count 2 points. After a defensive rebound or a steal, the ball must be taken behind the arc before a shot attempt; after a made basket, play resumes directly without a check-ball. Team fouls 6 through 9 award the opponent two free throws each; from the 10th team foul the opponent receives two free throws plus possession. Substitutions are allowed before free throws and during timeouts, and each team may take one timeout per game. Men and women play the identical rule set — the only difference between the Olympic tournaments is the competition category — and FIBA runs separate men's and women's World Cups and rankings (FIBA, n.d.-b). Streetball played in parks often uses house rules — games to 11 or 15 points, "1s and 2s", no free throws — so local formats vary widely.

History

FIBA debuted 3x3 at the 2010 Youth Olympic Games in Singapore and held the first FIBA 3x3 World Championship in Athens in 2012, where the Serbian men's team began a long era of dominance at World Cup level (FIBA, n.d.-c).
