Chomp

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Chomp is the mathematical 'poisoned chocolate' game: players take turns choosing a square of a rectangular chocolate bar and 'eating' it plus everything below and to the right; the player forced to eat the poisoned top-left square loses. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Nim, Wythoff's Game, Sprouts; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Gale, D, 1974).

Figure: Lord Belbury. (2020-01-28). A chomp position diagram [Diagram]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 4.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chomp_game.png

Rules

m x n grid; a move removes the chosen cell and all cells below/right; the (1,1) cell is poisoned; normal play (loser = eats poison); the first-player win is proven by strategy-stealing (no explicit strategy known for all sizes) (Gale, D, 1974).

History

Invented by David Gale in the 1970s ; the chomp position diagram shows a move; the Australian chomp candy bar photo (Evan-Amos, CC0) shows the chocolate metaphor (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
