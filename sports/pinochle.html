Pinochle

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Pinochle is a melding-and-trick-taking game played with a special 48-card double pinochle deck (A,10,K,Q,J of each suit, twice): players bid, then meld runs/marriages/aces and take tricks; it has been popular in the USA since the 1880s. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Euchre, Whist, Sheepshead; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pagat.com, n.d.).

Figure: — AM K 1211 t a lk!. (2008-08-01). Pinochle meld cards [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pinochle_meld.jpg

Rules

48-card deck (two of each A-10-K-Q-J); bidding in points; melds: run (A-10-K-Q-J of trump = 150), royal marriage (K-Q of trump = 4), pinochle (J♦ Q♠ = 4), aces around (8), etc.; trick-taking with trump; game to 50— partnership or solo; the 1914 'Indoor Sports — Pinochle' cartoon shows its popularity (Pagat.com, n.d.).

History

Invented in the 1880s in the US Midwest (attributed to a German immigrant in Milwaukee); the double deck was introduced in the early 1900s; the Pinochle meld photo (2008) documents the cards (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
