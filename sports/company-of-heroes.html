Company of Heroes

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Company of Heroes is Relic Entertainment's World War II real-time tactics series, played 1v1 to 4v4 with resource points (manpower, fuel, munitions), unit veterancy and map control; Company of Heroes 3 (2023) carries the active competitive scene with world championships and a Gamescom presence (Wikipedia, n.d.) (Liquipedia, n.d.). Matches are best-of-3/5 on the current patch with faction and commander bans; open to all genders.

Figure: M. Adrian Jurhami. (2026-04-10 17:27:18). 8.8 cm Flak from Company of Heroes 3 (in-game unit art) [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY 4.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cobi_8,8_Flak_from_Company_of_Heroes_3.jpg

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are Age of Empires, Warcraft III, Starcraft II and E-Sports; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Rules

CoH3 rules: victory points held on the map decide the match; destroying the enemy base is an alternative win; commanders (doctrines) are drafted or banned by the ruleset; fuel and munitions control tech tiers; standard settings lock, no mods; best-of series; pausing is disabled in tournaments (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

The series began in 2006 (Company of Heroes, Relic/THQ), became the leading WWII RTS esport in the late 2000s with big LANs, and CoH 3's 2023 release revived the scene with a 2022 Gamescom exhibition and a world championship circuit (Wikipedia, n.d.).
