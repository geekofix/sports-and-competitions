Outrigger Canoeing

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Outrigger canoeing (va'a paddling) is the racing of outrigger canoes, slender hulls stabilized by a float (ama) connected by booms (iako), crewed by one to twelve paddlers. The international classes are OC1, OC2, OC4 and OC6, a six-person single-hull canoe with a rudder steered from seat 6, and OC12, a double-hull twelve-person boat. The sport is governed worldwide by the International Va'a Federation (IVF), founded in 1986, and has been part of the Pacific Games since 1995. It is closely related to flatwater canoeing, kayaking and canoe sprint.

Rules

In OC6 sprint racing, six paddlers race 500 m, 1,000 m or 2,000 m courses with turns around buoys, while distance events run up to 42 km, the classic Hamilton Cup in Australia. The steerer in seat 6 controls the rudder and calls the strokes; in changeover races paddlers may be substituted. Para Va'a, added as a full medal discipline at the 2008 World Sprints in Sacramento, follows the same rules for athletes with impairments. Since 1990 the IVF has mandated a standard OC6 hull based on the Tahitian Galaide II design, so all crews race identical boats. Races are decided by electronic timing at the finish line.

History

Outrigger canoes are the traditional voyaging craft of Polynesia; va'a races in the lagoon played an important role in Tahiti's Heiva festival from the early 19th century, and organized racing emerged in Hawaii around the turn of the 20th century with clubs such as Hui Nalu and the Outrigger Canoe Club, founded in 1908 and still active today. The IVF organizes the World Sprint and World Distance Championships, and the sport has been contested at the Pacific Games since the 1995 edition in Papeete, Tahiti, which has dominated the medal count at every edition. In Australia, the Hamilton Island Cup, first staged in 1984, grew into the country's premier regatta with a signature 42 km marathon. ICF canoe ocean racing uses similar va'a hulls, and OC1 has been discussed for inclusion in ICF World Cup events.
