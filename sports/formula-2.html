Formula 2

≈ 4 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

The FIA Formula 2 Championship is the FIA's premier single-seater feeder series directly below Formula One, contested by two-car teams on the support program of most Formula 1 Grands Prix; the 2026 season is the 10th under the F2 name, running from March 7 in Melbourne to December 6 in Abu Dhabi over 14 rounds, with all cars built by Dallara (F2 2024 chassis) and powered by a Mecachrome 3.4-litre turbocharged V6 on Pirelli tyres (FIA, n.d.-b; FIA, n.d.-a). The Bahrain and Jeddah rounds were cancelled because of the regional conflict and replaced by the championship's first North American rounds in Miami and Montreal, while Baku was "supersized" with two feature races (FIA, n.d.-a). As of November 22, 2026, Rafael Câmara leads the drivers' standings by 7 points over Nikola Tsolov, with Alex Dunne still in the fight, and the Lusail (November 28–29) and Abu Dhabi (December 5–6) rounds remain; Câmara has already been confirmed as a Haas F1 driver for 2027 (FIA, n.d.-a). The reigning teams' champion is Invicta, and 2024 champion Gabriel Bortoleto graduated to Formula 1 (FIA, n.d.-a) (FIA, n.d.-b).

On the women's side of the feeder ladder, the all-female F1 Academy — run as an F1 support series since 2023 — is where the leading young women race, and its graduates are the most visible female talents on the path to Formula 1 and Formula 2 (F1 Academy, n.d.).

Rules

The FIA Formula 2 Sporting Regulations 2026 govern the championship (FIA, n.d.-c). Each weekend comprises free practice on Friday, a sprint race on Saturday and the feature race on Sunday; the sprint uses a partially reversed top-10 grid from qualifying, runs about 120 km (or 45 minutes), and awards points 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1, while the feature race runs about 170 km (or 60 minutes) with Formula 1-style points 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1; in both races 2 points are added for pole position and 1 for the fastest lap (FIA, n.d.-c). All teams use identical Dallara chassis, Mecachrome engines and Pirelli tyres, so results depend on driver skill; DRS is used as in F1. The drivers' champion must hold a FIA Super Licence, which requires 40 Super Licence points — the F2 champion earns exactly 40 (FIA, n.d.-c). Alongside the overall classification there is a rookie classification (the Anthoine Hubert Trophy), and teams compete for the teams' championship; men and women are eligible — the women racing today do so mainly through the F1 Academy feeder — and the minimum age for a Super Licence is 18 (FIA, n.d.-c).

History

The championship traces its lineage through the European Formula Two championship (1967–1984), the Formula 3000 era (1985–2004) and the GP2 Series (2005–2016), which was renamed the FIA Formula 2 Championship in 2017 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Champions of the GP2/F2 era who went on to star in Formula 1 include Nico Rosberg (2005), Lewis Hamilton (2006), Timo Glock (2007), Nico Hülkenberg (2009), Romain Grosjean (2011), Charles Leclerc (2017), George Russell (2018), Nyck de Vries (2019), Mick Schumacher (2020), Oscar Piastri (2021), Felipe Drugovich (2022), Théo Pourchaire (2023) and Gabriel Bortoleto (2024) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The series' most famous graduate is Lewis Hamilton, the 2006 GP2 champion, and 2026's title fight between Rafael Câmara and Nikola Tsolov continues that tradition of producing future F1 stars (FIA, n.d.-a; Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
