Tenjiku Shogi

≈ 1 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Tenjiku shogi ('astronomical shogi') is the largest historical shogi variant on a 16x16 board, named after the tenjiku (heavenly) theme of its pieces; it is played today by enthusiasts. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Shogi, Dai Shogi; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

Figure: Jmredford. (2022-02-15). A handmade tenjiku shogi set, 2022 [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 4.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tenjiku_shogi_set.jpg

Rules

16x16 board with astronomical-themed pieces and complex movement; drops as in shogi; mate wins (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

History

Tenjiku shogi represents the peak of the shogi-variant tradition before the 16th-century standardization on the 9x9 board; modern handmade sets (e.g. the 2022 set by John Redford of Austin, Texas) reconstruct it (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
