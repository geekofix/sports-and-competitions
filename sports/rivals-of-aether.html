Rivals of Aether

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Rivals of Aether is Aether Studios' 2017 indie platform fighter inspired by Smash, with workshop characters; Rivals 2 (2024) is the sequel (Aether Studios, n.d.). Its circuit runs at Genesis, Riptide and — plus the Aether — (Wikipedia, n.d.). Best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 finals); open to all genders.

Figure: Bruce Liu. (11 October 2015, 17:27:15). Esports world championship stage (2015 LoL Worlds group stage) [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2015_League_of_Legends_World_Championship_group_stage_(124787029).jpg

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are Super Smash Bros, Brawlhalla, MultiVersus and the other platform fighters; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Rules

Competitive rules: 1v1, stocks — lives, blast zones, best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 grand finals), stage bans; ranked and open brackets; all genders (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

Rivals of Aether launched in 2017 (early access 2015) by Aether Studios (former ; it became a Genesis/Riptide staple, and Rivals 2 launched in 2024 (Wikipedia, n.d.).
