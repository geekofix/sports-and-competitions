Fistball

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Fistball (German: Faustball) is a team ball sport played on a 50 m x 20 m court (outdoors, on grass) or in a hall, in which two teams of five players hit a ball over a net (2 m high at the center) using only the fist or forearm. It is governed by the International Fistball Association (IFA), which organizes the Fistball World Championships (men since 1968, women since 1994), the annual IFA Fistball World Tour for club teams and continental championships.

Rules

At IFA World Championship level, each team has five players on a 50 x 20 m field with a net 2.00-2.10 m high at the center (lower for youth/women in some categories). The ball (circumference 65-68 cm, weight 340-380 g) is served by one player throwing it up and striking it over the net with a closed fist or forearm; it may bounce once on the opponent's side before being played, and each team may touch the ball up to three times (block at the net counts as a touch). A point is scored when the ball lands in the opponent's court, goes out, or the opponent faults (more than three touches, carrying, net touch, double hit). Matches are played best-of-five sets to 11 points (win by two, capped at 15). Players may be substituted, and line judges and a referee enforce the IFA Laws of the Game. Indoor fistball (on a 40 x 20 m court with a 2 m net) follows similar rules under the IFA.

History

Fistball developed from Italian ball games of the 16th century (gioco del pallone) and spread to Germany in the 19th century, where it became organized within the German gymnastics movement; the first German championship was held in 1896. The International Fistball Association (IFA) was founded in 1960, the first men's World Championship was held in 1968 in Linz, Austria (won by West Germany), and the first women's World Championship followed in 1994 in Buenos Aires. Germany, Austria, Switzerland and Brazil dominate internationally, with Brazil the leading non-European nation (multiple men's world titles since 1990). Fistball was a demonstration sport at the 1936 Berlin Olympics. Stars include German national team players of the 1990s-2000s and Brazilian champions such as the 2018 world champions.
