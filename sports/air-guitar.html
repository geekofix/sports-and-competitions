Air Guitar

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Air Guitar is a performance competition in which contestants mime playing an invisible electric guitar to a song, judged on technique ("airness"), stage presence and originality. The Air Guitar World Championships have been held annually in Oulu, Finland, since 1996.

Rules

Each competitor performs a 60-second routine to a song of their choice, miming all the moves of a real guitarist — solos, chords, headbanging. A panel of judges scores airness (technique and believability), stage presence and originality; the highest total advances and wins. Props, costumes and pyrotechnics are encouraged but the "instrument" must stay invisible. Variants: national qualifiers exist in many countries, and some events add a "wild card" freestyle round.

History

The first Air Guitar World Championship was held in Oulu, Finland, in 1996, inspired by the city's rock culture and the idea of making air guitar "a real competition". It has been held annually in Oulu ever since and has been covered by international media; notable champions include Gareth "The Reverend" Icke and others from the air guitar community.
