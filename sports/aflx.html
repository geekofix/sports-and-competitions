AFLX

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

AFLX was the Australian Football League's short-format, entertainment-focused competition, played from 2018 to 2019. Teams of six players per side played on a reduced field with short quarters, aiming to make the game faster and more spectacular for new audiences.

Rules

AFLX used a modified version of the AFL laws: six players on the field per side, a smaller oval, four short quarters, and quicker restarts after goals. Tackling and marking remained, but the compressed field produced higher scoring and more open play than the traditional game.

History

The AFL launched AFLX in 2018 with a pre-season tournament and a series of regular-season rounds, but discontinued it after the 2019 season for cost and scheduling reasons. It remains a notable experiment in shortening the game for broadcast audiences.
