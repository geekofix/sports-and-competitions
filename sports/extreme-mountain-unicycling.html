Extreme Mountain Unicycling

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Extreme mountain unicycling (MUni) is the off-road discipline of unicycling in which riders tackle steep, rocky downhill terrain, jumps and drops on a large-wheeled unicycle (typically 24-36 inch wheel) with no gears and no brakes, using body balance alone. It is governed by the International Unicycling Federation (IUF), which includes MUni at the biennial Unicon World Unicycling Championships alongside downhill racing and trials.

Rules

At IUF Unicon and national MUni competitions, events include downhill races (timed runs on a closed course with jumps and technical sections, fastest time wins), uphill races, trials (riding over obstacles and logs without putting a foot down, judged on style and clean runs) and freestyle. Protective gear is mandatory or strongly recommended: helmet, knee pads, elbow pads, gloves and often shin guards; the unicycle must have a securely attached seat and pedals with grip pins, and wheel size is typically 24-29 inch for technical terrain, up to 36 inch for racing. Riders start individually with intervals, and falls or foot-downs in trials cost points; in downhill, leaving the course or missing a gate disqualifies the run. The IUF Rulebook (section for MUni) governs competition formats and safety recommendations.

History

Mountain unicycling emerged in the late 1990s in North America and Europe when unicyclists began riding off-road trails on large wheels with reinforced frames and knobby tires; the discipline grew alongside mountain biking, with pioneers such as Kris Holm (Canada) riding extreme terrain. The IUF, founded in 1982 and based in New York, has included MUni at Unicon since the early 2000s, with the first dedicated MUni events at Unicon in the 2000s and dedicated downhill and trials competitions at recent Unicons (Unicon 22 in Steyr 2026 includes MUni). Events such as Coker Jam, muni fests and the MUni downhill at Unicon attract riders worldwide. The sport demands exceptional balance, core strength and courage, with drops of several meters at the elite level.
