Subbuteo

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Subbuteo ('flick football') is the English table game of the 1920s–50s: players flick disc-shaped counters (players) across a cloth pitch on a hinged board, trying to score goals; it had clubs and championships in the mid-20th century. Within the table and arcade games on this site, its closest relatives are Table Football, Table Football, Air Hockey; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

Figure: Sportingn. (2008). Subbuteo players (2008) [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. Public domain. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Subbuteo_playersP1010292.JPG

Rules

Cloth pitch on a board (~90x60 cm); 11 disc players per side (one is the goalkeeper); flick discs with a finger (via a cue); goals by getting the disc into the opponent's goal; matches timed (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

History

Invented in England in the 1920s (London), boomed in the 1940s–50s with the Subbuteo Association; the 2008 PD photo shows players; equipment photo (2010) shows the discs (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
