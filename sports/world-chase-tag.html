World Chase Tag

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

World Chase Tag (WCT) is the world's first competitive tag league: two teams of up to six players contest a match on the Quad — a 12 m × 12 m square arena packed with obstacles that athletes move over, under and through with parkour skills — in a series of one-on-one, 20-second chase duels (World Chase Tag, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.). It was founded in Britain in 2012 by Christian Devaux after a back-garden game of tag with his son Orlando; his brother Damien joined in 2013 and together they built the format, the Quad and the rules, turning Hyde Park meet-ups in London into international competitions (World Chase Tag, n.d.). Both male and female athletes compete together in a single unified league, with women's and youth athletes prominent on the world stage (Wikipedia, n.d.).

The first major World Chase Tag competition was held on 18 December 2016 at Trinity Buoy Wharf, London, and won by the Marrero Gang; WCT4 on 25 August 2019 at York Hall was the first televised event (Channel 4 in the UK, NBCSN in the US), and WCT USA in Atlanta in October 2020 — won by Apex — was the first event outside the UK (Wikipedia, n.d.). Today WCT is a professional league with 46+ licensed Quad venues across 18 countries, a ranked global circuit of national, continental and world championships, and broadcasts in 37+ territories with billions of online views (World Chase Tag, n.d.).

Rules

A match consists of 16 rounds between two teams of up to six players; in each round one member of each team is the chaser and the other the evader, starting at opposite sides of the 12 m × 12 m Quad (Wikipedia, n.d.). The chaser has 20 seconds to tag the evader with their hand; stepping a foot outside the Quad forfeits the round for that player, and holding the chaser or hiding motionless behind obstacles is not allowed, while excessive contact and dangerous play are penalised (World Chase Tag, n.d.). Points can only be scored by the evading team: a successful tag scores nothing and the tagged player becomes the evader in the next round, while surviving the full 20 seconds scores one point for the evader's team, which retains the evader role; rounds are separated by 25-second rests (Wikipedia, n.d.). The team with the most points after 16 rounds wins, and ties go to a sudden-death chase-off of paired rounds, the team with the longest evasion time prevailing (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

Christian Devaux created World Chase Tag in the UK in 2012 after a game of tag with his son in their back garden, and his brother Damien joined the organisation in 2013; together they experimented with Quad designs — developing an "EQ" difficulty calculation — to tune how evadable each arena was, before staging the first organised competitions in London's Hyde Park (SportsPro, n.d.; World Chase Tag, n.d.). The first major championship, WCT, took place on 18 December 2016 at Trinity Buoy Wharf and was won by the Marrero Gang; WCT2 followed in 2017 and WCT3 in 2018 at York Hall, also won by the Marrero Gang, with WCT Euro 19 in 2019 won by Blacklist (Wikipedia, n.d.). WCT4 on 25 August 2019 — won by United — was the first televised event, broadcast by Channel 4 and NBCSN, and WCT USA 2020 at the Coca-Cola Roxy in Atlanta, won by Apex, took the sport outside the UK as a six-part series (Wikipedia, n.d.). In 2020 Cary Glotzer and Tupelo Honey became equity partners, giving the league the backing to scale globally into today's ranked circuit of licensed venues, national and world championships (World Chase Tag, n.d.).
