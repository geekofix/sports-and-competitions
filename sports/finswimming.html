Finswimming

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Finswimming is the alternate English spelling of fin swimming — competitive swimming with fins, governed by CMAS (World Underwater Federation). This entry cross-references the main article: see Fin Swimming, which covers the surface, apnea and immersion disciplines, the CMAS World Championships and the rules in full.

Rules

The rules are those of CMAS finswimming: surface events (50-1600 m) with bi-fins or monofin, breath-hold apnea events, and SCUBA immersion events, with the 15 m surface rule after starts and turns and electronic timing. Full details are given in the Fin Swimming entry on this site.

History

Finswimming as a competitive sport dates to the 1950s-60s in the Soviet bloc and has been governed by CMAS since the 1960s, with World Championships since 1967 and a place on the World Games program since 1981. See Fin Swimming for the full history.
