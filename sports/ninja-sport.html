Ninja Sport

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Ninja sport (ninja-style obstacle course racing) is a competitive format in which athletes race against the clock over short courses with closely spaced technical obstacles — the warped wall, salmon ladder, balance elements, climbing and swinging structures — inspired by the Japanese TV show SASUKE (1997) and its international adaptations such as American Ninja Warrior. It is a discipline of the obstacle sports family alongside obstacle course racing, and was governed internationally by World Obstacle (FISO), whose structure has been integrating into the UIPM since 2026; ninja-style obstacle racing will also become the fifth discipline of modern pentathlon at Los Angeles 2028.

Rules

In Ninja Speed, the athlete runs a linear course of about 12 obstacles (FISO competition rules) from start line to finish line in the fastest possible time; falling or failing an obstacle ends the run or costs time depending on the ruleset. In league formats such as the World Ninja League and the Ultimate Ninja Athlete Association, courses have 10 to 20 obstacles in a predetermined order, athletes get one attempt per obstacle (except the warped wall), and scoring combines points for completed obstacles with the clock time — most points wins, fastest time breaks ties; UNAA allows a maximum of three fails per course. Courses must contain a mix of upper-body, lower-body and balance obstacles, and athletes compete in age divisions from children (as young as 6 in some leagues) to adults.

History

The format originated with SASUKE, first broadcast in Japan in 1997, where 100 competitors faced a four-stage obstacle course; the franchise was exported as Ninja Warrior and aired in more than 160 countries. Outside television, ninja gyms and leagues emerged in the 2010s: the National Ninja League (now World Ninja League) and the Ultimate Ninja Athlete Association were both founded in 2015, each running season-long series that culminate in world finals. World Obstacle (FISO), founded in Lausanne in 2014-2018 as the international federation for obstacle sports, recognized Ninja alongside OCR, gained GAISF observer status in 2021, and staged World Championships; in 2026 its members voted to integrate into the UIPM, which in May 2026 also agreed with SASUKE producer TBS to use ninja-style obstacles in the LA 2028 modern pentathlon event.
