E-Sports

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Esports (electronic sports) is organized, competitive video gaming at a professional level, with leagues and world championships for games such as League of Legends, Dota 2, Counter-Strike, Valorant and Fortnite. Globally it is coordinated by the International Esports Federation (IESF, founded 2008, 152 member federations), and the IOC has launched the Olympic Esports Games, with the inaugural edition in Riyadh in 2027.

Rules

Rules are game-specific: each title has its own official rule set set by the game publisher (e.g., Riot Games for League of Legends and Valorant, Valve for Dota 2 and Counter-Strike), covering match format (best-of-one/three/five), map veto procedures, agent/hero/champion drafts, pauses and technical issues. Tournament organizers layer competition regulations on top, including anti-doping (WADA-based where federations require), roster locks, and standardized hardware at LAN events. At the IESF World Esports Championship, national teams compete in a range of titles under IESF regulations; the 2026 edition is scheduled for Banten, Indonesia, in December 2026. The Olympic Esports Games will feature a curated selection of titles in a quadrennial event run with the IOC.

History

Competitive video gaming began with arcade tournaments in the 1970s-80s (e.g., Space Invaders championships in 1980) and grew with LAN parties and online play in the 1990s; StarCraft in South Korea became the first mass-televised esport in the late 1990s and early 2000s, with dedicated TV channels. The 2000s saw the rise of esports organizations (Fnatic, SK Telecom T1, Team SoloMid), prize-pool-funded tournaments (The International from 2011), and streaming platforms (Twitch, 2011). The IESF was founded in Daegu, South Korea, on 8 August 2008 and now runs annual World Esports Championships. In 2018, esports was a demonstration sport at the Asian Games, and in 2023 the IOC announced the Olympic Esports Week (Singapore) and then the Olympic Esports Games in Riyadh 2027, marking esports' arrival in the Olympic movement.
