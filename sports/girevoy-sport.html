Girevoy Sport

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Girevoy sport (kettlebell sport) is the competitive lifting of cast-iron kettlebells (giri) for maximum repetitions in a 10-minute set, in disciplines such as jerk (two bells to overhead), long cycle (clean and jerk) and snatch (one bell). The main international governing body is the International Union of Kettlebell Lifting (IUKL), which runs the IUKL Kettlebell Sport World Championship; the traditional Russian body is the Girevoy Sport Union (GSU). It is recognized in Russia as an official sport and has world championships in many countries.

Rules

Under IUKL rules, standard competition sets last 10 minutes; athletes perform as many valid repetitions as possible with the prescribed technique, and the kettlebell(s) may not be set down during the set (if the bell touches the platform, the set ends and completed reps count). Disciplines: Jerk (two kettlebells, men; one for women, lifted from chest to overhead lockout), Long Cycle (clean and jerk, two bells for men, one for women, with rack position allowed between reps), Snatch (one kettlebell, may switch hands once), Biathlon (jerk + snatch), and relays. Weight classes (e.g., 63-105+ kg for men at internationals) and bell weights (16-32 kg) are set by category; athletes wear a singlet, shoes and a weightlifting belt (allowed). Judges count reps and check technique; anti-doping follows WADA. Rank titles (MSIC, MS, etc.) are awarded by the IUKL/GSU.

History

Kettlebells (giri) originated as counterweights in Russian markets in the 18th century and became lifting tools in the 19th century; competitive girevoy sport was formalized in the USSR in the mid-20th century and recognized as an official sport in the 1980s. The International Union of Kettlebell Lifting (IUKL), established in the 1990s, has organized World Championships since then, with events worldwide. Legends include Sergey Rudnev and Ivan Denisov (multiple world champion). Russia, Ukraine, Kazakhstan and the Baltic states dominate the elite scene. Girevoy sport is part of the World Games program discussions and national multi-sport games.
