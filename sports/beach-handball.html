Beach Handball

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Beach handball is the sand-court variant of team handball, played by two teams of four players (three court players plus a goalkeeper) on a small pitch with double points for spectacular goals; it is governed by the International Handball Federation (International Handball Federation [IHF], n.d.-b). The 12th IHF Men's and Women's Beach Handball World Championships took place June 23–28, 2026 at Lake Jarun in Zagreb, Croatia, with 31 teams; in the women's final Argentina beat Denmark on a shoot-out — the third nation after Brazil and Germany to hold the world-championship, continental and World Games triple crown — coached by Leticia Brunati, while Spain took bronze 2–0 over Brazil with Jimena Laguna Contreras top scorer with 14 points in the match (IHF, n.d.-a). In the men's final Germany beat Brazil 2–0 under coach Marten Franke, completing Germany's own triple crown alongside the 2025 European title and the 2025 World Games title in Chengdu. The next World Championships are scheduled for 2028 in Salalah, Oman. Beach handball debuted at the Youth Olympic Games in Buenos Aires 2018 and returned in 2024 (International Olympic Committee [IOC], n.d.) Rules

Beach handball is played on a sand court 27 m × 12 m with a 3 m × 2 m goal and a 6 m goal-area semicircle; a match consists of two 10-minute halves (IHF, n.d.-b). Four players per team take the court — three court players and one specialist goalkeeper — and substitutions are unlimited and can be made at any time. Scoring follows handball principles with a beach twist: a normal goal counts one point, but a goal scored by the goalkeeper, by a specialist player, or with a spin shot counts two points. There is no offside; restarts are taken as throw-offs, free throws and penalty throws, and a tied match goes to overtime decided by golden goal. Men's and women's matches are played under identical rules, and youth editions at the Youth Olympic Games use the same format with under-18 squads (IHF, n.d.-b).

History

Beach handball grew out of indoor handball in the 1990s, with the first informal tournaments in Italy and the rules codified for international play in the early 2000s; the IHF staged the first men's and women's World Championships in 2004 and has run them every two years since, adding the sport to the Youth Olympic Games programme at Buenos Aires 2018 and again at Paris 2024 (IHF, n.d.-a). Brazil and Germany dominated the early women's editions, and the 2026 Zagreb championships confirmed Argentina as the third women's nation to complete the triple crown of world title, continental title and World Games title. The men's side has been led by Germany, whose 2026 world title under Marten Franke completed the same treble after the 2025 European championship and the 2025 World Games in Chengdu (IOC, n.d.).
