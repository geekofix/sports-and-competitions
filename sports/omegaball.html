Omegaball

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Omegaball is a three-team variant of association football played on a circular pitch with three goals, invented in the United States and first tested in 2020 in Oklahoma City before its public debut in Irvine, California, on 10 March 2022 (Wikipedia, n.d.). Three teams of five players each — four outfield players plus a goalkeeper — compete simultaneously for 39 minutes, and the team with the most goals at the final whistle wins; women's and men's teams compete under the same format in growing league play (Omegaball, n.d.). The inaugural OmegaBall Championship was broadcast on Fox Sports 2 and on ESPN's streaming channel ESPN8: The Ocho (Wikipedia, n.d.).

Rules

Omegaball is played on a circular field about 60 yards (roughly 68 m) in diameter — small enough to fit inside half of a standard soccer pitch — with three goals placed about 60 yards apart around the perimeter, each roughly 30 yards from the center (Omegaball, n.d.). Each team fields five players, four outfield and one goalkeeper, and defends one goal while attacking the other two; goals can be scored against either of the two opposing goals (Omegaball, n.d.). There is no offside rule and no throw-ins; out-of-play balls are restarted with free kicks, and standard football fouls are penalized (Omegaball, n.d.). A match lasts 39 minutes, divided into three 13-minute periods, and the team with the most goals wins; ties are broken by a penalty shootout in tournament play (Omegaball, n.d.). The official rules are published by Omegaball and apply equally to men's and women's divisions, with youth formats using smaller fields and fewer players (Omegaball, n.d.).

History

Omegaball was invented in the United States and first tested in 2020 in Oklahoma City, with the format refined so that three teams could attack and defend simultaneously without constant stoppages (Wikipedia, n.d.). The sport made its public debut in Irvine, California, on 10 March 2022, and the inaugural OmegaBall Championship was broadcast on Fox Sports 2 and ESPN8 (Wikipedia, n.d.). Since then, Omegaball has grown league play in the United States and internationally, with men's and women's teams competing and exhibition matches drawing former professional footballers (Wikipedia, n.d.).
