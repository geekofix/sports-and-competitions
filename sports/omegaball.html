Omegaball

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Omegaball is a three-team variant of association football (soccer) played on a circular pitch with three goals, invented in the United States and first tested in 2020 in Oklahoma City before its public debut in Irvine, California, on 10 March 2022. Three teams of five players each (four outfield players plus a goalkeeper) compete simultaneously for 39 minutes, and the team with the most goals at the final whistle wins. The inaugural OmegaBall Championship was broadcast on Fox Sports 2 and on ESPN's streaming channel ESPN8: The Ocho.

Rules

Omegaball is played on a circular field about 60 yards (roughly 68 m) in diameter, small enough to fit inside half of a standard soccer pitch, with three goals placed about 60 yards apart around the perimeter, each roughly 30 yards from the center. Each team fields five players, four outfield and one goalkeeper, and defends one goal while attacking the other two. There is no offside rule and no throw-ins; out-of-play balls are restarted with free kicks. A match lasts 39 minutes, divided into three 13-minute periods, and goals can be scored against either of the two opposing goals. The official rules are published on the league's website, and standard soccer rules apply where not modified.

History

Omegaball was invented by Anthony Dittmann, a former ESPN producer, together with a small group of partners, and was tested privately in Oklahoma City in 2020. The public debut took place on 10 March 2022 in Irvine, California, and the inaugural OmegaBall Championship was broadcast on Fox Sports 2 and ESPN8: The Ocho, giving the new sport national television exposure in the United States. The league continues to stage showcase events and youth programs, and the format has been promoted as a faster, three-team alternative to traditional soccer that can be played on fields half the size of a full soccer pitch.
