Gin Rummy

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Gin rummy is the two-player rummy variant invented in 1909 in New York (by Elwood T. Baker, ): players form melds and 'knock' when their deadwood (unmelded cards) is 10 or less; the opponent may 'lay off' on your melds. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Rummy, Canasta, Piquet; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pagat.com, n.d.).

Figure: Roland Scheicher / Roland Scheicher at German Wikipedia. (2000). A gin rummy card image [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. Public domain. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gin_Rummy.jpg

Rules

52-card deck + 2 jokers; 10 cards each; draw/discard; knock with deadwood ≤ 10; if the knocker's deadwood ≤ opponent's after layoff, knocker scores the difference; 'gin' (no deadwood) scores 25 bonus; 'big gin' rules (Pagat.com, n.d.).

History

Invented in 1909 in Brooklyn/New York by Elwood T. Baker ; it became a US classic; the PD 'Gin Rummy' card and the 1947 Bikini Atoll Navy photo show its spread (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
