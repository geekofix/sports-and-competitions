25m Rapid Fire Pistol Shooting

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

25m Rapid Fire Pistol is an ISSF shooting discipline: fast, timed strings at a turning target 25 meters away. It is one of the original Olympic shooting events, present since the first modern Games in Athens 1896, and is contested today as a men's Olympic event (women shoot the 25m pistol duel).

Rules

Under ISSF rules shooters use a .22 Long Rifle rimfire pistol at 25 m. The target carries five bullseyes in a row and rotates to face the shooter for each 5-shot string. The qualification is 60 shots in two stages of 30. The top eight advance to a final of four series of five shots, each string on a 4-second time limit, with the lowest scorer eliminated after each series. Variants: qualification string times (150/20/10-second stages) differ between rule editions and between ISSF and national events, so local competitions can feel quite different from the Olympic format.

History

Rapid fire pistol has been on the Olympic program since 1896. Germany's Ralf Schumann won three Olympic golds (1992, 1996, 2004), Christian Reitz won in London 2012, and Leuris Pupo of Cuba took gold at Rio 2016.
