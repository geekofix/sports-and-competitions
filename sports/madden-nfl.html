Madden NFL

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Madden NFL (EA Tiburon) is the NFL football simulation series; its top esport is the Madden Championship Series (MCS), played 1v1 on Ultimate Team (MUT) squads through online qualifiers into a knockout bracket and a championship event (EA, n.d.). The 2026 MCS runs on the current Madden title (Wikipedia, n.d.).

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are FIFA (video game series), eFootball, NBA 2K, MLB The Show, Rocket League and E-Sports; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Rules

MCS rules: 1v1 matches on Ultimate Team lineups under the league salary rules, with short in-game quarters; online qualifier phase, bracket playoffs and a championship LAN; best-of-1 in groups, best-of-3 in the bracket; disconnects are ruled losses; open to all genders (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

Madden's competitive scene grew from early 2000s tournaments to the official Madden Championship Series launched in the mid-2010s; EA runs the MCS with a multi-hundred-thousand-dollar prize pool, and the game has appeared at the Esports World Cup (Wikipedia, n.d.).
