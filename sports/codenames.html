Codenames

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Codenames (Vlaada Chvátil, 2015, Czech Games Edition) is the word-association party game for 2+ teams: spymasters give one-word clues + a number to identify their agents on a 5x5 grid; avoid the assassin. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Taboo, Mastermind, Avalon; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Czech Games Edition, n.d.).

Figure: JIP. (2015-11-07). A game of Codenames at a board-game festival [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Codenames_board_game.jpg

Rules

25 word cards (5x5); two spymasters know the key; clue = one word + number; guessing wrong can hit the opponent's agent or the assassin (instant loss); first team to find all their agents wins. World Codenames championship exists (Czech Games Edition, n.d.).

History

Designed by Vlaada Chvátil, published 2015 by Czech Games Edition; won the 2016 Spiel des Jahres; the Finland board-game-fair photos (JIP, 2015–2020) show play (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
