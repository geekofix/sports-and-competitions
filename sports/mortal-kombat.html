Mortal Kombat

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Mortal Kombat is NetherRealm Studios' 1992 fighting game famous for fatalities; its esport runs the MK Pro League — the MK Pro League / Kombat League and major LANs such as Combo Breaker and EVO, on the current title (Mortal Kombat 1, 2023) with the X launch tournament as the 2015 kickoff (Wikipedia, n.d.) (NetherRealm Studios, n.d.). Matches: 2-of-3 rounds, 99-second — standard fighting rules; best-of-3 sets, best-of-5 grand finals; open brackets, all genders.

Figure: NetherRealm Studios. (2015-04-15). Mortal Kombat X launch tournament screenshot [Screenshot]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY 3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mortal_Kombat_X_Launch_Tournament_screenshot.png

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are Street Fighter, Tekken, Guilty Gear, Soulcalibur, King of Fighters and the other fighting games; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Rules

Competitive rules: best-of-3 rounds of 99 seconds, best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 in grand finals), no items, stage select by veto; kameo — kameo assists in MK1; fatalities disabled in competitive; double elimination brackets at majors; open to all genders (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

Ed Boon and John Tobias created Mortal Kombat in 1992; it sparked the 1990s US Senate hearings on video-game violence and the creation of the ESRB; the esport grew from 2000s tournaments to the pro league era of the 2010s–2020s (Wikipedia, n.d.).
