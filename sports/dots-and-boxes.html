Dots and Boxes

≈ 1 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Dots and boxes (timbiriche in Latin America) is the paper game: players alternately draw a line between adjacent dots on a grid; completing the fourth side of a 1x1 box scores it and grants another turn. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Tic Tac Toe, Sprouts, Chomp; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Lucas, É, n.d.).

Figure: Anahí Iuê. (2022-11-27). A timbiriche (dots and boxes) game between an adult and a child [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 4.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Timbiriche_0.jpg

Rules

Grid of dots (5x5 typical); one line per turn; a completed box scores a point and an extra turn; most boxes at the end wins; the 'double-cross' strategy decides close games (Lucas, É, n.d.).

History

Published as a game by Édouard Lucas in the 1880s (France); known worldwide; the timbiriche photo (2022, Brazil) shows a game between an adult and a child (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
