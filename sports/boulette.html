Boulette

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Boulette is a traditional game of the Avesnes region (Nord, France) and Provence: played with a wooden ball (boulette) and cards — the boules-style throwing game with cards; the wooden boulettes of Avesnes are preserved. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Pétanque, Pétanque, Belote; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

Figure: Ancalagon. (2012-11-26). A wooden boulette of Avesnes [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Boulette_d'Avesnes_01.jpg

Rules

Played on a boule alley (boulodrome) with a small wooden ball and larger 'boulettes'; card variant assigns roles; regional rules of Avesnes/Provence; the Boulodrome at Lac du Bel Air (Priziac) shows the venue type (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

History

Boulette is documented in northern France (Avesnes) and Brittany/Provence from the 19th century; it combines boules throwing with card play in some versions (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
