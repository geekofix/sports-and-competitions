Connect Four

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Connect Four is the two-player connection game invented by Howard Wexler in 1974 and sold by Milton Bradley: players drop colored discs into a 7x6 vertical grid; first to connect four in a row (horizontal, vertical, diagonal) wins. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Tic Tac Toe, Hex, Gomoku; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

Figure: Popperipopp. (2008-04-11). The classic Connect Four board [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY 3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Connect_Four.jpg

Rules

7 columns x 6 rows vertical grid; gravity drop; four in a row of your color wins; if the grid fills without a line it's a draw. World championships (human and robot) exist (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

History

Patented by Howard Wexler in 1974; Milton Bradley (Hasbro) published it; the 2008 Connect Four photo documents the classic board (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
