Trontza

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Trontza is the Basque two-man crosscut-sawing contest: a pair saws through a log of set diameter with a large two-man saw (trontza); the fastest clean cut wins. It is a classic herri kirolak at lumberjack-style festivals and ties wood chopping (aizkolaritza) to team work. Within the Basque rural sports on this site, its closest relatives are Aizkolaritza, Segala, Harri Jasotzaile; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Euskal Herri Kirolak Elkartea, n.d.).

Rules

Both sawyers must pull full strokes on alternate ends; the log rests on trestles at fixed height; the cut must be complete and vertical within the marked tolerance. Timed heats, knockout final; safety glasses and gloves required. Mixed pairs compete at modern events (Euskal Herri Kirolak Elkartea, n.d.).

History

Two-man crosscut saws were the tool of Basque forestry for centuries; competitive sawing appears in 19th-century fair programmes, and the historic Wellcome photograph shows a man sawing from a log. Today trontza is run within the Herri Kirolak circuit alongside aizkolaritza (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
