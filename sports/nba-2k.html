NBA 2K

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

NBA 2K is 2K/Visual Concepts' NBA basketball simulation (annual releases); its esport is the NBA 2K League, a franchised league tied to NBA teams playing 5v5 matches with custom player builds in fixed positions (PG/SG/SF/PF/C), plus open community tournaments (NBA 2K League, n.d.). The 2026 season runs on the current NBA 2K title (Wikipedia, n.d.).

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are FIFA (video game series), eFootball, Madden NFL, MLB The Show, Rocket League and E-Sports; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Rules

NBA 2K League rules: 5v5 matches with league-set quarter length; team salary cap and draft; positions fixed (one each of PG, SG, SF, PF, C); custom player builds within league rules; regular season plus playoffs and a championship event; all genders may compete in open qualifiers (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

The NBA 2K League launched in 2018 as a joint NBA/2K venture with 17 NBA-affiliated teams (expanded since); it is the first esport league built around a traditional-sports property with real team branding, playing on the annual NBA 2K game (Wikipedia, n.d.).
