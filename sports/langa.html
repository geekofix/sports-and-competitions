Langa

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Langa is a traditional Nigerian hopping game played in rounds: one player per team, the Ruwa (point-scorer), hops on one foot while holding the other foot with one hand and tries to reach a designated area on the opponent's side to score; opponents may dislodge the held hand, eliminating the Ruwa for that round (Wikipedia, n.d.). Within the folk and festival games on this site, its closest relatives are Abula, Ampe, Asafo Atwele, Gidigbo, Kho Kho, Tag Rugby; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Figure: M Bash Ne. (2026-04-14). Langa traditional game, Nigeria — hopping on one foot [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 4.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Langa_Traditional_Game_4.jpg

Rules

Each round, teams nominate a Ruwa; all players hop on one foot holding the raised foot. The Ruwa must cross to the opponent's marked zone and back without being touched on the hand holding the foot; a dislodged hand ends the attempt. Points accumulate over rounds; the team with the most points wins. Played by children of both sexes in Nigerian schools and festivals (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

Langa is among the traditional Nigerian games preserved at the National Sports Festival; like abula it is a playground game with regional rule variants (Wikipedia, n.d.).
