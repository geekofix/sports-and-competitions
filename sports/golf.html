Golf

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Golf is a club-and-ball sport in which players hit a ball from a tee into a hole on a course of 9 or 18 holes, aiming to complete each hole in the fewest strokes. The Rules of Golf are maintained by The R&A (St Andrews) and the USGA, and the international amateur game is governed by the International Golf Federation (IGF). The four men's major championships are the Masters, PGA Championship, U.S. Open and The Open Championship; the premier team events are the Ryder Cup and Solheim Cup.

Rules

Under the Rules of Golf (2023 edition, updated every four years), each hole begins with a tee shot from the teeing ground; the ball is then played as it lies until holed. The player farthest from the hole plays first. Penalty strokes apply for lost balls (1 stroke + replay), out of bounds (1 + replay), water hazards (1 + drop options), unplayable lies (1 + options) and relief from abnormal course conditions (free relief in many cases). On the putting green, the flagstick may be left in (since 2019), and since 2019 a ball at rest on the edge of the hole may be treated as holed with one additional stroke. Handicap strokes are allocated by stroke index. Equipment rules (club length, ball size, groove rules) are set in the Equipment Rules.

History

Golf developed in 15th-century Scotland, with the first written rules at Brunsfield Links in Edinburgh (1744) and the Royal and Ancient Golf Club of St Andrews founded in 1754; the first Open Championship was played at Prestwick in 1860. The USGA was founded in 1894 and the first U.S. Open was 1895. Golf returned to the Olympics in Rio 2016 (last played 1904). The modern professional game is dominated by the PGA Tour (USA) and DP World Tour (Europe), with the Official World Golf Ranking. Legends include Harry Vardon, Bobby Jones (1930 Grand Slam), Ben Hogan, Jack Nicklaus (18 majors, record), Tiger Woods (15 majors), Annika Sörenstam and Nelly Korda. The Ryder Cup (USA vs Europe, since 1927) and Presidents Cup (1994) are the iconic team events.
