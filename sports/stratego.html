Stratego

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Stratego is the two-player deduction board game (French origin c. 1900, Milton Bradley 1960): each army has 40 pieces with hidden ranks (flag, bombs, spy, up to marshal); you capture the enemy flag to win. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Risk, Diplomacy, Chess; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

Figure: Catlemur. (2024-06-28). Various Stratego sets at the 2024 Patras Battles tournament [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 4.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Various_Stratego_Sets_Patras_Battles_2024.jpg

Rules

10x10 board; setup zones; pieces move one square orthogonally (scout any distance); ranks hidden until revealed on capture; combat: higher rank wins, equal both die, spy beats marshal if attacking, bombs immobile and beaten by miner; flag captured = win. World Stratego Championship exists (e.g. Patras Battles 2024 tournament photo) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

History

Stratego derives from 19th-century French battle games; published around 1900 in the Netherlands and by Milton Bradley in 1960; the 2024 Patras tournament photo shows modern sets (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
