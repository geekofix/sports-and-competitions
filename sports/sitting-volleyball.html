Sitting Volleyball

≈ 5 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Sitting volleyball is the Paralympic variant of volleyball played seated on the floor, in which two teams of six try to ground the ball on the opponent's side of a smaller court; it is governed by World ParaVolley and has been on the Paralympic programme since Rome 1960 (men) and Arnhem 1980 (women), with the Paris 2024 tournaments held at the Arena Paris Nord in Villepinte from 29 August to 7 September 2024 (IPC, n.d.; World ParaVolley, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-a). At Paris 2024 Iran won a record eighth men's gold medal by beating Bosnia and Herzegovina 3–1 in the final, with Morteza Mehrzadselakjani as MVP, while the United States won their third consecutive women's gold with a 3–1 victory over China, Heather Erickson taking MVP honours, and Canada claimed their first-ever women's Paralympic medal with a straight-sets win over Brazil (IPC, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-b).

Among today's elite, the women's game is headed by the USA's Heather Erickson — MVP of the Paris 2024 gold-medal team and a multi-Paralympic medallist — and by Canada's Heidi Peters, who led her team to bronze in 2024, while the men's dynasty is led by Iran's Morteza Mehrzadselakjani, a gold medallist at Rio 2016, Tokyo 2020 and Paris 2024 and top scorer of the 2024 final; both genders play under identical rules with the same court and net-height categories (IPC, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-b).

Within the net and court team ball games family on this site, its closest relatives are Volleyball, Beach Volleyball, Beach Volleyball 4x4, Snow Volleyball, Snow Volleyball 3x3; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Rules

The court measures 10 × 6 m with an attack line 2 m from the centre line, and the net is set at 1.15 m for men's competition and 1.05 m for women's; a player must keep part of the body between the buttocks and the shoulders in contact with the floor while playing or attempting to play the ball, the block is allowed, and the serve is made from behind the end line (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Teams have six players on court, substitutions are free, and the rally rules follow standing volleyball — three contacts maximum, no catching or holding — with the ball grounded on the opponent's side scoring a point; sets are played to 25 points, best of five (World ParaVolley, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Athletes are classified as disabled or minimally disabled, and at most one minimally disabled player may be on the court per team at any time; men and women compete in separate tournaments but with the same rules, the only difference being the lower net for women (IPC, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

History

Sitting volleyball was invented in the Netherlands in 1956 by combining elements of the German game Sitzball, and the first international match was played in 1967; it joined the Paralympic programme for men at Rome 1960 and for women at Arnhem 1980, and standing volleyball left the programme after 2000 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Iran and Bosnia and Herzegovina have dominated the men's event — they met in the gold-medal match five times between 2000 and 2024, with Iran winning eight titles in all — while China, the Netherlands and the United States have led the women's event; at Paris 2024 the finals were Iran over Bosnia 3–1 in the men's final and the USA over China 3–1 in the women's final (IPC, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-b).
