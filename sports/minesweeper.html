Minesweeper

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Minesweeper is the single-player logic game shipped with Windows since 1990: clear a minefield using number clues (adjacent mine counts) without detonating a mine; speedrunning (beginner/intermediate/expert) has world records (Minesweeper.info). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Sudoku, Nonogram, Kakuro; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Minesweeper.info, n.d.).

Figure: Bichoes78. (2026-01-01). A Minesweeper game board (ship-themed variant) [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Minesweeper_Mesta.jpg

Rules

Grid with hidden mines (30 in 16x16 expert); click a cell: number shows adjacent mines; flag mines; clear all non-mine cells to win; 3BV speed metric determines speedrun records (world record under two seconds on expert) (Minesweeper.info, n.d.).

History

Written by Curt Johnson for Windows 2.0 (written in 1989, shipped with Windows in 1990); became a global classic; competitive speedrunning with official world rankings (minesweeper.info) since the 2000s (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
