Sudoku

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Sudoku is the logic number-placement puzzle on a 9x9 grid: fill each row, column and 3x3 box with 1–9; the World Sudoku Championship (WSC) has been held since 2006 (e.g. Sofia 2015, photographed). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Crossword, Kakuro, Nonogram; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

Figure: Спасимир. (2015-10-13). World Sudoku Championship 2015, Sofia [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:World_Sudoku_Championship_2015_Sofia_Bulgaria_01.JPG

Rules

9x9 grid with given clues; each row/column/box contains 1–9 exactly once; unique solution required in competition puzzles; solving speed and accuracy are judged at the WSC (individual + team) (World Sudoku Championship, n.d.).

History

Sudoku was popularized by Nikoli in Japan from the 1980s (name from 'sūji wa dokushin ni kagiru') and by Wayne Gould's Times puzzles from 2004; the World Sudoku Championship since 2006 (Prague 2018 photo) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
