Basic Information

MultiVersus is Player First Games' 2022 crossover platform fighter (Warner Bros (Player First Games / Warner Bros, n.d.). characters); it was an EVO main-stage title from 2022 and runs the Multiversus — ranked and the — (Wikipedia, n.d.). Best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 finals), 2v2 modes; open to all genders.

Figure: Warner Bros. Games. (2021-11-18). MultiVersus gameplay screenshot [Screenshot]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY 3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MultiVersus_gameplay_screenshot.png

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are Super Smash Bros, Brawlhalla, Rivals of Aether and the other platform fighters; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Rules

Competitive rules: 1v1/2v2, best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 grand finals), blast zones and perks per character; double-elimination at majors; open brackets, all genders (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

MultiVersus launched in May 2022 (open beta 2021) by Player First (Warner Bros. Games); it was an EVO main stage title 2022–2024 with a $100k — EVO — prize pools; online — (Wikipedia, n.d.).
