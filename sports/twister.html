Twister

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Twister is the physical party game invented by Charles Foley and Harold Funtime in 1966 and sold by Milton Bradley: a spinner calls 'left hand red' etc. on a mat of colored dots; players place limbs without falling. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Jenga, Connect Four, Taboo; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Hasbro/Milton Bradley, n.d.).

Figure: Mike384. (2009-12-22). A family game of Twister, 1972 [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Twister_game_ph.jpg

Rules

A giant mat with 24 circles in 4 colors (red, yellow, green, blue); spinner with 16 combinations (limb + color); players place the called limb on the called color; elbows/knees must stay down; first to fall loses. Guinness-record group games exist (Hasbro/Milton Bradley, n.d.).

History

Invented by Charles Foley in 1966 (with Harold Funtime); Milton Bradley published 1966; the 1972 PD photo shows a family game; twister parties photographed in Italy (2021) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
