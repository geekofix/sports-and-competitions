Durak

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Durak ('fool') is the most popular Russian card game: an attacker plays cards of the same rank as cards on the table; the defender must beat each with a higher card of the same suit or a trump; undefended cards are taken up; the player left holding cards at the end is the fool (durak). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Preferans, Belote, Euchre; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Pagat.com, n.d.).

Figure: Gzhegozh. (2021-06-04). A three-player durak game with a 1974 deck [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 4.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Russian_card_game_Durak.jpg

Rules

36-card deck (6–A) in the classic form; trump suit from the bottom card; defender beats by higher rank of same suit or any trump; attacker may add cards up to the number of cards the defender holds (up to six initially, more in perevodnoy durak); first to empty hand wins, last with cards is the fool (Pagat.com, n.d.).

History

Durak developed in Russia in the 18th–19th centuries; it spread across the former USSR and is now played worldwide online; the 2021 photo shows a three-player game with a 1974 Atlasnye deck (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
