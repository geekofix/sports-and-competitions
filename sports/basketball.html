Basketball

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Basketball is a team sport in which five players per side try to score by throwing a ball through the opponent's raised hoop (the basket). It is governed internationally by FIBA, which organizes the FIBA Basketball World Cup, and is the top professional league's home in the NBA in the United States. Basketball has been an Olympic sport since Berlin 1936.

Rules

Under FIBA rules, a game lasts four 10-minute quarters. A team in possession has 24 seconds to attempt a shot (14 seconds after an offensive rebound). Shots from inside the three-point line are worth 2 points, from beyond it 3 points, and free throws 1 point. Personal fouls (five per player) and team fouls (four per quarter, bonus free throws on the fifth) are enforced, as are the shot clock, 8-second backcourt and 3-second lane rules. At the FIBA World Cup and the Olympics, games are played on a 28 x 15 m court with a 10-minute overtime if tied.

History

Basketball was invented in 1891 by James Naismith, a Canadian physical education teacher in Springfield, Massachusetts, who wanted an indoor winter game. The NBA was founded in 1946 and became the world's premier league. FIBA was founded in 1932 and has run the World Championship since 1950. The "Dream Team" at Barcelona 1992 globalized the sport; legends include Michael Jordan, Magic Johnson, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Larry Bird and today's stars LeBron James and Stephen Curry.
