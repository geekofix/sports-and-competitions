Arena Football

≈ 3 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Arena football (indoor football) is the padded-wall variant of American football: eight players per side play on a field of about 50 yards between the goal lines, surrounded by rebound walls, in four 15-minute quarters with the same scoring as the outdoor game (touchdown 6, field goal 3, safety 2). The original Arena Football League (AFL) was founded in 1987 by Jim Foster — after test games on April 27, 1986 between the Chicago Politicians and the Rockford Metros — closed in 2019 and relaunched in 2024. The Indoor Football League (IFL), formed in 2008 by the merger of the Intense Football League and United Indoor Football, is now the oldest active professional indoor football league in North America: its 2026 season has 14 teams, began on March 14, 2026, and the 2026 IFL National Championship was played on August 16, 2026 at Tucson Arena, won by the Arizona Rattlers — the Sioux Falls Storm hold the record with seven titles (Indoor Football League [IFL], n.d.-b). Other 2026 leagues include the AF1 (Nashville Kats), the NAL (Southwest Kansas Storm) and the TAL (Saint Joseph Goats). Women play in dedicated indoor leagues, for example the Women's Indoor Football League (Indoor Football League, n.d.-a) (Indoor Football League, n.d.-b). Arena football is the indoor variant within the American-football code family — American Football, Flag Football, Sprint Football — and the wider football-codes family listed under Important Links.

Rules

The field is about 50 yards (150 ft) long between the goal lines, roughly 85 ft wide, fully padded, and enclosed by rebound walls — the ball stays live off the walls, which makes the game faster and harder-hitting than the outdoor game. Teams field eight players, and a game lasts four 15-minute quarters. As in American Football, the offense has four downs to gain 10 yards, and scoring follows the same point values; kickoffs are drop-kicked, and overtime periods decide ties (IFL, n.d.-a).

History

Jim Foster conceived arena football in the 1980s and staged the first test games in 1986; the AFL launched in 1987 and crowned its champions in the ArenaBowl until its 2019 closure (the league relaunched in 2024). After the AFL's closure, the IFL — created in 2008 — became the oldest continuously active indoor league; its United Bowl was rebranded as the IFL National Championship in 2022, and since 2025 the final has been held at Tucson Arena, where the Arizona Rattlers won on August 16, 2026 (IFL, n.d.-b).
