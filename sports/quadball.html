Quadball

≈ 3 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Quadball (called quidditch until its 2022 renaming) is a mixed-gender contact team sport inspired by the fictional game in the Harry Potter novels, played with a broom or PVC stick held between every player's legs (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). Two teams of seven — three chasers, two beaters, a keeper and a seeker — score by throwing a volleyball (the quaffle) through the opponent's hoops while dodging dodgeballs (bludgers) thrown by beaters; the game ends when a neutral flag runner standing in for the snitch is caught (International Quadball Association, n.d.). The sport's world governing body is the International Quadball Association (IQA); the 2025 IQA World Cup saw Australia's "Dropbears" take bronze after becoming the only team besides the USA's 2014 win to beat the United States twice, and the 2027 World Cup will be held at Barn Elms, London, 23–25 July 2027 (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Quadball is explicitly mixed-gender: the IQA rule caps each team at a maximum of four players of the same gender on the pitch, so women and men play together on every team (International Quadball Association, n.d.).

Rules

Under IQA rules, play is on a rectangular pitch with three hoops of different heights at each end; chasers advance the quaffle and score 10 points per goal through any hoop, with contact regulated like rugby — tackles below the shoulders and above the knees are disallowed and dangerous contact sends players off (International Quadball Association, n.d.). Beaters throw bludgers at opponents; any player hit must drop any ball and return to their hoops before rejoining. All players hold a broom between their legs at all times. The snitch is replaced by a neutral runner in yellow released after a set time; each team's seeker tries to catch them — worth 30 points (35 in US leagues) and ending the game — by removing the flag. Games have no fixed duration. The gender rule limits each seven-player side to at most four players of one gender (Wikipedia, n.d.-b).

History

Quadball began in October 2005 at Middlebury College in Vermont, where freshmen Xander Manshel and Alex Benepe adapted J.K. Rowling's fictional game into a working outdoor sport blending lacrosse, dodgeball and rugby; the first intercollegiate match was Middlebury vs Vassar in 2007 (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The Intercollegiate Quidditch Association was founded in 2009, became the International Quidditch Association in 2010, and the first national-team World Cup ran in 2012; in July 2022 the sport and both bodies were renamed quadball — partly to avoid Warner Bros. trademark disputes and to distance the community from the author's public statements (Wikipedia, n.d.-b).
