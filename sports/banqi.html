Banqi

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Banqi ('dark chess', also half-board chess) is a Chinese gambling/strategy game played on half a xiangqi board (4 files x 8 ranks) with pieces placed face-down; ranks are revealed only when a piece moves or captures. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Xiangqi, Minixiangqi, Sittuyin; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

Figure: w: Zen. , wc: User:Alton. (21 January). A Banqi game on half a xiangqi board [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BanQi.jpg

Rules

32 pieces (16 per side, xiangqi types) placed face-down on a 4x8 grid; a piece may capture an enemy piece of equal or lower revealed rank (kings highest); the goal is to capture the enemy general or leave the opponent without pieces (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

History

Banqi derives from xiangqi and is played throughout China (played in Temple of Heaven Park, Beijing — photographed 2014); it blends memory and bluffing with chess-like captures (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
