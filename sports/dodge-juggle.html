Dodge Juggle

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Dodge-juggle is a hybrid novelty sport that pits a single dodgeball thrower against a team of jugglers. It became known to a wide audience through ESPN's The Ocho and is played at skill-sport events such as the SkillCon (organized by Moxie Games). One variant, Dodge-Juggle Protect, is played with five-player teams.

Rules

In the classic format, one player armed with dodgeballs faces five jugglers, each of whom gets a 60-second turn. The jugglers must keep at least three objects in the air at all times while the thrower tries to hit them with soft dodgeballs; a juggler who is hit or drops a ball is out, and the thrower scores for each juggler eliminated. The thrower wins by getting all jugglers out within the allotted throws, and the jugglers win by surviving all 60 seconds each. In the team variant Dodge-Juggle Protect, five-player teams defend a set of objects (pins or juggling clubs) from the throwers while maintaining their juggling pattern; the team that protects its objects longest wins the round.

History

Dodge-juggle emerged from the juggling and skill-sports festival scene in the United States in the 2000s, combining the aiming challenge of dodgeball with the coordination challenge of juggling. It gained national exposure when ESPN's The Ocho, the fictional sports network from the film Dodgeball, included it among its invented sports in 2006, with a televised-style feature explaining the rules. Today it is played at events such as SkillCon, where Moxie Games organizes dodge-juggle competitions alongside other coordination sports, and in casual form at juggling conventions.
