Battleship

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Battleship (the paper-and-pencil guessing game, 'Battleships') is played by two players on hidden grids: each secretly places ships (lengths 5,4,3,3,2 in the classic set) and calls shots ('B4'); hits/misses are marked; the first to sink all enemy ships wins. The board game was commercialized in 1931. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Mastermind, Tic Tac Toe, Stratego; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

Figure: shakko. (2011). An early-20th-century Battleship board [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Battleship_(game)_early_20th_c._by_shakko.JPG

Rules

10x10 grid (classic); ships: 1x5, 1x4, 2x3, 1x2; no adjacent ships (in some rule sets); call shots; 'hit'/'miss'/'sunk'; first to sink all wins. World championships (paper battleship) are held (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

History

Paper battleship predates the 1931 Milton Bradley board game (a similar paper game appeared in the 1890s); the early-20th-century board (shakko, 2011 photo) and the 2016 Navy photo show play (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
