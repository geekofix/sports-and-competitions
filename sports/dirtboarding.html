Dirtboarding

≈ 1 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Dirtboarding is mountainboarding on purpose-built dirt courses — jumps, berms and rollers — often at bike parks; it bridges mountainboarding and BMX/dirt-jump culture, with timed downhill runs and slopestyle/jib events at series such as the Dirtboard World Cup (Wikipedia, n.d.). Riders use full-face helmets and pads; men's and women's categories exist at events.

Figure: Ricardo Villela from Belo Horizonte, Brazil. (2005-08-19 17:53). Mountainboarder on a dirt course (Biagioni by Villela) [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 2.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mountainboarding_Biagioni_by_Villela.jpg

Within the board-sports family on this site its closest relatives are Mountainboarding, Landboarding, BMX Freestyle, Mountain Biking and Skateboarding; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Rules

Dirtboard competition rules follow mountainboarding: timed downhill on a dirt course with jumps/berms, or slopestyle judged on tricks; full-face helmet, gloves, pads mandatory; equipment checks before runs; categories by age and gender (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

Dirt courses for mountainboards grew out of UK and US bike parks in the 2000s; the dirtboard/dirt-mountainboard scene formalized with dedicated series in the 2010s (Wikipedia, n.d.).
