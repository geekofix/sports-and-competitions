King of the Hill Chess

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

King of the Hill is a chess variant: besides checkmate, you win by moving your king to the central four squares (d4, d5, e4, e5 — the 'hill'). It is one of the most popular lichess variants. Within the chess variants and mind games on this site, its closest relatives are Racing Kings, Three-Check Chess, Chess; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Lichess, n.d.).

Figure: EricABCAT. (2020-04-11). King of the Hill board diagram [Diagram]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 4.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:King_of_the_Hill_chess.png

Rules

Standard chess, plus the king may win by occupying any of the four central squares; entering the hill wins immediately (even if it would be 'check'). Checkmate also wins. Castling into/out of the hill is allowed per standard rules (Lichess, n.d.).

History

The variant dates to the 19th century and is a standard online variant; world championships on lichess (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
