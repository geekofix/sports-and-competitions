Nonogram

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Nonogram (Picross in Nintendo games, 1990s) is the logic picture puzzle: row/column clue numbers give the lengths of consecutive filled blocks; shading cells according to clues reveals a hidden picture. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Kakuro, Sudoku, Crossword; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

Figure: TadejM. (2020-08-01). A nonogram puzzle (Android Easter egg) [Diagram]. Wikimedia Commons. Public domain. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nonogram_Android_10_Easter_egg.jpg

Rules

Grid with row/column run-length clues; fill or leave blank cells so runs match clues exactly; unique solution; competitions (e.g. the World Puzzle Championship's nonogram events) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).

History

Nonograms were popularized by Japanese publisher Nikoli in the 1980s–90s; Nintendo's Picross (1994+) made them mainstream; the Android 10 nonogram Easter egg (PD) shows a solved grid (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
