Zaldi Probak

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Zaldi probak (horse test) is the Basque horse-drawn stone-pulling contest: one horse or a pair harnessed to a sledge drags a heavy stone along the fiesta course in the town square, against the clock or for the set distance. It is a core herri kirolak event at fiestas such as San Pankrazio in Vitoria-Gasteiz and in Urnieta. Within the Basque rural sports on this site, its closest relatives are Idi Probak, Asto Probak, Giza Probak; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Euskal Herri Kirolak Elkartea, n.d.).

Figure: Alberto Cabello from Vitoria Gasteiz. (2015-08-09). Zaldi probak — horse-drawn stone pulling at the Foruen Plaza, Vitoria-Gasteiz, 2015 [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY 2.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Zaldi_probak_(19806754753).jpg

Rules

The horse wears the traditional collar harness (kolar); the stone weight is fixed per event (often 1,000–3,000 kg for a single horse). The team must complete the course within the time limit; veterinary checks, harness inspection and welfare rules (no whipping) apply. Men and women compete as drivers in separate or open categories at modern events (Euskal Herri Kirolak Elkartea, n.d.).

History

Horse-drawn stone pulling reflects the horse's role in Basque agriculture and transport; photographs from 2008–2015 show the events at Urnieta and the Foruen Plaza in Vitoria. The Herri Kirolak federation standardized the modern format with animal-welfare rules (Wikipedia, n.d.).
