Aquabike

≈ 3 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Aquabike is the official name of jet ski racing: personal watercraft (PWC) compete in closed-circuit, offshore, endurance and freestyle events, in runabout (seated) and ski (stand-up) classes. The sport is governed by the UIM — Union Internationale Motonautique, the IOC-recognized world federation of powerboating — and the UIM Aquabike World Championship, organized with H2O Racing, has been held since 1992 (Aquabike World Championship [AWC], n.d.-a; UIM, n.d.) (Aquabike World Championship, n.d.-a) (Aquabike World Championship, n.d.-b).

Among the leading riders today, the women come first: Jessica Chavanne is the 2026 Ski Ladies GP1 world champion and Annemarie Oiemets the Runabout Ladies GP4 world champion. Among the men, Belgium's Quinten Bossche swept the 2026 Ski GP1 titles, Gyorgy Kasza won Runabout GP1 at the GP of Shanghai on October 3, 2026, Samuel Johansson took the Runabout GP1 world title at the GP of Italy in Olbia on October 16–18, 2026, and freestyle star Rashid Al Mulla claimed his seventh career freestyle world title in 2026 (AWC, n.d.-a; AWC, n.d.-b).

Within the multisport and endurance race family on this site, its closest relatives are Triathlon, Duathlon, Aquathlon, Winter Triathlon, Cross Triathlon; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Rules

UIM competition rules divide craft into classes — Ski GP1, GP2 and GP3 and Runabout GP1, GP2 and GP4, each with Ladies categories — by engine specification and rider posture. Closed-circuit races run on marked courses of up to about 1,300 m: riders must pass yellow buoys on the right and red buoys on the left, and cutting a buoy brings penalties; heats and finals award points toward the world title. Offshore and endurance events add waves and long-distance legs, while parallel slalom pits two riders head-to-head on mirrored courses. Freestyle runs last about three minutes and are judged by five judges on quality, quantity and variety of tricks — bar rolls, backflips, superman moves. Riders must wear approved life jackets and helmets, and safety boats and flag signals officiate (UIM, n.d.; AWC, n.d.-b).

History

Racing personal watercraft exploded in the 1980s once mass-produced sit-down (runabout) and stand-up (ski) machines changed water recreation, and manufacturer-backed series formed in the US and Europe. The UIM brought the discipline into its world championship structure with the Aquabike World Championship from 1992, organized today with H2O Racing and with rounds across Europe, the Middle East and Asia; the world rankings were updated on October 18, 2026 after the GP of Italy in Olbia (AWC, n.d.-a; AWC, n.d.-b; UIM, n.d.).
