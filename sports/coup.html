Coup

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Coup (2012, Reformation Games) is a fast bluffing card game for 2–6 players: each player has two influence cards (Duke, Assassin, Contessa, Captain, Ambassador) and 2 coins; you may claim any role but can be challenged; losing a challenge costs an influence; last player with influence wins. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Avalon, The Resistance, Mafia (Werewolf); the related pages are listed under Important Links (Reformation Games, n.d.).

Figure: El Pantera. (2023-07-26). A game of Coup being played [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 4.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Playing_board_game_-_Play_351_1690369753634.jpg

Rules

2 influence cards each from 5 roles (Duke: tax +3 coins; Assassin: pay 3 to assassinate; Contessa: blocks assassination; Captain: steal 2; Ambassador: exchange); actions may be challenged; a successful challenge kills the claimant's card; a failed challenge kills the challenger's. Last with influence wins (Reformation Games, n.d.).

History

Designed by Rikki Tahta, published 2012 by Reformation Games (Indie Game Studios); became a hit at game conventions; the 2023 'Playing board game — Coup' photo shows play (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
