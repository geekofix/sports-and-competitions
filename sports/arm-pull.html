Arm Pull

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Arm pull is a seated Arctic strength event: two competitors sit facing each other and pull a stick over a centre line with one arm, like a one-armed tug of war; the first to pull the opponent's hand across the line wins (AWG, n.d.; WEIO, n.d.). Men's, women's and youth categories at AWG and WEIO. Within the Arctic and Indigenous sports on this site, its closest relatives are Blanket Toss, Snow Snake, Two-Foot High Kick, Finger Pull, Hand Games, World Eskimo Indian Olympics; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Figure: Public Affairs Office Fort Wainwright. (2014-07-16). Arm pull at the 2014 World Eskimo-Indian Olympics, Fairbanks [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY 2.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2014_World_Eskimo-Indian_Olympics.jpg

Rules

Seated with feet braced, competitors grip the stick at arm's length; on the referee's signal they pull. Best of three; the bout ends when a hand crosses the centre board (WEIO, n.d.).

History

Arm pull derives from hauling and paddling strength tests in Arctic communities and has been a WEIO/AWG event since the 1970s (Wikipedia, n.d.).
