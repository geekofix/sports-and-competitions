E1 Series

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

The E1 Series is the world’s first all-electric offshore powerboat championship, sanctioned by the UIM: identical RaceBird hydrofoil boats race on tight city waterfront circuits (Wikipedia, n.d.). It belongs to the powerboat family with F1H2O and offshore racing, and to the electric-racing family with Formula E and Extreme E.

Figure: E1 Series. (2022-06-28). E1 Series logo [Logo]. Wikimedia Commons. Public domain. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/E1_Series_logo.png

The series was created in 2020 by Rodi Basso (ex-F1/McLaren Applied) and Alejandro Agag (founder of Formula E and Extreme E), announced at the Monaco Yacht Club on 25 September 2020; the inaugural 2024 season started in Jeddah on 2 February 2024 and was won by Team Brady — owned by Tom Brady, with Sam Coleman and Emma Kimiläinen as pilots; Team Brady also won the 2025 championship, decided at the Miami finale (Wikipedia, n.d.).

Rules

The RaceBird is a 7.5 m all-electric hydrofoil built by Victory Marine (design SeaBird Technologies, powertrain Mercury Racing, batteries Kreisel Electric): a 150 kW motor and 35 kWh battery lift the hull on foils above 17 knots, reaching 50 knots (93 km/h) (Wikipedia, n.d.). Race format: practice and qualifying on day one; group races, race-off, place race and finals on day two, 6 laps per race with a mandatory short and long lap, marked by electronic SmartMarks buoys (Wikipedia, n.d.). Each team must field one male and one female pilot who share driving duties equally (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

The series launched with 9 teams and 18 pilots and has grown — team owners include Will Smith, Virat Kohli, Rafael Nadal, LeBron James and Didier Drogba; the UIM E1 Pilot Academy trains new talent (Wikipedia, n.d.). The 2025 season added Doha, Dubrovnik, Lake Maggiore, Lagos (first African round) and Miami, where Team Brady clinched the title (Wikipedia, n.d.).
