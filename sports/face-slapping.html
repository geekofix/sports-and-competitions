Face Slapping

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Face slapping (slap fighting) is a combat sport in which two competitors take turns striking each other in the face with an open hand; a knockout or a judges' decision ends the bout (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The leading professional promotion is Power Slap, co-owned by UFC CEO Dana White and Zuffa co-founder Lorenzo Fertitta and launched in 2023, which is sanctioned by the Nevada State Athletic Commission and broadcast on television; events in 2026 included Power Slap 27 in Doha, Qatar, on 19 November 2026 (Power Slap, n.d.; Nevada State Athletic Commission, n.d.). Competitions are organized in weight classes with champions in each division, and the bouts are overseen by referees, judges, a supervising doctor and on-site medical staff including EMTs and ambulances (Nevada State Athletic Commission, n.d.). As an adult, human-consent combat format it falls under the project's ethics policy the same way boxing or bare-knuckle fighting do: included, with the well-documented criticism of the format — including brain-injury concerns voiced by fighters and commentators — part of the public debate (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Men and women compete in separate weight divisions with identical rules (Power Slap, n.d.).

Rules

Power Slap bouts are governed by the promotion's official rules as sanctioned by the Nevada State Athletic Commission: a coin toss decides who strikes first in round one, then the competitors alternate slaps; the striker must announce the hand to be used and the number of wind-ups (1 = no wind-up, 2 or 3 = wind-up allowed) and line up with an open palm — fingers, thumb and heel of the hand must make contact — with the hand placed flat against the chin area, and the referee may reposition the hand (Power Slap, n.d.). The defender has a 60-second recovery clock to return to the defensive box, re-establish fitness and be declared recovered before the roles switch (Power Slap, n.d.). A fighter who is knocked out, or who cannot continue, loses; otherwise three judges score the bout, and the referee has discretion over fouls such as closed-fist strikes, eye gouging or striking after the bell (Power Slap, n.d.; Nevada State Athletic Commission, n.d.). Weight classes run from lightweight to super heavyweight, with championship bouts at major events (Power Slap, n.d.). Separate men's and women's divisions use identical rules (Power Slap, n.d.).

History

Slap fighting circulated as viral internet content — Russian slap competitions — before Dana White, inspired by videos of the format, launched Power Slap with Lorenzo Fertitta and producer Craig Piligian in early 2023, in partnership with the UFC (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The Nevada State Athletic Commission sanctioned the league, giving it the same regulatory oversight as boxing and MMA, with two referees, three judges and full medical staffing at every event (Nevada State Athletic Commission, n.d.). The format has drawn heavy criticism — boxer Ryan Garcia called it "a horrible idea", and fighters have raised brain-injury concerns — but it has continued as a televised league with regular events through 2026, including shows in Doha (Power Slap, n.d.; Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Women's slap fighting developed alongside the men's league in separate divisions under the same sanctioned rules (Power Slap, n.d.).
