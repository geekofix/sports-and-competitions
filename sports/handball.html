Handball

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Handball is the fast Olympic team sport of seven-a-side indoor handball governed by the International Handball Federation (IHF) — see Field Handball for the full sport article, since this entry and that one refer to the same discipline. For clarity: the IHF World Men's and Women's Handball Championships and the Olympic handball tournament are the premier competitions, played on a 40 m x 20 m court with a 3 m x 2 m goal.

Rules

The rules are those of IHF team handball: seven players per team, two 30-minute halves, dribbling and three-step rule, no holding or dangerous contact, free throws and 7 m penalties for fouls, yellow/red cards with two-minute suspensions, and goals scored when the whole ball crosses the goal line. Full details are in the Field Handball entry on this site.

History

Modern handball was codified in northern Europe around 1906-1917 and organized by the IHF, founded in Copenhagen in 1946; it has been an Olympic sport since Munich 1972 (men) and Montreal 1976 (women). See Field Handball for the complete history, from the 1936 outdoor World Championships to the modern indoor game dominated by Denmark, France, Sweden, Germany, Norway and Spain.
