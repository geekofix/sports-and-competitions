Crossword

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

The crossword is the word puzzle of interlocking answers in a grid with numbered clues; competitive solving (American-style and cryptic) has world championships (e.g. the American Crossword Puzzle Tournament since 1978). Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Sudoku, Kakuro, Scrabble; the related pages are listed under Important Links (American Crossword Puzzle Tournament, n.d.).

Figure: Barbara (WVS). (2018-08-26). A Signpost crossword puzzle cover [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 4.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Signpost_Crossword_puzzle.png

Rules

Grid with across/down clues; black squares; unique letters per square; timed solving in competitions (ACPT and world crossword championships); cryptic crosswords use wordplay clues (American Crossword Puzzle Tournament, n.d.).

History

Crosswords appeared in the New York World in 1913 (Arthur Wynne); cryptic crosswords in the UK from the 1920s (Torquemada, Edward Powys Mathers); the Signpost crossword cover (2018) and setter photos document the scene (American Crossword Puzzle Tournament, n.d.).
