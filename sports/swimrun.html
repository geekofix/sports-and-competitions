Swimrun

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Swimrun is the endurance sport that alternates trail running and open-water swimming along a linked course: athletes race in teams of two (or solo) across islands, coasts and lakes, switching repeatedly between land and water without rest stops. Created in Sweden as the obstacle of a single legendary race, it is today a worldwide sport with hundreds of events and a world championship "+E+" and the defining rule is that whatever you start with, you carry with you throughout.

Rules

A swimrun course strings together multiple running and swimming legs "+E+" the flagship world championship in the Stockholm archipelago runs across roughly twenty-four islands with more than twenty swims and runs "+E+" and athletes alternate continuously with no transition zones: they run in wetsuits, swim in shoes and carry their hand paddles and pull buoy. Equipment rules key on self-sufficiency (no outside help, no change of gear, team members required to stay within a set distance of each other in the team race) and standard kit includes a swimrun-specific wetsuit with the front unzipped on land. Race distances are tiered: sprint (around 15 kilometres), standard World Series (around 40 kilometres) and the full championship distance of about 70 kilometres, with ranking points, qualification and age-group waves in a worldwide calendar.

History

Swimrun was born in 2002 when a drunken bet between friends (to race the length of the Stockholm archipelago, from Sandhamn to Uto, over a night and a day of swimming and running) became the OTILLO race in 2006 "+E+" widely called one of the toughest one-day endurance contests in the world. The concept spread through the 2010s into a genuine sport: the OTILLO Swimrun World Series launched in 2015 with qualifying races in Europe and North America; hundreds of independent swimrun events sprouted with an estimated tens of thousands of active athletes, and gear manufacturers built an entire wetsuit-and-paddle industry category around the sport. The sport's governing moment arrived in 2025, when the World Triathlon Federation congress formally added swimrun to its activity with individual and team events "+E+" the clearest sign yet that the sport of running and swimming island to island has become a permanent fixture of endurance racing.
