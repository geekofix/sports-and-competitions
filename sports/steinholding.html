Steinholding

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Steinholding (German: Masskrugstemmen) is the Bavarian strength and endurance contest in which competitors hold a full one-litre beer stein (a Masskrug, about five pounds filled) out in front of the body with a straight arm for as long as possible. Born of Oktoberfest toasts and tavern games, it is now a regulated competitive sport with championships in Germany and the United States.

Rules

Competitors hold the stein in one hand, arm extended straight out in front, elbow locked, thumb clear so the stein is fully off it, arm parallel to the ground and separated from the chest "+E+" the chest may not be used as a shelf. Judges give warnings for drooping arms, bent elbows or leaning, but spilling any beer, setting the stein down, or steadying it with the other hand means immediate disqualification. The competitor who holds longest with proper form wins; events are contested with regulation one-litre dimpled steins with handles, and governing rules such as those of the U.S. Steinholding Association specify stein weight, apparel (no braces, gloves or grip aids; sleeves rolled so the elbow is visible), qualification rounds and judging procedures, including standardized stein inspections to guard against tampering.

History

Steinholding grew organically from Bavarian beer culture, where holding the heavy Mass aloft for a toast "+E+" Masskrugstemmen "+E+" became a fixture of Oktoberfest amusement in Munich and across Bavaria in the twentieth century. Over time, tavern contests formalized rules around the stein, the straight arm and the strict no-spill disqualification. In 2015 the U.S. Steinholding Association was founded to bring standardized rules and championship structure to the sport in North America, running state championships and an inaugural national championship; the sport has also thrived at beer gardens, charity events and cultural festivals from Munich to Cincinnati, with elite steinholders routinely posting holds of several minutes.
