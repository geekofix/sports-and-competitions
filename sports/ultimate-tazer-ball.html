Ultimate Tazer Ball

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Ultimate Tazer Ball is the extreme exhibition team sport conceived in 2011 by Leif Kellenberger, Eric Prumm and Erik Wunsch at a paintball conference in Chicago, staged from January 2012 in California: two teams of four contest a 24-inch (61 cm) medicine ball on a 200 × 85 ft (about 61 × 26 m) field with a goal at each end, and every player carries a stun gun delivering a 3–5 milliamp shock that may be used on the opponent in possession of the ball — so holding the ball hurts, and full-contact tackling is allowed (Wikipedia, n.d.). Matches consist of three 7-minute periods, a 3-minute overtime and, if still tied, a one-on-one duel (Wikipedia, n.d.). Four professional teams were formed — the Philadelphia Killawatts, San Diego Spartans, Toronto Terror and LA Nightlight — and the 2012 season received television coverage on Discovery's Daily Planet, ITN and in the Bangkok Post, but the league folded within a few years amid safety criticism from commentators (Wikipedia, n.d.). The exhibition format was played by women and men alike, with mixed teams common in demonstration matches (Wikipedia, n.d.).

Rules

The game is played by two teams of four on a rectangular field roughly 200 ft long and 85 ft wide with a goal at each end; players run, pass and carry a 24-inch medicine ball and score by putting the ball into the opponent's goal (Wikipedia, n.d.). The signature rule: each player wears or carries a stun gun (about 3–5 milliamps) that may be discharged at the opponent who is in possession of the ball, so a ball carrier can hold on only briefly before the shocks force a pass, interception or tackle (Wikipedia, n.d.). Full-contact tackling and blocking are permitted — the sport is explicitly a contact game — and play is divided into three 7-minute periods, followed by a 3-minute overtime period and then a one-on-one tiebreaker if the score is level (Wikipedia, n.d.). Safety measures in the staged league included referees, protective gear recommendations and medical staff on site, but there is no permanent world federation: the rules were set by the league organizers, and women's and men's teams played under the same rules (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

Ultimate Tazer Ball was conceptualized in 2011 by Leif Kellenberger, Eric Prumm and Erik Wunsch at a paintball industry conference in Chicago, who wanted a ball game with a built-in deterrent against ball-hogging; the first tournament was held in California in January 2012 (Wikipedia, n.d.). During 2012 the four founding professional teams — Philadelphia Killawatts, San Diego Spartans, Toronto Terror and LA Nightlight — played a showcase season that was covered by Discovery Channel's Daily Planet, ITN and other international media, making the game a viral curiosity of the invented-sport boom (Wikipedia, n.d.). Safety critics and the physical toll of repeated shocks led to the league's collapse within a few years, and the sport survives mainly through internet footage, nostalgia lists of extreme sports and occasional one-off revival matches (Wikipedia, n.d.).
