Ultimate Tazer Ball

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Ultimate Tazer Ball is the extreme exhibition team sport in which players carry, throw and strategize around an electrified ball: teams move a purpose-built ball (which delivers a mild electric charge to whoever holds it too long) down a large indoor or court field and attempt to score in the opponent's goal zone. A staged invention of the extreme-sports showcase circuit, it is contested as a media-friendly spectacle rather than a mass participant sport.

Rules

The game format is simple in outline: teams of four per side contest the ball on an open court, with players running, passing and shooting at the goal, except the ball itself punishes possession: the object's built-in electric charge means a player can hold it only briefly before pain forces a pass, giving the game its signature built-in urgency. Defenders may intercept, no protective contact rules beyond the showcase organizer's own apply (bodies and tackles limited by the exhibition format, with no full-contact tackling in the standard rules), and matches are timed, with the scoring team the one that puts the ball in the goal most often. The sport's honest requirement is pain tolerance: the charge is calibrated to be safe but distinctly uncomfortable.

History

Ultimate Tazer Ball was developed in the early 2010s as an engineered extreme sport for the showcase circuit (a California-based creation of event developers), designed as a novel, TV-friendly combination of court ball game and dare: its creators staged demonstration matches, and the game drew wide viral attention as one of the most unusual invented sports of its era. It has not grown into a mass league structure: the sport exists through demonstration events, internet notoriety and occasional revival matches, and its lasting legacy is as a landmark of the invented-sport boom and a fixture of those who collect strange sports.
