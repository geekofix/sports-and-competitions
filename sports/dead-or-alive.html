Dead or Alive

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Dead or Alive is Team Ninja's 3D fighting series (DOA 6, 2019, current); it has been an EVO main-stage title and runs the DOA World Tour — community and pro circuit (Wikipedia, n.d.) (Team Ninja / Koei Tecmo, n.d.). Matches: holds and launchers, danger zones; best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 finals); open to all genders.

Figure: Team Ninja. (2012-04-03). Dead or Alive 5 logo [Logo]. Wikimedia Commons. Public domain. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dead_or_Alive_5_logo.png

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are Tekken, Soulcalibur, Virtua Fighter, Mortal Kombat and the other fighting games; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Rules

Competitive rules: best-of-3 sets (best-of-5 grand finals), 60-second — round timer per ruleset, hold and launcher mechanics per patch; stage select; double-elimination at majors; open brackets, all genders (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

Dead or Alive launched in 1996 (Team Ninja, Tecmo); the series became known for its physics and holds and has been at EVO since the 2000s, with DOA 6 at EVO 2019–2022 (Wikipedia, n.d.).
