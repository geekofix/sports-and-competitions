Diplomacy

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Diplomacy is the classic seven-player strategy board game invented by Allan B. Calhamer in 1959: players control the great powers of pre-WWI Europe, negotiating alliances and writing orders (move/support/hold/convoy) simultaneously each turn. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Risk, Stratego, Avalon; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Diplomacy Players / World Diplomacy Championship, n.d.).

Figure: Porge and Edinborgarstefan. (24 August ). The Diplomacy board map [Diagram]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 3.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:DiplomacyBoard.png

Rules

Map of 1901 Europe with 34 supply centers; each player has 3–4 units; turns: diplomacy phase (negotiation), then simultaneous written orders; supports and convoys; a unit enters a province if— standard adjudication; first to 18 supply centers wins; variants exist (world championships, Diplomacy world championship since 1990s— ) (Diplomacy Players / World Diplomacy Championship, n.d.).

History

Invented by Allan B. Calhamer (University of Chicago law student) in 1959, published by Games Research Group; the Diplomacy World Championship is held annually (since 1999); the board diagrams (2014) show the classic map (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
