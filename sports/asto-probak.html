Asto Probak

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Asto probak (donkey test) is the lightest of the Basque animal-drawn stone-pulling contests: a donkey harnessed to a small sledge drags a stone (typically up to about 1,000 kg) along a short course at village fiestas — a traditional, family-friendly herri kirolak event photographed on Istingorra street. Within the Basque rural sports on this site, its closest relatives are Zaldi Probak, Idi Probak, Giza Probak; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Euskal Herri Kirolak Elkartea, n.d.).

Figure: Luistxo eta Marije. (2008-01-19). Asto proba — donkey-drawn stone pull on Istingorra street, 2008 [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 2.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Asto_proba_Istingorra_kalean.jpg

Rules

The donkey pulls the weighed stone the set distance within a generous time limit; harness and welfare rules mirror zaldi probak. Often run as a demonstration or youth event alongside the main probak contests; men and women participate as handlers (Euskal Herri Kirolak Elkartea, n.d.).

History

Donkeys were the smallholder's beast of burden in the Basque Country, and donkey-drawn stone pulls were a fixture of rural fêtes from the 19th century; the 2008 photographs from Istingorra show the street-contest format still used today (Wikipedia, n.d.).
