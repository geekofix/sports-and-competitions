Blokus

≈ 1 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Blokus is a polyomino placement game invented in 1997 by Bernard Tavitian (France): 1–4 players place pieces made of 1–5 squares on a 20x20 board; each new piece must touch a previous piece of the same color corner-to-corner but not edge-to-edge; the player with the fewest squares left wins. Within the mind, board and card games on this site, its closest relatives are Quoridor, Quarto, Go; the related pages are listed under Important Links (Mattel, n.d.).

Figure: Bill Abbott. (2016-03-28). A three-way Blokus game at the 2016 Maker Faire [Photograph]. Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 2.0. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Excellent_Blokus_game_-_3_way_tie_for_1st_place!_DSC_0238_(26563345952).jpg

Rules

20x20 board, 21 polyomino pieces per color; first piece covers the corner square; later pieces must touch same-color pieces at corners only. Fewest remaining squares at the end wins; bonuses for finishing. Blokus world championships are held (Mattel, n.d.).

History

Invented by Bernard Tavitian in 1997 and published by Mattel; it it became a classic family game with world championships (World Blokus Championship) (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
