Slamball

≈ 4 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Slamball is a full-contact team sport played on a court with four trampolines and glass backboards, combining basketball, American football and gymnastics: teams score by shooting or dunking the ball through the hoop, with higher points for shots from further out and for dunks off the trampolines (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The game was invented in 1999 by Mason Gordon, who built a prototype court in a Los Angeles warehouse with producer Mike Tollin of Tollin/Robbins Productions (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). It was first televised in 2002 on The National Network, moved to Spike TV in 2003, returned in 2008 on Versus, CBS and Cartoon Network, staged a tournament in China in 2012, and was relaunched in August 2022 with the 2023 season played in Las Vegas and broadcast on ESPN and ESPN+, followed by a second season back in 2026 (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The inaugural 2002 champion was the LA Rumble, and MaxAir is the official trampoline supplier of the league (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). The professional league fields men's rosters; women play the sport at amateur and exhibition level, and co-ed formats exist at grass-roots events (Slamball, n.d.).

Rules

Slamball is played 4-on-4 on a court roughly 96 by 64 feet, with four trampolines set into the floor at each end and Plexiglass boards behind each hoop (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). A match consists of four five-minute quarters. Scoring zones differ from basketball: a shot from inside the painted "Slam Zone" is worth 2 points, a shot from the "Gold Zone" floor mark or any dunk off a trampoline is worth 3 points, and a shot from beyond the NBA three-point arc is worth 4 points (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Goaltending is legal on 2-point shots and on shots taken off the trampolines, adding the aerial dimension that defines the game (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Substitutions happen on the fly within five feet of the team's bench box, like in ice hockey. Personal fouls lead to one-on-one penalty plays rather than free throws. Teams play four positions — handler, gunner, stopper and center. Overtime is an extra quarter, and if still tied, a three-minute sudden-death period decides (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). Women's and co-ed divisions follow the same rules with adjusted roster sizes at amateur level (Slamball, n.d.).

History

Slamball was invented in 1999 by Mason Gordon, who, together with producer Mike Tollin, built the first court in a Los Angeles warehouse and tested the trampoline-and-board concept (Wikipedia, n.d.-a). The game debuted on television in 2002 on The National Network, where the LA Rumble won the inaugural championship, and moved to Spike TV in 2003, returning in 2008 on Versus, CBS and Cartoon Network with a tournament in China in 2012 (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). After a hiatus, the league was relaunched in August 2022 and played its 2023 season in Las Vegas on ESPN and ESPN+, with a second season in 2026 (Wikipedia, n.d.-b). Founder Mason Gordon and producer Mike Tollin remain the sport's defining figures, and the LA Rumble's 2002 title is its first championship banner (Wikipedia, n.d.-a).
