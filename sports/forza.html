Forza

≈ 2 min read

Basic Information Rules History Sources Important Links

Basic Information

Forza is Microsoft/Turn 10's racing simulation franchise (Forza Motorsport 2023 and the Forza Horizon series); its esport is the Forza Racing Championship (Forza RC), run on Forza Motorsport with open qualifiers, a professional league and a world championship, plus community cups (Turn 10 / Microsoft, n.d.). The 2026 Forza RC season runs on the current Forza Motorsport title on road and oval courses (Wikipedia, n.d.).

Figure: Xbox Game Studios. (2020-07-23). Forza Motorsport (2023) new logo [Logo]. Wikimedia Commons. Public domain. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Forza_Motorsport_new_logo.png

Within the esports family on this site its closest relatives are Gran Turismo, iRacing, Assetto Corsa, F1 (video game series), MotoGP (video game series), WRC (video game series), Zwift and E-Sports; the related pages are listed under Important Links.

Rules

Forza RC rules: time-trial qualifiers and race heats on the current game in fixed, homologated car classes with no tuning in official events; bracket playoffs decided by series results; points for the season championship; open to all genders (Wikipedia, n.d.).

History

Forza Motorsport's competitive scene began with community cups in the 2000s; the official Forza Racing Championship launched in the late 2010s (Forza RC 2019 on FM7) and has run on the 2023 Forza Motorsport since its release (Wikipedia, n.d.).
